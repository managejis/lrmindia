Abraham Rescues Lot

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 1: Beginning with God

Aim

To teach the children that Lot’s bad friends led him into trouble, while Abraham’s life of obedience and worship was blessed by God.

Scripture Reading

Genesis 14:8-24

Memory Verse

Psalm 34:15

The eyes of the Lord are on the righteous and his ears are attentive to their cry …

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Teach the class about friendship. Some friends are good and helpful; others can be a bad influence on us.

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. Lot lived in the valley of the River Jordan, where the kings or chiefs of the various tribes waged war against each other. It is not necessary to teach them the names of these rulers. They were evil men, and as Lot lived near them he was caught up in their fighting and taken prisoner (14:11-12).

2. A person who had escaped ran and told Abraham that Lot had been captured. Perhaps Lot deserved to run into trouble, as he had chosen to live among those bad people (see Lesson 14). But Abraham went to rescue him at once (14:13-14).

3. Abraham then proved he was a very good soldier as well as a good husbandman (keeper of livestock). He took over 300 of his armed men and marched them during the night to the place where Lot was being held prisoner. He defeated the enemy kings and took back all the property and people they had taken (14:14-16).

4. The king of Sodom led the other kings in a procession to honour Abraham and reward him for saving their people. They wanted to present him with many gifts, but Abraham refused. He would not accept even a sandal strap from them for himself; he did not want to be under obligation to ungodly kings (14:17, 21-24).

5. Another king also came to meet Abraham. His name was Melchizedek, the king of Salem (Salem means “Peace”). He was a very different leader from the wicked king of Sodom. He worshipped the true God. Show Picture 15. Melchizedek brought simple gifts of food and drink to Abraham and blessed him. Abraham respected Melchizedek and presented him with gifts (14:18-20).

Instil the Memory Verse

Our Response to the Story

Abraham and Lot had different friends. The people whom Lot mixed with were wicked and led him into trouble. Abraham refused to even accept gifts from wicked people.

Abraham’s friendship with Melchizedek was one that honoured God. Melchizedek also taught Abraham that it was God who had delivered his enemies into his hands (14:20). This is what healthy friendships look like.

Lesson - 15: ABRAHAM RESCUES LOT

Scripture Reading: Genesis 14:8-24

1. Good Friend or Bad Friend?

Write G for a good friend or B for a bad friend:

  1. Helps me to do right. ( ___ ) 👉 G

  2. Tells me to tell lies. ( ___ ) 👉 B

  3. Shares and prays with me. ( ___ ) 👉 G

  4. Makes me disobey my parents. ( ___ ) 👉 B

2. Match the Following

A B
Lot Was taken prisoner
Abraham Rescued Lot at once
Melchizedek King of Salem who blessed Abraham
King of Sodom Offered gifts that Abraham refused

Answers:
Lot → Was taken prisoner
Abraham → Rescued Lot at once
Melchizedek → King of Salem who blessed Abraham
King of Sodom → Offered gifts that Abraham refused

3. Circle the Right Words

Circle the correct words:

  1. Abraham went to rescue Lot (at once / much later). 👉 at once

  2. Abraham (took / refused) gifts from the wicked king. 👉 refused

  3. Salem means (Peace / War). 👉 Peace

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did Lot get into trouble?

    Sample answer: He lived among wicked people and was caught up in their fighting.

  2. What kind of friend was Abraham to Lot?

    Sample answer: A faithful friend who went to rescue him at once.

  3. How can we choose good friends?

    Sample answer: Choose friends who love God and help us to do right.

5. Memory Verse:

Psalm 34:15 — “The eyes of the Lord are on the righteous, and his ears are attentive to their cry.”

1 Corinthians 15:33 — “Bad company corrupts good character.”

Craft 1: Friendship Bracelet

Materials: Paper strips, crayons, glue or tape

Instructions:

  1. Decorate a paper strip with hearts and stars.

  2. Write on it: “Friends who love God.”

  3. Glue the ends to make a bracelet and give it to a friend.

Lesson Point: Good friends help us to honour God.

Craft 2: Bread and Cup Picture

Materials: Paper, crayons, scissors, glue

Instructions:

  1. Draw a table with bread and a cup on it.

  2. Cut out and glue a paper crown beside it.

  3. Write: “Melchizedek blessed Abraham.”

Lesson Point: Godly friends bless one another.

Game 1: Rescue Mission

How to Play:

  1. Put a toy “Lot” in a corner marked as the enemy’s camp.

  2. Children walk quietly in a line, like Abraham’s men, to “rescue” him.

  3. Bring him back and clap together.

Lesson Point: Abraham went at once to help his family.

Game 2: Good Friend, Bad Friend

How to Play:

  1. Call out actions, such as “shares” or “tells lies”.

  2. If it is a good friend, children stand up; if bad, they sit down.

  3. Discuss why.

Lesson Point: We should choose friends who lead us towards God.
