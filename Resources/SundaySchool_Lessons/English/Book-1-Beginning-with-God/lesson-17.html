Ishmael is Born

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 1: Beginning with God

Aim

To teach the children that when we try to do things our way, it may bring sorrow and suffering to many people.

Scripture Reading

Genesis 16:1-16

Memory Verse

Proverbs 3:5

Trust in the Lord with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding …

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Sometimes people are impatient. When they plant seeds they expect to see signs of growth within a day. Children especially often cannot wait, so they dig up the seeds to see what is happening.

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. Abraham and Sarah had been married much longer than ten years but they still had no children. Yet God had promised them as many descendants as there are stars in the sky! Show Picture 16. Abraham kept on believing God.

2. Sarah could not wait any longer. She arranged for Abraham to have a child by her Egyptian servant girl, Hagar (16:1-4).

3. From the time Hagar became pregnant with Abraham’s child there was no peace in the household. Hagar became proud and despised Sarah, who had not been able to have a child, while Sarah was jealous of Hagar (16:4-5).

4. Abraham did not stop the quarrels. Instead, they became worse. Finally, Hagar ran away (16:6).

5. God sent an angel to comfort Hagar and tell her that she would have a son who would be called Ishmael, which means “God hears”. The angel told her that this son would have many descendants … but he also would fight against everybody else. Hagar obediently returned to Sarah (16:7-12). Ishmael became the forefather of the Arab people.

6. Hagar’s baby was born and she named him Ishmael. Show Picture 17. Not everyone in the picture is really celebrating the birth of this child. Sarah, who arranged it all, looks very unhappy (16:15).

Instil the Memory Verse

Our Response to the Story

Sometimes we try to do things our way. Sarah and Abraham did not wait for a son of their own to arrive in God’s good time. They became impatient and doubted God. Had he forgotten them? When we follow our own heads we may bring sorrow and suffering to many, as Abraham and Sarah did to all their descendants. We need to trust God and wait for him to show the way ahead.

Lesson - 17: ISHMAEL IS BORN

Scripture Reading: Genesis 16:1-16

1. Choose the Right Answer

Circle a), b) or c):

  1. Who was Hagar?
    a) Sarah’s servant b) A queen c) Abraham’s sister 👉 a) Sarah’s servant

  2. What does Ishmael mean?
    a) He laughs b) God hears c) Peace 👉 b) God hears

  3. Who tried to do things her own way?
    a) Hagar b) Sarah c) An angel 👉 b) Sarah

2. Unscramble the Words

Use the clue to help you:

  1. GRAHA (Sarah’s servant) → __________ 👉 HAGAR

  2. LAMSIHE (Hagar’s son) → __________ 👉 ISHMAEL

  3. TIAW (what Sarah did not do) → __________ 👉 WAIT

3. Wait or Rush?

Write W for waiting on God, or R for rushing ahead:

  1. Pray and wait for God to show the way. ( ___ ) 👉 W

  2. Do it my own way quickly. ( ___ ) 👉 R

  3. Plant a seed and wait for it to grow. ( ___ ) 👉 W

  4. Dig up the seed to see if it has grown. ( ___ ) 👉 R

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did Sarah give Hagar to Abraham?

    Sample answer: She was tired of waiting for God’s promise.

  2. What happened when they did it their own way?

    Sample answer: There was quarrelling, jealousy and sadness in the family.

  3. What can we do when we have to wait?

    Sample answer: Pray, trust God and be patient.

5. Memory Verse:

Proverbs 3:5 — “Trust in the Lord with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding.”

Psalm 27:14 — “Wait for the Lord; be strong and take heart and wait for the Lord.”

Craft 1: Bean Seed in a Cup

Materials: Clear plastic cup, cotton wool, a bean seed, water

Instructions:

  1. Put wet cotton wool in the cup.

  2. Place a bean seed in the middle against the glass.

  3. Write on the cup: “Wait for God.”

  4. Look at it each day and see it grow slowly.

Lesson Point: Good things grow when we wait for God’s time.

Craft 2: God Hears Me Card

Materials: Folded paper, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Draw a big ear on the front of the card.

  2. Inside write: “Ishmael means God hears.”

  3. Draw yourself praying.

Lesson Point: God hears us when we cry to Him.

Game 1: Statue Patience

How to Play:

  1. Children freeze like statues when you say “Wait”.

  2. See who can stay still the longest.

  3. Talk about how hard it is to wait for something we want.

Lesson Point: Waiting on God takes patience.

Game 2: Listening Game

How to Play:

  1. One child whispers a short sentence to the next.

  2. Pass it around the circle.

  3. Compare the first and last sentence and talk about how God hears every word we say.

Lesson Point: God always hears us.
