A Time of Great Trouble

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 1: Beginning with God

Aim

To teach the children that even great suffering did not turn Job away from God.

Scripture Reading

Job 1:13-22

Memory Verse

Job 1:21

[Job] said: “Naked I came from my mother’s womb, and naked I shall depart. The Lord gave and the Lord has taken away; may the name of the Lord be praised.”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Ask the children to tell the class about some happy days in their lives. Perhaps they are also willing to share some sad memories.

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. Job used to be a very happy man. Show Picture 9. Lead the class by means of questions through a revision of Lesson 9. Highlight the following facts: Job was a rich, honest and happy family man. He served God without expecting any reward. He had many happy days. But Satan was out to test him. There came a day in his life when he received all sorts of terrible news.

2. A messenger arrived to tell Job that enemies had stolen all his donkeys and oxen after having killed the servants who were looking after them (1:13-15).

3. Hot on his heels came a second servant with the news that lightning had struck all his sheep and their shepherds (1:16).

4. Then a third servant came, telling Job that a neighbouring tribe had taken off with all his camels after having killed their keepers (1:17).

5. The fourth servant brought the worst news. Job’s sons and daughters were having a feast in the eldest brother’s house when a great wind destroyed the house, killing all his children in one blow (1:18-19).

6. Job accepted the terrible news his servants reported. He did not punish the servants who had escaped with their lives to tell the story; it was not their fault. Nor was he angry with God. Show Picture 10. Job was just very sad, as his actions show (1:20-21).

Instil the Memory Verse

This is a relatively long verse. If the children are young, teach only the second half (starting from “The Lord gave …”).

Our Response to the Story

Trouble often drives people away from God. But Job knew that God is sovereign and the source of all that is good (1:21). Therefore, his troubles brought him closer to God. This is an example we should follow.

Lesson - 10: A TIME OF GREAT TROUBLE

Scripture Reading: Job 1:13-22

1. Put the Bad News in Order

a) A strong wind destroyed the house of Job’s children.
b) Lightning struck Job’s sheep.
c) Enemies took Job’s donkeys and oxen.
d) Enemies took Job’s camels.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. Enemies took Job’s donkeys and oxen.

  2. Lightning struck Job’s sheep.

  3. Enemies took Job’s camels.

  4. A strong wind destroyed the house of Job’s children.

2. Circle the Right Words

Circle the correct words:

  1. Job (praised / cursed) God in his trouble. 👉 praised

  2. Job was very (sad / happy). 👉 sad

  3. Job knew that God is (in control / far away). 👉 in control

3. Missing Letters

Write the missing letter:

  1. _OB (the man in our story) __________ 👉 J

  2. PRA_SE (what Job did to God) __________ 👉 I

  3. FAI_H (trusting God) __________ 👉 T

  4. TRU_T (rely on God) __________ 👉 S

4. Discussion Questions

  1. How did Job feel when he heard the bad news?

    Sample answer: He was very sad, but he did not blame God.

  2. What did Job do when bad things happened?

    Sample answer: He worshipped God and praised Him.

  3. What can we do when we feel sad?

    Sample answer: We can pray, trust God and tell someone who loves us.

5. Memory Verse:

Job 1:21 — “The Lord gave and the Lord has taken away; may the name of the Lord be praised.”

1 Thessalonians 5:18 — “Give thanks in all circumstances.”

Craft 1: Happy and Sad Face Stick

Materials: Paper plate, craft stick, crayons, glue

Instructions:

  1. Draw a happy face on one side of a paper plate and a sad face on the other.

  2. Glue a craft stick to the bottom as a handle.

  3. Turn the plate to show how Job felt, then write on the back: “God is still good.”

Lesson Point: Even when we are sad, we can still trust God.

Craft 2: Praise Banner

Materials: Long paper strip, crayons, stickers

Instructions:

  1. Write “May the name of the Lord be praised” on the strip.

  2. Decorate with stars, hearts and flowers.

  3. Hang the banner in the classroom or at home.

Lesson Point: Job praised God in good times and in bad times.

Game 1: Happy Day, Sad Day

How to Play:

  1. Call out happy or sad events, such as “It is your birthday” or “You lost your toy”.

  2. Children show a happy or sad face.

  3. After each sad event, all say together: “God is still good.”

Lesson Point: Our feelings change, but God does not.

Game 2: Praise Pass

How to Play:

  1. Sit in a circle and play music.

  2. Pass a small object around; when the music stops, that child says one way to praise God.

  3. Continue for several rounds.

Lesson Point: We can praise God at all times.
