Finding a Wife for Isaac

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 1: Beginning with God

Aim

To teach the children that God guides us clearly when we trust him.

Scripture Reading

Genesis 24:1-31; 24:50-67

Memory Verse

Genesis 24:27

“Praise be to the Lord … who has not abandoned his kindness and faithful- ness …”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Ask the children to tell you about the preparations their families make for a wedding.

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. Abraham was a very old man by now and his son, Isaac, was still unmarried. So he called his trusted servant and told him to go and find a wife for Isaac. The servant had to travel a long way, back to the land Abraham had come from. That was because Abraham did not want Isaac to leave the Promised Land, but he also didn’t want him to take a wife from the heathen tribes among whom they were living. Abraham made the servant swear a special oath that he would follow his master’s instructions. Show Picture 21. Abraham also told the servant that God would guide him in his search (24:1-9).

2. The servant took 10 of Abraham’s camels loaded with gifts. He travelled to the city of Nahor and asked God to guide him to find the right wife for Isaac (24:10-14).

3. As the servant was resting his camels at the well outside the city, a beautiful young woman named Rebekah came to fetch water for her family. She willingly drew water for Abraham’s servant and his camels. Then the servant knew she was the right wife for Isaac (24:15-21).

4. When the servant went to meet Rebekah’s family, he discovered that she was the daughter of Abraham’s own brother! He praised God for guiding him and answering his prayer (24:22-27).

5. Abraham’s servant was welcomed into Rebekah’s home and the marriage arrangements were made straight away (24:28-31, 50-61). The servant repeatedly thanked God for his faithful guidance.

6. That is how Rebekah became Isaac’s wife (24:62-67).

7. Abraham died when he was 175 years old. All his life he had trusted God to guide him. God had called Abraham to follow him and he blessed Abraham and his descendants, just as he had promised he would.

Instil the Memory Verse

Our Response to the Story

We can learn from the example of this servant, who knew he could not carry out Abraham’s instructions without God’s help. So he prayed and then he repeatedly thanked God for his faithful guidance. He also told other people how God had guided him.

Share an example of God’s guidance in your own life.

Lesson - 21: FINDING A WIFE FOR ISAAC

Scripture Reading: Genesis 24:1-31; 24:50-67

1. Match the Following

A B
The servant Travelled with ten camels
Rebekah Gave water to the servant and camels
Isaac Abraham’s son
Abraham Said God would guide the servant

Answers:
The servant → Travelled with ten camels
Rebekah → Gave water to the servant and camels
Isaac → Abraham’s son
Abraham → Said God would guide the servant

2. Choose the Right Answer

Circle a), b) or c):

  1. Where did the servant stop to rest his camels?
    a) At a well b) At a mountain c) At a market 👉 a) At a well

  2. What did the servant do first?
    a) Went to sleep b) Prayed to God c) Bought gifts 👉 b) Prayed to God

3. Put These Events in Order

a) Rebekah became Isaac’s wife.
b) The servant prayed at the well.
c) Abraham sent the servant to find a wife for Isaac.
d) Rebekah gave water to the camels.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. Abraham sent the servant to find a wife for Isaac.

  2. The servant prayed at the well.

  3. Rebekah gave water to the camels.

  4. Rebekah became Isaac’s wife.

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did the servant pray before looking for a wife?

    Sample answer: He knew he needed God’s help and guidance.

  2. How did the servant show he was thankful?

    Sample answer: He praised God and told others how God guided him.

  3. When can we ask God for help?

    Sample answer: Any time, in everything we need.

5. Memory Verse:

Genesis 24:27 — “Praise be to the Lord, the God of my master Abraham, who has not abandoned his kindness and faithfulness.”

Proverbs 3:6 — “In all your ways submit to him, and he will make your paths straight.”

Craft 1: Paper Camel

Materials: Brown paper, scissors, glue, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Cut a camel body, a long neck and four legs from brown paper.

  2. Glue the pieces together.

  3. Draw the eye and a blanket on its back.

  4. Write: “God guides me.”

Lesson Point: God guides us clearly when we trust Him.

Craft 2: Well and Water Jar Card

Materials: Paper, blue crayon, scissors

Instructions:

  1. Draw a round well and a water jar on a folded card.

  2. Colour blue water drops.

  3. Write inside: “Thank You, God, for answering prayer.”

Lesson Point: We should pray and thank God for His help.

Game 1: Camel Caravan

How to Play:

  1. Children walk on hands and knees in a line like camels.

  2. The leader is the servant and stops at the “well” in the corner.

  3. Each camel says one thing they thank God for.

Lesson Point: God guides those who trust Him on the journey.

Game 2: Who Will Help?

How to Play:

  1. Place a toy cup at the end of the room.

  2. Call out “I am thirsty!” and see who offers help first.

  3. Talk about Rebekah’s kindness.

Lesson Point: Kindness shows love to others and to God.
