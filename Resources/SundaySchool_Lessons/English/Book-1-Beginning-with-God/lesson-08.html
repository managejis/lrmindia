The Tower of Babel

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 1: Beginning with God

Aim

To show the children that mankind’s pride separates us from God. Pride also separates people from each other.

Scripture Reading

Genesis 11:1-9

Memory Verse

Proverbs 11:2

When pride comes, then comes disgrace …
NOTE: The word “Babel” has two meanings: (1) the court or gate of God and (2) confusion. In English, “babble” describes a confused sound with no meaning.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Get the children to all talk at once. Once they have created a fair racket, stop them and ask if they could hear what everyone was saying. No! They could not, because there was so much noise and confusion.

If the class consists of members from various language groups, ask each child to greet the class in his or her mother tongue. Ask the class if they could understand every language. Many people can speak several languages, but no-one speaks all the languages of the African continent, let alone the world!

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. Today we hear how the Bible explains the existence of so many different languages among the human family. Show Picture 5. Ask the class if they remember the story of Noah. He obeyed God, built an enormous ark and saved his family from God’s judgement. Noah’s family grew. They became many people, but they all still spoke one language (11:1).

2. People eventually learnt to bake bricks like we do today, and decided to build a city with a mighty tower. The builders wanted to show off how clever they were. They had forgotten that it was God who had given them their building skills (11:1-4).

3. God had to cut down their pride and limit their wickedness. He did this by mixing up their language so that they could not understand each other. Show Picture 8. Point out the confusion and anger on the builders’ faces (11:5-7).

4. The building project was abandoned and people scattered again, inhabiting all the earth, as God had originally instructed them (1:28; 11:8-9).

Instil the Memory Verse

Our Response to the Story

When babies have just learnt to walk they are very proud of their achievement. They will run off in one direction, even though it may be into danger, and refuse to come back. Someone has to go and collect them. Often, we are like such babies, very proud of ourselves. We want to be independent of God. But God did not make us that way. He wants us to have fellowship with him by obeying Him.

Lesson - 8: THE TOWER OF BABEL

Scripture Reading: Genesis 11:1-9

1. Put These Events in Order

a) God mixed up their language.
b) All the people spoke one language.
c) The people scattered over the earth.
d) The people built a city with a tall tower.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. All the people spoke one language.

  2. The people built a city with a tall tower.

  3. God mixed up their language.

  4. The people scattered over the earth.

2. Choose the Right Answer

Circle a), b) or c):

  1. What did the people want to build?
    a) A boat b) A city with a tower c) A garden 👉 b) A city with a tower

  2. How did God stop the work?
    a) With rain b) By mixing their language c) With fire 👉 b) By mixing their language

  3. What filled the builders’ hearts?
    a) Pride b) Fear c) Sleep 👉 a) Pride

3. Proud or Humble?

Write P for proud or H for humble:

  1. “Look how clever I am!” ( ___ ) 👉 P

  2. “Thank You, God, for my skills.” ( ___ ) 👉 H

  3. “I don’t need God’s help.” ( ___ ) 👉 P

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did the people want to build the tower?

    Sample answer: They wanted to show off how clever they were.

  2. Why did God mix up their language?

    Sample answer: To stop their pride and wickedness.

  3. How can we thank God for the things we can do?

    Sample answer: By remembering that God gave us our skills and using them to honour Him.

5. Memory Verse:

Proverbs 11:2 — “When pride comes, then comes disgrace, but with humility comes wisdom.”

1 Peter 5:5 — “God opposes the proud but gives grace to the humble.”

Craft 1: Brick Tower

Materials: Red and brown paper, scissors, glue, a sheet of card

Instructions:

  1. Cut many small brick shapes from the red and brown paper.

  2. Glue the bricks in rows to build a tall tower on the card.

  3. Write at the bottom: “God gave me my skills.”

Lesson Point: Everything we can do is a gift from God.

Craft 2: Hello in Many Languages

Materials: Paper, pencil, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Fold a paper to make a card.

  2. Write “Hello” in different languages the children know on the front.

  3. Draw a heart inside and write: “God made every language.”

Lesson Point: Pride separates people, but God wants us to live together in peace.

Game 1: Babble Noise

How to Play:

  1. Ask all the children to talk at once for 10 seconds.

  2. Stop them and ask: “Could you understand anyone?”

  3. Then let one child speak at a time and notice the difference.

Lesson Point: Confusion came to Babel when people turned from God.

Game 2: Cup Tower Challenge

How to Play:

  1. Give each team plastic cups.

  2. Teams try to build the tallest tower they can.

  3. Afterwards, ask each team to say thank you to God for the hands and minds He gave.

Lesson Point: We should be humble and thank God for our skills.
