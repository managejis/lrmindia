Job is Restored

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 1: Beginning with God

Aim

To teach the children that God reigns. He allowed Job to suffer, but in the end blessed Job for his faith and steadfastness.

Scripture Reading

Job 38:1-4; 42:1-7

Memory Verse

Job 42:2

“I know that you can do all things; no plan of yours can be thwarted.”
NOTE: In chapters 38 – 41 God reveals himself to Job. Through reading them, you will glimpse a picture of the greatness of God. In this lesson, use some of the examples of God’s power in creation to show what God taught Job.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

If you are sick and go to the clinic, the nurse or doctor may order a blood test. In a blood test, a drop of blood is examined under a microscope (a glass or lens that makes things look much bigger than they are). This makes it possible for the laboratory technician to see things in the blood that one cannot see with the naked eye.

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. Job was suffering much hardship and he did not know why. Show Picture 11. This made him so sad and miserable that he even wished he had never been born (3:11). His wife and friends were no help to him.

2. Then God spoke to Job. God showed that he alone knows all things, for he is the One who made everything, the Creator. No human being can understand God’s amazing ways. God bombarded Job with questions about the creation, but Job couldn’t answer any of them (38:1-41). That made him realise how great God is; it also made him ashamed of trying to accuse God and argue with him (42:3-6). Like when a laboratory technician looks through a microscope at a drop of blood, this encounter with God opened Job’s eyes for God’s hidden actions and wisdom.

3. God instructed Job to pray for his friends. Job was the one who had tried to argue with God, but they were the ones with whom God was angry! These people had tried to convince Job that his terrible suffering was fair punishment for something very wicked he had done. They couldn’t have been more wrong (see Lesson 11, points 5 and 6), but in the end God showed them mercy (42:7-9).

4. God once again showered Job with blessings and grace; God made him well again and twice as rich as he was before. Show Picture 12. Everyone came to celebrate with him and brought him gifts (42:10-11).

5. God continued to bless Job even more than he had before. He had seven more sons and three beautiful daughters, and lived to see many of his descendants (42:12-17).

Instil the Memory Verse

Our Response to the Story

Job is an example of unwavering trust in God – in suffering as well as in blessing. This man knew that God was the only One he could turn to.

We must remember that God loves us and that we cannot understand his ways. In the end, he wants only what is best for those who serve him faithfully.

Joni Eareckson Tada lives in America. When she was 17 she had a diving accident, and for over 20 years she has not walked or used her hands. She has learnt many skills like typing, driving a car, and painting beautiful pictures by holding a stick in her mouth. She has had to learn to trust God and to be very patient through all her suffering. She has said that her great handicaps have deepened her love for God.

Lesson - 12: JOB IS RESTORED

Scripture Reading: Job 38:1-4; 42:1-7

1. Put the Story in Order

a) God blessed Job twice as much.
b) Job lost everything.
c) Job prayed for his friends.
d) God spoke to Job.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. Job lost everything.

  2. God spoke to Job.

  3. Job prayed for his friends.

  4. God blessed Job twice as much.

2. Who Can? God or People

Write G if only God can, or P if people can too:

  1. Make the sun and the stars. ( ___ ) 👉 G

  2. Know everything. ( ___ ) 👉 G

  3. Ride a bike. ( ___ ) 👉 P

  4. Build a house. ( ___ ) 👉 P

3. Complete the Sentences

Word box: twice, friends, creation

  1. God asked Job questions about ____________. __________ 👉 creation

  2. Job prayed for his ____________. __________ 👉 friends

  3. God gave Job ____________ as much as before. __________ 👉 twice

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did Job feel small when God spoke?

    Sample answer: He saw how great and wise God is.

  2. Who is in control of everything?

    Sample answer: God is.

  3. What can we do when we do not understand why bad things happen?

    Sample answer: Trust God, because He loves us and knows what is best.

5. Memory Verse:

Job 42:2 — “I know that you can do all things; no plan of yours can be thwarted.”

Luke 1:37 — “For nothing is impossible with God.”

Craft 1: Wonders of God Collage

Materials: Paper, crayons or magazine pictures, glue

Instructions:

  1. Fold a paper into four parts.

  2. In each part draw or glue something God made: sun, stars, animals, sea.

  3. Write at the top: “God made it all.”

Lesson Point: God is great and made everything.

Craft 2: Blessings Chain

Materials: Paper strips, glue, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Write one blessing from God on each paper strip.

  2. Glue the strips into a chain with the ends joined.

  3. Hang the chain up and count the blessings together.

Lesson Point: God blesses those who trust Him.

Game 1: God Made It!

How to Play:

  1. Go around the circle naming something God made, in turns.

  2. Each child must add a new thing without repeating.

  3. Anyone who cannot think of a new thing says “God is great!” and the next child continues.

Lesson Point: God made everything and knows everything.

Game 2: Magnifying Look

How to Play:

  1. Give each child a magnifying glass or a paper tube.

  2. They look closely at a leaf, an insect picture or their finger.

  3. Talk about the tiny details God made that we cannot see easily.

Lesson Point: God’s wisdom is bigger than we can understand.
