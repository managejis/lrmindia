The Rainbow – God’s Promise

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 1: Beginning with God

Aim

To teach the children that when God makes a promise, he always keeps it.

Scripture Reading

Genesis 8:1-22; 9:8-12

Memory Verse

Genesis 8:22

“As long as the earth en- dures, seedtime and har- vest … will never cease.”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Talk to the class about promises. Give examples of promises that have been made and kept and promises that have been broken.

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. God made promises to Noah: He and his family would be kept safe if he followed God’s plan and built an ark for them and the animals God told him to gather from all over. Show Picture 5. Noah obeyed God and they were indeed kept safe inside the ark. But all other creatures on land and in the air died. Show Picture 6.

2. Noah and his family and the animals were shut up in the ark for many months. Had God forgotten them? The rain stopped, and after seven months the boat came to rest on some mountains (7:24 – 8:5).

3. After eleven months in the boat Noah let out a raven, a large black bird that feeds on dead meat (8:5-7).

4. Then Noah sent out a dove, but at first it came back because it did not find anywhere to rest. Describe how Noah learnt from the dove about the state of the world outside (8:8-12).

5. A year after he had taken his family and the animals into the ark, Noah let everybody out. The first thing Noah and his family did was to worship God. They thanked him for fulfilling his promise to keep them safe. Show Picture 7. They built an altar and made a sacrifice. God was pleased (8:13-19).

6. God promised he would never again destroy the earth by water. He promised there would always be planting and harvesting and normal seasons. God put the rainbow in the sky as a sign to remind people that he would always keep his promise. Whenever we see a rainbow, we should remember that we can rely on God’s word.

Instil the Memory Verse

Our Response to the Story

Ask the class to look around them and name everything that is a colour of the rainbow. (The seven colours of the rainbow are: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo and violet.) Let these things remind us that God always keeps his promises. We can count on God to do what he says.

Lesson - 7: THE RAINBOW – GOD’S PROMISE

Scripture Reading: Genesis 8:1-22; 9:8-12

1. Yes or No

Write Yes or No:

  1. Did God forget Noah in the ark? ( ___ ) 👉 No

  2. Did Noah send out a raven and a dove? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

  3. Did Noah worship God after leaving the ark? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

  4. Did God promise never to flood the whole earth again? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

2. Unscramble the Words

Use the clue to help you:

  1. WNIOBAR (a sign in the sky) → __________ 👉 RAINBOW

  2. VOED (a white bird) → __________ 👉 DOVE

  3. EPSIMOR (God always keeps it) → __________ 👉 PROMISE

  4. RALTA (Noah built one to worship God) → __________ 👉 ALTAR

3. Complete the Sentences

Word box: promises, worship, rainbow

  1. God put the ____________ in the sky as a sign. __________ 👉 rainbow

  2. God always keeps His ____________. __________ 👉 promises

  3. Noah and his family built an altar to ____________ God. __________ 👉 worship

4. Discussion Questions

  1. What did God promise after the flood?

    Sample answer: He promised never to destroy the earth by water again.

  2. Why did Noah build an altar?

    Sample answer: To thank and worship God for keeping his family safe.

  3. What should we remember when we see a rainbow?

    Sample answer: That God always keeps His promises.

5. Memory Verse:

Genesis 8:22 — “As long as the earth endures, seedtime and harvest, cold and heat, summer and winter, day and night will never cease.”

Psalm 145:13 — “The LORD is trustworthy in all he promises.”

Craft 1: Dove with an Olive Leaf

Materials: White paper, green paper, scissors, glue, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Cut a dove shape from white paper.

  2. Cut a small green leaf and glue it to the dove’s beak.

  3. Colour the eye and wings.

  4. Write: “God keeps His promises.”

Lesson Point: Noah learnt from the dove about the world outside the ark.

Craft 2: Rainbow Streamer Ring

Materials: Paper plate, coloured crepe paper or ribbons, scissors, glue

Instructions:

  1. Cut the centre out of a paper plate to make a ring.

  2. Glue seven coloured streamers (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet) to the ring.

  3. Hold it up and wave it, saying: “God always keeps His word.”

Lesson Point: Whenever we see a rainbow, we can rely on God’s word.

Game 1: Rainbow Colour Hunt

How to Play:

  1. Children look around the room or outside for things in each rainbow colour.

  2. They name each thing they find.

  3. The child who names a thing for all seven colours first wins.

Lesson Point: God’s rainbow reminds us that He keeps His promises.

Game 2: Promise Keepers

How to Play:

  1. Make a promise to each child, for example: “I promise to give you a sticker at the end.”

  2. Keep every promise before the children leave.

  3. Talk about how it feels when a promise is kept or broken.

Lesson Point: God always does what He says.
