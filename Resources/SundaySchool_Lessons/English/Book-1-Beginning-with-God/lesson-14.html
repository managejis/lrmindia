Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 1: Beginning with God

Aim

To teach the children how, as he continued to trust in God, Abraham was able to settle a quarrel wisely.

Scripture Reading

Genesis 13:5-18

Memory Verse

Psalm 37:5-6

Commit your way to the Lord; trust in him and he will … make your right- eousness shine like the dawn …

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Ask two adults or two children (they may be members of the class, but not necessarily) to meet you before the session. Make up a little play about a quarrel. Practice it beforehand so you are ready to perform it in front of the class at the beginning of the lesson.

1st person: You have my pen! 2nd person: Oh no, I haven’t. This one’s my own. 1st person: No, it isn’t. I saw you pick it up from my table. 2nd person: …

Stop the play before a fight begins!

Ask the class what you have acted out. There has been a quarrel about a lost pen.

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. Abraham obeyed God’s command and took his whole household to a new land. Show Picture 13. Abraham and Lot (his nephew) were very rich. They owned large numbers of animals. They found there was not enough land for grazing all these animals. Abraham and Lot’s herdsmen began to fight over grazing land (13:5-7).

2. Abraham, the older and wiser man, called on Lot to settle the problem. They needed to go their separate ways so that all the animals would have enough grazing (13:4-9).

3. Show Picture 14. Abraham let Lot choose where he wanted to go. Usually younger people give way to older ones. Lot chose the beautiful, fertile valley of the River Jordan. There were cities to live in and life was relatively easy. But the city of Sodom, close to where Lot lived, had many wicked people (13:10-13).

4. Abraham stayed in the land of Canaan – a hilly, less fertile area. God had blessed Abraham and had promised him land and many descendants. But so far Abraham owned no land and had no children (13:14-18)!

Instil the Memory Verse

Our Response to the Story

If we look around us most of us can see people who seem to have an easy life but do not really worship God. They look for and follow the easy route, like Lot. They have their reward immediately.

Abraham continued to trust in God’s promises. His life was not easy but he knew trusting God would be the best way in the end. It still is the best way.

Lesson - 14: LOT’S CHOICE

Scripture Reading: Genesis 13:5-18

1. Abraham or Lot?

Write A for Abraham or L for Lot:

  1. Let the other person choose first. ( ___ ) 👉 A

  2. Chose the green valley of the Jordan. ( ___ ) 👉 L

  3. Stayed in the hilly land of Canaan. ( ___ ) 👉 A

  4. Lived near wicked Sodom. ( ___ ) 👉 L

2. Choose the Right Answer

Circle a), b) or c):

  1. Why did the herdsmen quarrel?
    a) Not enough grazing land b) A lost pen c) A broken tent 👉 a) Not enough grazing land

  2. Who settled the problem wisely?
    a) Lot b) Abraham c) Sarah 👉 b) Abraham

3. Peacemaker Choices

Circle the choice that makes peace:

  1. When we quarrel, we can (shout / say sorry). 👉 say sorry

  2. We can (share / grab) our toys. 👉 share

  3. We should (let others choose first / push to the front). 👉 let others choose first

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did Abraham let Lot choose first?

    Sample answer: He wanted peace and trusted God to take care of him.

  2. Why was Lot’s choice not the wisest?

    Sample answer: He chose the easy way and lived near wicked people.

  3. How can we stop a quarrel at home or school?

    Sample answer: By saying sorry, sharing, and letting others choose.

5. Memory Verse:

Psalm 37:5-6 — “Commit your way to the Lord; trust in him and he will do this: He will make your righteousness shine like the dawn.”

Matthew 5:9 — “Blessed are the peacemakers, for they will be called children of God.”

Craft 1: Two Paths Picture

Materials: Paper, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Draw two paths: one to a green valley and one to the hills.

  2. Write on the green path: “Easy way?” and on the hill path: “God’s way.”

  3. Colour the picture.

Lesson Point: Trusting God’s way is always best.

Craft 2: Peacemaker Badge

Materials: Paper circle, crayons, safety pin or tape

Instructions:

  1. Cut a circle from paper.

  2. Draw a dove in the middle and colour it.

  3. Write: “I am a peacemaker.” and wear it.

Lesson Point: Peacemakers are called children of God.

Game 1: Quarrel Play

How to Play:

  1. Two children act out a quarrel over a toy, then stop before it gets bad.

  2. The class suggests how to make peace.

  3. Act it out again with a peaceful ending.

Lesson Point: Wise people solve quarrels peacefully.

Game 2: Share the Treats

How to Play:

  1. Place a few treats or counters on a table and tell the children to share.

  2. They agree how to divide them fairly so that everyone has enough.

  3. Talk about how Abraham let Lot choose first.

Lesson Point: Giving way to others makes peace.
