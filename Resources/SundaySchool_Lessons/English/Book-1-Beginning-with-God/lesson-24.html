Thomas Meets the Risen Jesus

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 1: Beginning with God

Aim

To teach the children that we are freed from sin by believing in Jesus Christ, the Son of God.

Scripture Reading

John 20:19-31

Memory Verse

John 20:29

Then Jesus told him, “Be- cause you have seen me, you have believed; blessed are those who have not seen and yet have believed.”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Tell the class about some surprising news you once received – so surprising that at first you did not believe it. Your example must refer to good news.

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. Jesus’ friends were very sad and frightened. Why? Show Picture 23. Jesus had been crucified. But on the third day after his death, Jesus came to life. Death could not hold God’s Son in its grasp.

2. Describe how the disciples were together on the Sunday after Jesus was crucified. They were hiding, because they feared that Jesus’ enemies would try to kill them, too. Suddenly Jesus was there with them. They could not believe it! Was it really, truly Jesus himself? He showed them the crucifixion marks on his hands and feet and where the soldier pierced his side with a spear. Then, convinced, they were overcome with happiness to see him (20:19-20).

3. Thomas was not with the other disciples on that occasion, and just could not believe the good news when they told him. He insisted that he first would have to see and touch the marks of the crucifixion on Jesus’ body before he would believe that Jesus really was alive again (20:24-25).

4. A week later Jesus came to them again. This time Thomas was present. Show Picture 24. Recount Jesus’ words to Thomas and Thomas’s reply. Only after he had seen the risen Lord with his own eyes, Thomas realised who Jesus was (20:26-29).

5. When Jesus said to Thomas, “… blessed are those who have not seen and yet have believed,” he included those people of future generations who would understand who he was. That means Jesus was speaking of us, too. We cannot see him with our eyes. Yet we know he died and rose again. It is by believing in the living Lord Jesus that we are saved to enjoy an everlasting life with God. This is what the memory verse tells us.

Instil the Memory Verse

Our Response to the Story

Do not doubt like Thomas, that Jesus lives. Believe that Jesus is alive, as he said. Thank him for suffering and dying on the cross for you. Accept and worship him as your Lord, as Thomas did.

Lesson - 24: THOMAS MEETS THE RISEN JESUS

Scripture Reading: John 20:19-31

1. Put the Story in Order

a) Thomas said he would not believe unless he saw.
b) Jesus appeared again and Thomas believed.
c) The disciples hid in a room.
d) Jesus appeared to the disciples.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. The disciples hid in a room.

  2. Jesus appeared to the disciples.

  3. Thomas said he would not believe unless he saw.

  4. Jesus appeared again and Thomas believed.

2. Complete the Sentences

Word box: Lord, God, alive

  1. Thomas said, “My ____________ and my ____________!” __________ 👉 Lord, God

  2. Jesus is ____________ and lives forever. __________ 👉 alive

3. Match the Following

A B
Thomas Doubted at first
The disciples Hid in fear
Jesus Alive again
Blessed Believing without seeing

Answers:
Thomas → Doubted at first
The disciples → Hid in fear
Jesus → Alive again
Blessed → Believing without seeing

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did Thomas doubt?

    Sample answer: He had not seen Jesus alive.

  2. How did Thomas feel when he saw Jesus?

    Sample answer: He believed and worshipped Him.

  3. How can we believe in Jesus when we cannot see Him?

    Sample answer: By trusting what the Bible says and believing in our hearts.

5. Memory Verse:

John 20:29 — “Then Jesus told him, “Because you have seen me, you have believed; blessed are those who have not seen and yet have believed.””

Luke 24:6 — “He is not here; he has risen!”

Craft 1: Empty Tomb Scene

Materials: Paper plate, grey paper circle, glue, paper fastener

Instructions:

  1. Cut an opening in a paper plate to make the tomb.

  2. Attach a grey paper circle as the stone with a fastener so it rolls aside.

  3. Write behind the stone: “He is alive!”

Lesson Point: Jesus is alive and conquered death.

Craft 2: Believing Heart

Materials: Red paper, scissors, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Cut a heart from red paper.

  2. Write: “I believe in Jesus.”

  3. Decorate and hang it up at home.

Lesson Point: We are saved by believing in Jesus, the Son of God.

Game 1: Mystery Box

How to Play:

  1. Put an object in a box.

  2. Children feel it without looking and guess what it is.

  3. Talk about how Thomas needed to see and touch, but we believe without seeing.

Lesson Point: Blessed are those who believe without seeing.

Game 2: Good News Relay

How to Play:

  1. Place a card reading “Jesus is alive!” at the far end of the room.

  2. Teams race one by one to fetch it and shout the message.

  3. Everyone cheers together.

Lesson Point: We share the good news that Jesus lives.
