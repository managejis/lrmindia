Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 1: Beginning with God

Aim

To teach that God, because He is holy, must punish sin.

Scripture Reading

Genesis 7:1-24

Memory Verse

John 3:36

“Whoever believes in the Son has eternal life, but whoever rejects the Son will not see life, for God’s wrath remains on him.”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Before the meeting, work out a short play to be presented during this week’s session. The summary goes like this: A mother warns her child to be careful not to touch a pot in which food is cooking. However, the child takes no notice and knocks the pot over. The mother punishes the child.

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. God warned the inhabitants of the earth that their disobedience and wicked ways would result in death. But only Noah listened to God and obeyed his words. Show Picture 5. Noah gathered his family, along with all the animals God had instructed him to collect into the ark he had built. Then God himself shut the door behind them (7:1-9, 16).

2. You could introduce some activity into the lesson at this point by marking off a certain area and calling it the ark. The children should then walk in pairs through the “entrance”, saying what animal they are, and sit down “inside”. You could be Noah.

3. After seven days the rain began to fall. Describe how the rains came, referring to 7:10-12.

Show Picture 6. This is a very sad picture. God always keeps his promises, which means he does what he says. But people will not believe – or have faith in – his words.

4. The rain kept falling for 40 days and 40 nights. All the creatures of the land and the air died, but the ark with Noah and his family and the animals floated safely on the floodwaters (7:17-23).

Instil the Memory Verse

Our Response to the Story

Make up a story or use an account of something that really happened which shows that disobedience brings punishment – if not now, then later. For example: Children are warned not to play near rivers or go there alone. Still, every year children drown in the River Nile because they ignore this wise rule. We need to follow the example of Noah, who obeyed God.

God sent his Son to take the punishment we deserve. Obeying him leads to life.

Lesson - 6: THE FLOOD

Scripture Reading: Genesis 7:1-24

1. Who Am I?

Choose from: Noah, God, Rain

  1. I obeyed God and went into the ark. Who am I? __________ 👉 Noah

  2. I shut the door of the ark. Who am I? __________ 👉 God

  3. I fell for 40 days and 40 nights. Who am I? __________ 👉 Rain

2. Odd One Out

Circle the one that does not belong:

  1. ark – rain – animals – candy 👉 candy

  2. obeyed – listened – trusted – ignored 👉 ignored

  3. Noah – wife – sons – Pharaoh 👉 Pharaoh

3. Obey or Disobey?

Write O if it is obeying, or D if it is disobeying:

  1. Noah went into the ark when God told him. ( ___ ) 👉 O

  2. The people did not listen to God’s warning. ( ___ ) 👉 D

  3. A child touched the hot pot after Mother said no. ( ___ ) 👉 D

  4. I do what my parents tell me. ( ___ ) 👉 O

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did God send the flood?

    Sample answer: Because God is holy and must punish sin.

  2. Why was Noah safe in the ark?

    Sample answer: Noah listened to God and obeyed Him.

  3. How can we obey God every day?

    Sample answer: By obeying our parents and teachers, telling the truth and reading God’s Word.

5. Memory Verse:

John 3:36 — “Whoever believes in the Son has eternal life, but whoever rejects the Son will not see life, for God’s wrath remains on him.”

Genesis 7:5 — “And Noah did all that the LORD had commanded him.”

Craft 1: Ark Door Flap Card

Materials: Coloured paper, scissors, glue, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Fold a paper in half and draw a big ark on the front.

  2. Cut a small door flap in the ark.

  3. Under the flap, draw Noah’s family and the animals.

  4. Write: “God shut the door.”

Lesson Point: God keeps those who obey Him safe.

Craft 2: Rain Cloud Mobile

Materials: Paper, cotton, blue paper strips, string, glue

Instructions:

  1. Cut a cloud shape from paper and glue cotton on it.

  2. Glue blue paper strips (raindrops) hanging from the cloud.

  3. Count the raindrops together: 40 days and 40 nights.

  4. Write: “Obey God.”

Lesson Point: God is holy and must punish sin, so we should obey Him.

Game 1: Noah Says

How to Play:

  1. The teacher is Noah. Say actions such as “Noah says walk like an elephant.”

  2. Children do the action only when the teacher says “Noah says”.

  3. A child who moves without hearing “Noah says” sits down for one round.

Lesson Point: Listening carefully and obeying keeps us safe.

Game 2: Walk into the Ark

How to Play:

  1. Mark an area on the floor as the ark.

  2. Each child picks an animal card and walks in pairs through the door.

  3. Each pair says which animal they are, then sits inside the ark.

  4. The teacher (Noah) shuts the door at the end.

Lesson Point: Noah obeyed God and went in; God Himself shut the door.
