Stephen – Faithful unto Death

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 8: Acts of the Holy Spirit

Aim

To teach the children that sometimes God asks his followers to die in his service.

Scripture Reading

Acts 6:1-15; 7:51-60; 8:1

Memory Verse

Philippians 1:29

[I]t has been granted to you on behalf of Christ not only to believe on him, but also to suffer for him …

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

In recent years people in different parts of Africa have survived only because of famine relief food parcels. Many people feel they never get their fair share because most people lie about the size of their families. They say it is larger than it really is, because they are afraid that they will not get enough food.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Some members of the church in Jerusalem were short of food, too. As more people kept joining the congregation, the different groups started to quarrel about the distribution of food (6:1).

2. Therefore, the 12 apostles appointed seven special people to make sure that food was shared out fairly. They had to be wise and full of the Holy Spirit (6:2-5).

3. One of them was a man called Stephen. He was a very active servant of Jesus. Even opposition did not stop him, and no-one could win an argument with him. Describe what Stephen did (6:8-11).

4. Because Stephen’s enemies could not stop him, they plotted against him and spread lies about him. They brought him to trial before the Jewish religious leaders, but Stephen remained faithful to Jesus (6:12-15).

5. Stephen told the Jews that they were deaf to God’s message. They had not listened to any of God’s messengers, the prophets. Finally, they had murdered Jesus Christ himself, who had come to bring everlasting life (7:51-53).

6. The religious leaders were so angry that they dragged Stephen outside the city and stoned him to death. Show Picture 7. Point out the man Saul watching, and tell the class that they will hear much more of him later. Through it all, Stephen was very courageous. He died a terrible death, but the Lord Jesus was with him all the time and was waiting for him at the end of his ordeal (7:54 – 8:1).

Our Response to the Story

In October 1963 the army accused the pastor and church members in Melut, Sudan, of helping the Anya-nya guerrilla movement. Pastor Gideon and four of his elders were taken away and shot. There was no trial. Their bodies were thrown in the River Nile. Many other Christians in the Sudan died in the last few years. In various parts of the world there are Christians who, like Stephen, have died because of their faith in Jesus Christ.

Come, let us pray for those who are being persecuted because they are Christians.

Lesson - 7: STEPHEN – FAITHFUL UNTO DEATH

Scripture Reading: Acts 6:1-15; 7:51-60; 8:1

1. Who Am I?

Choose from: Stephen, Saul, apostles

  1. I was full of the Holy Spirit and served others. I am ____________. __________ 👉 Stephen

  2. We chose seven helpers to share food fairly. We are the ____________. __________ 👉 apostles

  3. I watched and heard all this, but later I followed Jesus. I am ____________. __________ 👉 Saul

2. True or False

Write T for True or F for False:

  1. The seven helpers made sure food was shared fairly. ( ___ ) 👉 T

  2. Stephen was afraid and stopped telling people about Jesus. ( ___ ) 👉 F

  3. The Lord Jesus was with Stephen to the end. ( ___ ) 👉 T

3. Circle the Right Words

Circle the right word:

  1. Stephen was full of (anger / faith) and the Holy Spirit. Right word: 👉 faith

  2. Stephen’s enemies told (lies / songs) about him. Right word: 👉 lies

  3. Stephen was (faithful / frightened) to Jesus. Right word: 👉 faithful

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why were seven helpers chosen?

    Sample answer: So that food would be shared fairly in the church.

  2. How did Stephen show he was faithful?

    Sample answer: He kept telling the truth about Jesus even when it was hard.

  3. How can we stay faithful to Jesus?

    Sample answer: By praying, trusting Him and not giving up.

5. Memory Verse:

Philippians 1:29 — “For it has been granted to you on behalf of Christ not only to believe on him, but also to suffer for him.”

Hebrews 13:5 — “Never will I leave you; never will I forsake you.”

Craft 1: Faithful Servant Badge

Materials: Card circle, pin or tape, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Cut a card circle.

  2. Write “Faithful” in the middle.

  3. Decorate with a star and tape on a pin.

Lesson Point: Stephen served God faithfully.

Craft 2: Helping Hands

Materials: Paper, crayons, scissors

Instructions:

  1. Trace both your hands on paper and cut them out.

  2. Write on them ways you can help at home or church.

  3. Glue them on a card.

Lesson Point: Stephen helped others in the church.

Game 1: Fair Shares

How to Play:

  1. Give out paper “food” pieces to groups of children.

  2. Ask each group to share them out fairly.

  3. Talk about why fair sharing is important.

Lesson Point: The helpers shared fairly.

Game 2: Stand for Jesus

How to Play:

  1. Read statements about Jesus aloud.

  2. If it is true, children stand tall; if not, they stay sitting.

  3. Say together: “I will be faithful to Jesus.”

Lesson Point: We stay faithful to Jesus.
