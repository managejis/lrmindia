The First Congregation

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 8: Acts of the Holy Spirit

Aim

To teach the children how the first Christian community lived in fellowship.

Scripture Reading

Acts 2:42-47

Memory Verse

Acts 2:42, 47

They devoted themselves to the apostles’ teaching and to the fellowship … they gave to anyone as he had need.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

What do people do when they meet as a congregation for a church service? Possible answers: They sing songs of praise to God.

They read/listen to God’s Word.

They take Holy Communion.

They attend Sunday school, women’s league meetings, etc.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Show Picture 3. Review Lesson 3 by asking questions like the following: Question: Who was preaching?

Answer: Peter.

Question: What happened when he shared the message about Jesus?

Answer: 3 000 people were saved. They did what the memory verse said.

Review the memory verse for Lesson 3, Acts 2:38.

2. Show Picture 4. This picture shows us some of the things Peter and the other apostles did within the new Christian community. What happened in this first congregation? They lived as one big family, God’s family (2:44). This was the beginning of the church. Discuss the pictures one by one.

3. The congregation of the first church learnt about the Saviour from the apostles. They looked at the Old Testament to see what it said about Jesus. (The New Testament had not been written yet.) They met in the temple every day to sing and pray (2:42, 46-47a).

4. Those first Christians helped one another. The healthy looked after the sick and old. Those who had a lot gave to those who were needy. They shared everything (2:44-45).

5. They took their meals together in their homes. They ate bread and wine in communion to remember the Lord’s suffering and death that brought them salvation (2:46).

6. Their life in loving community made other people want to join them. Also, people were amazed by the miracles performed by the apostles. The result was that more and more people came to believe in Jesus Christ (2:43, 47).

Our Response to the Story

Have we stopped practicing any of the activities of the first Christian congregation? Talk about the meaning of the memory verse and how we can follow the example of the early church.

Lesson - 4: THE FIRST CONGREGATION

Scripture Reading: Acts 2:42-47

1. Complete the Sentences

Word box: shared, prayed, family

  1. The first Christians were like one big ____________. __________ 👉 family

  2. They ____________ everything they had. __________ 👉 shared

  3. They met every day to sing and ____________. __________ 👉 prayed

2. Odd One Out

Circle the one that does not belong:

  1. Praying, singing, quarrelling, sharing 👉 quarrelling

  2. Helping the sick, giving to the poor, keeping everything, eating together 👉 keeping everything

  3. Learning from the apostles, listening to the Bible, ignoring God, praising God 👉 ignoring God

3. Put the Story in Order

a) They shared with those in need.
b) Peter preached and 3000 believed.
c) More people joined the church.
d) The believers learnt from the apostles.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. Peter preached and 3000 believed.

  2. The believers learnt from the apostles.

  3. They shared with those in need.

  4. More people joined the church.

4. Discussion Questions

  1. What did the first Christians do together?

    Sample answer: They prayed, sang, learnt and ate together.

  2. How did they help one another?

    Sample answer: They shared what they had with people in need.

  3. How can our church be like them?

    Sample answer: We can love, share, pray and help each other.

5. Memory Verse:

Acts 2:42 — “They devoted themselves to the apostles’ teaching and to the fellowship, to the breaking of bread and to prayer.”

Psalm 133:1 — “How good and pleasant it is when God’s people live together in unity!”

Craft 1: Church Family Chain

Materials: Paper, crayons, scissors, glue

Instructions:

  1. Fold paper and cut a chain of people holding hands.

  2. Colour each person differently.

  3. Write: “We are God’s family.”

Lesson Point: The first church lived together like a family.

Craft 2: Sharing Basket

Materials: Paper plate, paper fruit and bread, glue

Instructions:

  1. Colour a paper plate as a basket.

  2. Draw bread and fruit and glue them on.

  3. Write: “We share.”

Lesson Point: The first Christians shared with those in need.

Game 1: Share the Food

How to Play:

  1. Give some children “paper food” and others none.

  2. Ask the children who have more to share.

  3. Talk about how it felt to share.

Lesson Point: Sharing shows God’s love.

Game 2: Circle of Prayer

How to Play:

  1. Sit in a circle holding hands.

  2. Each child says a short thank-you prayer.

  3. Sing a praise song together.

Lesson Point: Christians pray and sing together.
