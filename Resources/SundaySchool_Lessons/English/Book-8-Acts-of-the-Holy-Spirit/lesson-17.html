God Guides Paul

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 8: Acts of the Holy Spirit

Aim

To teach the children that God guided Paul. He told him where to preach the Gospel.

Scripture Reading

Acts 16:6-10

Memory Verse

Psalm 48:14

[T]his God is our God for ever and ever; he will be our guide even to the end.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

When you come to a place you do not know, you need a guide to show you the way. Describe a time when you or someone you know needed guidance.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Paul made many journeys to different towns and countries to spread the good news of Jesus Christ. On one journey he did not know where to go next. He had thought of several places, but in some way or another God stopped him from going there (Acts 16:6-8). What was Paul to do? He knew God would guide him.

2. Describe how Paul had a vision one night (16:9). He saw a man from Macedonia (the country we now call Greece). Paul knew from the man’s clothes that he was from that region. Show Picture 17. The Macedonian was asking Paul to come and help them. The Bible uses a very strong word here for “ask”; it says the man begged Paul.

3. Paul knew it was God telling him that he had to take the Gospel to Greece. That is exactly what he and his companions did next (16:10).

Our Response to the Story

God still guides us today. He may give us guidance in dreams or through our church and Christian friends. God most often speaks to us through his Word, the Bible. When we pray, God may use that time to put an idea into our minds. We should ask God to guide us, and then trust that he will, as the memory verse promises.

Lesson - 17: GOD GUIDES PAUL

Scripture Reading: Acts 16:6-10

1. Missing Letters

Fill in the missing letters:

  1. G _ _ D guided Paul. (4 letters) __________ 👉 GOD

  2. Paul saw a v _ s _ _ n at night. __________ 👉 vision

  3. The man was from M _ c _ d _ n _ a. __________ 👉 Macedonia

2. True or False

Write T for True or F for False:

  1. Paul knew where to go next without asking. ( ___ ) 👉 F

  2. The man in the vision asked for help. ( ___ ) 👉 T

  3. Paul went to Macedonia right away. ( ___ ) 👉 T

3. Put the Story in Order

a) Paul saw a man in a vision.
b) Paul and his friends set off.
c) God stopped Paul from going to some places.
d) The man said, “Come and help us.”

👉 Correct Order:

  1. God stopped Paul from going to some places.

  2. Paul saw a man in a vision.

  3. The man said, “Come and help us.”

  4. Paul and his friends set off.

4. Discussion Questions

  1. How did God guide Paul?

    Sample answer: Through a vision and by closing some doors.

  2. How does God guide us today?

    Sample answer: Through the Bible, prayer, church and good friends.

  3. What should we do when we need guidance?

    Sample answer: Ask God and trust Him.

5. Memory Verse:

Psalm 48:14 — “For this God is our God for ever and ever; he will be our guide even to the end.”

Psalm 32:8 — “I will instruct you and teach you in the way you should go.”

Materials: Paper, crayons

Instructions:

  2. Draw a ship between them.

  3. Write: “Come and help us.”

Game 1: Follow the Guide

How to Play:

  1. One child leads the group around the room.

  2. The group follows closely.

  3. Talk about how God leads us.

Lesson Point: We follow God’s guidance.

Game 2: Left or Right?

How to Play:

  1. The teacher says a direction and children move that way.

  2. Say: “God shows us the way.”

  3. Repeat with new directions.

Lesson Point: God shows us where to go.
