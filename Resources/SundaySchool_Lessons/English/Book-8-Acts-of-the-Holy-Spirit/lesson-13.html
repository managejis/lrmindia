Paul Becomes a New Person

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 8: Acts of the Holy Spirit

Aim

To teach the children how Paul came to meet the Lord Jesus and believe in him.

Scripture Reading

Acts 9:1-9; 26:9-18

Memory Verse

1 Timothy 1:15

Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners …

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Suppose you are walking along a road at night-time, and it is very dark. Suddenly a car comes along with its lights on bright. What do you do? Yes, you cover your eyes because the light is so bright that it hurts them and you cannot see.

Main Points of the Lesson

Before telling the story, explain to the class that Saul is the Hebrew version of Paul’s name. After he became an apostle of Jesus he wasn’t called Saul anymore, because he was working among foreigners, not among Hebrew speakers. In order not to confuse the children, use only one version of his name: Paul.

1. A man named Paul went on a journey to the town Damascus. He was a clever man, one who knew the law. He was one of those Jews who hated the followers of Jesus Christ. He was carrying a special letter from the high priest in Jerusalem, giving him permission to put all the Christians in Damascus in prison (Acts 9:1-2).

2. Paul and his companions had nearly reached Damascus when, suddenly, a very bright light from heaven shone on him (9:3).

3. Show Picture 13. As Paul and his companions shielded themselves, he heard a voice speaking to him. It was Jesus himself. As a very religious Jew, Paul had refused to believe that Jesus was alive, but now Jesus was speaking to him!

Describe the conversation between Jesus and Paul (12:4-6).

4. The people who were with Paul could not understand what was going on. When the voice stopped talking, they had to lead him into Damascus. The bright light had blinded him (12:7-8).

5. For three days Paul waited in Damascus for Jesus to tell him what to do. He did not eat or drink a thing, but he was ready to listen to the Lord (12:9).

6. Paul had been a cruel man. He had thought he was defending God’s honour by persecuting the Christians. That was why he did not prevent Stephen from being stoned (7:58; 8:1). Remind the class of that story by showing Picture 7 again. Point out Paul guarding the clothes. Paul had thought he was following God, but he was spiritually blind and on the wrong path. Meeting Jesus changed Paul into a new person. However, he would never forget that he was a sinner or stop praising the Lord for saving him. He wrote the words of today’s memory verse.

Our Response to the Story

No-one is so wicked that Jesus can’t change them if they come to him as repentant sinners. The best way to end this lesson would be to say how you, the teacher, met the Lord Jesus.

Note: This lesson opens up an opportunity to invite the children to accept Jesus as their personal Saviour. Most children like to please the teacher. If you say, “Put up your hand if you want to accept Jesus as your Saviour,” many will do it even though they may not really mean it, or are not ready to do so. It is better for you to say that anyone who wants to talk more about accepting Jesus as their Saviour should come and talk to you after the class, or at any other time that you can arrange.

Lesson - 13: PAUL BECOMES A NEW PERSON

Scripture Reading: Acts 9:1-9; 26:9-18

1. Put the Story in Order

a) Paul was led into the city, unable to see.
b) Paul travelled to Damascus.
c) Paul heard Jesus’ voice.
d) A bright light shone from heaven.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. Paul travelled to Damascus.

  2. A bright light shone from heaven.

  3. Paul heard Jesus’ voice.

  4. Paul was led into the city, unable to see.

2. Circle the Right Words

Circle the right word:

  1. Paul used to (help / hurt) Christians. Right word: 👉 hurt

  2. Paul was (blind / deaf) for three days. Right word: 👉 blind

  3. Jesus changed Paul into a (new / sad) person. Right word: 👉 new

3. Choose the Right Answer

Circle a), b) or c):

  1. Where was Paul going?
    a) Damascus b) Rome c) Egypt 👉 a) Damascus

  2. Who spoke to Paul from the light?
    a) Peter b) Jesus c) An angel 👉 b) Jesus

4. Discussion Questions

  1. How did Jesus change Paul?

    Sample answer: Paul stopped hurting Christians and began to follow Jesus.

  2. Who can Jesus change?

    Sample answer: Anyone who comes to Him and is sorry.

  3. How has Jesus helped you?

    Sample answer: Answers will vary.

5. Memory Verse:

1 Timothy 1:15 — “Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners—of whom I am the worst.”

2 Corinthians 5:17 — “If anyone is in Christ, the new creation has come: the old has gone, the new is here!”

Craft 1: Bright Light Picture

Materials: Yellow and black paper, crayons, glue

Instructions:

  1. Cover a page with black paper.

  2. Tear yellow paper into bright rays.

  3. Glue the rays coming from a corner.

Lesson Point: Jesus’ light met Paul on the road.

Craft 2: Before and After Card

Materials: Card, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Fold a card into two.

  2. On the left draw Paul with a sad face.

  3. On the right draw Paul with a happy face.

Lesson Point: Jesus makes people new.

Game 1: Bright Light, Stop!

How to Play:

  1. Children walk. When the teacher says “Bright light!”, they stop and cover their eyes.

  2. Say what Paul heard.

  3. Then everyone says “Yes, Lord!”

Lesson Point: Paul stopped and listened to Jesus.

Game 2: Old and New

How to Play:

  1. Say pairs: old/new, sad/glad.

  2. Children show with actions.

  3. Say: “Jesus makes me new.”

Lesson Point: Jesus can change anyone.
