The Lord Jesus Returns to the Father

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 8: Acts of the Holy Spirit

Aim

To teach the children that Jesus returned to the Father, but will come back to this world one day.

Scripture Reading

Acts 1:1-11; 1 Thessalonians 4:13-18

Memory Verse

1 Thessalonians 4:16

[T]he Lord himself will come down from heaven, with a loud command … and with the trumpet call of God …

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

How do you feel when someone you love goes away for a long time? We often feel sad when we have to say goodbye, because we are going to miss the person who leaves us behind.

What questions do you ask the one who is leaving? A typical question is: “When are you coming back?”

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Jesus’ disciples were just like this. After Jesus had completed the task he had to do on earth – after he had been crucified and God had raised him from the dead – Jesus returned to the father. Show Picture 1. This picture shows the Lord Jesus being taken up into heaven. See how sad his friends are.

2. After his resurrection Jesus appeared to his disciples many times. He taught them, preparing them to continue God’s work in this world. Jesus promised that they would receive his Holy Spirit and that the Spirit would strengthen them with God’s power. They had to wait for this gift that would give them the power and wisdom to talk about Jesus, and what he had done for mankind (Acts 1:2-5, 8).

To help the children understand the working of the Holy Spirit, show them an inner tube of a tyre that is not inflated. Ask them if it is any good while there is no air in it. They will know that it is useless like that. If possible, bring along a pump and pump up the tube so that the class can see how it becomes useful once it is filled with air. Note: The New Testament was originally written in the Greek language. In Greek the words “spirit” and “air” are the same. Followers of Jesus need to be filled with the Holy Spirit, just like tyres have to be filled with air in order to be useful.

3. Jesus’ friends asked him what was going to happen in the future. When would he return? Jesus made a promise: He would surely return to this world. However, only the Father knows when this will be. We know that his second coming will be announced with a great shout and that we shall go to meet him (Acts 1:6-7, 1 Th 4:13-18).

4. When Jesus had disappeared from their sight, two messengers from God brought the disciples a message. Point to the messengers in the picture, then say the message in your own words (Acts 1:9-11).

The messengers called the disciples “men of Galilee,” because that was where the disciples came from. It was like saying “men of Equatoria”. In the next lessons we shall see how the disciples obeyed Jesus’ last instructions. From now on they would be called Jesus’ apostles. All his other followers would become disciples.

Our Response to the Story

Think about this question: Are you ready for Jesus if he should come again today? As we do not know when he will return, the only way to be ready is to follow Jesus faithfully every day.

Lesson - 1: THE LORD JESUS RETURNS TO THE FATHER

Scripture Reading: Acts 1:1-11; 1 Thessalonians 4:13-18

1. Put the Story in Order

a) Jesus was taken up into heaven.
b) Jesus rose from the dead.
c) Two messengers spoke to the disciples.
d) Jesus taught His friends many times.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. Jesus rose from the dead.

  2. Jesus taught His friends many times.

  3. Jesus was taken up into heaven.

  4. Two messengers spoke to the disciples.

2. Complete the Sentences

Word box: Spirit, return, heaven

  1. Jesus went back to ____________. __________ 👉 heaven

  2. Jesus promised to send the Holy ____________. __________ 👉 Spirit

  3. Jesus will one day ____________ to this world. __________ 👉 return

3. Yes or No

Write Yes or No:

  1. Do we know the day Jesus will come back? ( ___ ) 👉 No

  2. Did Jesus tell His friends to wait for the Holy Spirit? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

  3. Should we follow Jesus every day to be ready? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

4. Discussion Questions

  1. How did the disciples feel when Jesus went up?

    Sample answer: They were sad, but they were also given a promise.

  2. What did the messengers say?

    Sample answer: Jesus will come back in the same way.

  3. How can we be ready for Jesus?

    Sample answer: By loving and obeying Him every day.

5. Memory Verse:

1 Thessalonians 4:16 — “For the Lord himself will come down from heaven, with a loud command, with the voice of the archangel and with the trumpet call of God.”

John 14:3 — “I will come back and take you to be with me.”

Craft 1: Balloon Spirit

Materials: Balloon, marker, card, string

Instructions:

  1. Blow up a balloon and tie it.

  2. Draw a cross on it with a marker.

  3. Tie it to a card that says: “Filled with the Spirit”.

Lesson Point: The Holy Spirit fills and helps us like air fills a balloon.

Craft 2: Jesus Goes Up

Materials: Blue paper, cotton wool, glue, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Glue cotton wool clouds on blue paper.

  2. Draw Jesus above the clouds with open arms.

  3. Draw the disciples looking up at the bottom.

Lesson Point: Jesus went to heaven and will come back.

Game 1: Looking Up

How to Play:

  1. Everyone stands and looks up when the teacher says “Jesus”.

  2. When the teacher says “wait”, children sit still and quiet.

  3. Say: “Jesus is coming back!” together.

Lesson Point: We wait and watch for Jesus.

Game 2: Balloon Power

How to Play:

  1. Show a flat balloon and a blown-up balloon.

  2. Ask which one can fly and bounce.

  3. Talk about how the Holy Spirit gives us power.

Lesson Point: The Holy Spirit gives us power for God’s work.
