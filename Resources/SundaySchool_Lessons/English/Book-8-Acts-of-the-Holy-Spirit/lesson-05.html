Divine Power to Heal

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 8: Acts of the Holy Spirit

Aim

To teach the children that the Lord Jesus worked through Peter to heal a cripple.

Scripture Reading

Acts 3:1-16

Memory Verse

Acts 3:6

“Silver or gold I do not have, but what I have I give you. In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, walk.”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Either prepare a child before the lesson to act as a beggar or pretend to be one yourself. What is this person doing? Why do you think he is begging? Possible answers: he may be crippled or too ill to work.

Main Points of the Lesson

This is a very dramatic story. Read the Bible passage several times so you know it well and then tell the story in your own words.

1. Peter and John, who were two of Jesus’ disciples while he lived on earth, were leaders of the first Christian congregation. They went to the temple every day at three o’clock (3:1) to pray.

2. At the gate they met a beggar. Describe the man and what was wrong with him (3:2-3).

3. Peter and John stopped and looked at him (3:4-5).

4. Peter spoke to the beggar (3:6).

5. The man stood up. He was healed (3:7)! Show Picture 5. He jumped about and went into the temple praising God. Everyone was surprised (3:8-10).

6. Peter told the people it was through faith in the power of the living Lord Jesus that the beggar was healed (3:15-16).

Activity

This is a very suitable story to act. Divide the whole class into groups of three. One person in the group should be the beggar, another John and the third Peter, who has to say the memory verse. Then let them change roles so that, in the end, every child will have recited today’s memory verse.

Lesson - 5: DIVINE POWER TO HEAL

Scripture Reading: Acts 3:1-16

1. Choose the Right Answer

Circle a), b) or c):

  1. Where were Peter and John going?
    a) The market b) The temple c) The river 👉 b) The temple

  2. What did the man at the gate ask for?
    a) Money b) Food c) Clothes 👉 a) Money

  3. What did the man do after he was healed?
    a) Went away quietly b) Hid c) Walked, jumped and praised God 👉 c) Walked, jumped and praised God

2. Count and Write

Write the answer:

  1. Peter and John went to pray at ____ o’clock. __________ 👉 3

  2. How many apostles went to the temple together? __________ 👉 2

  3. Peter and John met the beggar at the gate called ____________. __________ 👉 Beautiful

3. Who Healed? Yes or No

Write Yes or No:

  1. Did Peter heal the man by his own power? ( ___ ) 👉 No

  2. Did Jesus’ power heal the man? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

  3. Were the people surprised? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

4. Discussion Questions

  1. What did Peter give the man?

    Sample answer: He gave him healing in Jesus’ name.

  2. Who really healed the man?

    Sample answer: Jesus did, through Peter.

  3. What is better than silver or gold?

    Sample answer: Knowing and trusting Jesus.

5. Memory Verse:

Acts 3:6 — “Silver or gold I do not have, but what I have I give you. In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, walk.”

Luke 18:27 — “What is impossible with men is possible with God.”

Craft 1: Walking Man Puppet

Materials: Card, crayons, split pins or paper strips, scissors

Instructions:

  1. Draw a man and cut it out.

  2. Add paper-strip legs.

  3. Move the legs to show him walking and jumping.

Lesson Point: Jesus healed the lame man through Peter.

Craft 2: Temple Gate Picture

Materials: Paper, crayons, glue

Instructions:

  1. Draw a big gate.

  2. Draw the beggar sitting beside it.

  3. Draw him jumping and add the words: “Praise God!”

Lesson Point: The man went into the temple praising God.

Game 1: Act the Story

How to Play:

  1. Make groups of three: beggar, Peter and John.

  2. Act out the story and say the memory verse.

  3. Change roles so that everyone has a turn.

Lesson Point: We give what we have in Jesus’ name.

Game 2: Jump for Joy

How to Play:

  1. Sit down as the lame man.

  2. When the teacher says “In Jesus’ name, walk!”, jump up and cheer.

  3. Say “Praise God!” together.

Lesson Point: We praise God when He helps us.
