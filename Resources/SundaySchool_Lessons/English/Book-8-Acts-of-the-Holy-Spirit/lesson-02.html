The Coming of the Holy Spirit

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 8: Acts of the Holy Spirit

Aim

To teach the children that Jesus sent his Holy Spirit upon his followers so that they might be witnesses for the Lord.

Scripture Reading

Acts 2:1-12

Memory Verse

John 14:26

[T]he Counsellor, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name, will teach you all things …

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Bring along a torch from which you have removed the batteries. Do not let the class know this. What do we use this for? Give the children time to respond. We use it to give light, of course, so that we may see in the dark. Switch on the torch. It will not work. It has no power! It has no power because there are no batteries inside, see?

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Show Picture 1. Briefly review what is happening here. Who is leaving his friends behind? Where is Jesus going? Who did he promise to send to them? Jesus promised he would send the Holy Spirit. Read Acts 1:8 out loud.

2. Ten days after Jesus had left his friends to return to the Father, all the apostles were gathered in one place. Tell the story of 2:1-3 in your own words: There was a noise like a strong wind, and what looked like tongues of fire came to rest on each of them. Show Picture 2. They were filled with God’s power, the power of the Holy Spirit.

3. Put the batteries in the torch in front of the class. Switch it on. Now that it has power, it works! In the same way, Jesus’ friends had his power inside them from that day onward. Describe what this power enabled them to do (2:5-12). There were people from many different countries gathered in Jerusalem for the Jewish feast of Pentecost. The Holy Spirit gave the apostles the ability to speak to all those people in their own language about Jesus.

Our Response to the Story

Jesus gives his Holy Spirit to all who believe in him and love him. In this way, Jesus helps us to be his witnesses; that is, to tell others about him.

There are many ways to do this, even if you are a child. You can tell your neighbours and friends the stories you hear in Sunday school. You can sing the songs you learn here wherever you find yourself. You can invite someone along to Sunday school or to church.

Instil the Memory Verse

Conclude the session with a song about witnessing.

Lesson - 2: THE COMING OF THE HOLY SPIRIT

Scripture Reading: Acts 2:1-12

1. Who Am I?

Choose from: Holy Spirit, Peter, Jesus

  1. I came like a strong wind. I am the ____________. __________ 👉 Holy Spirit

  2. I promised to send the Counsellor. I am ____________. __________ 👉 Jesus

  3. I was one of the apostles who waited in Jerusalem. I am ____________. __________ 👉 Peter

2. Choose the Right Answer

Circle a), b) or c):

  1. What came to rest on each person?
    a) Water b) Tongues of fire c) Stones 👉 b) Tongues of fire

  2. What did the people hear in their own language?
    a) Songs b) Stories about Jesus c) News 👉 b) Stories about Jesus

  3. How many days after Jesus went up did this happen? (about)
    a) 10 b) 100 c) 1 👉 a) 10

3. True or False

Write T for True or F for False:

  1. The apostles were all in one place. ( ___ ) 👉 T

  2. The people came from only one country. ( ___ ) 👉 F

  3. The Holy Spirit gave the apostles power to tell about Jesus. ( ___ ) 👉 T

4. Discussion Questions

  1. What did the torch need to work?

    Sample answer: Batteries, which are like the power of the Holy Spirit.

  2. Why could everyone understand the apostles?

    Sample answer: The Holy Spirit helped them speak in many languages.

  3. How can a child tell others about Jesus?

    Sample answer: Tell Bible stories, sing songs or invite a friend to church.

5. Memory Verse:

John 14:26 — “But the Counsellor, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name, will teach you all things and will remind you of everything I have said to you.”

Acts 1:8 — “You will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes on you.”

Craft 1: Flame Picture

Materials: Red, orange and yellow paper, glue, card

Instructions:

  1. Tear red, orange and yellow paper into flame shapes.

  2. Glue the flames on a card.

  3. Write: “God’s Spirit gives power.”

Lesson Point: The Holy Spirit came on Jesus’ friends like tongues of fire.

Craft 2: Wind Spinner

Materials: Paper square, scissors (teacher helps), pin or stick, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Colour a paper square.

  2. The teacher cuts four slits from the corners to the middle.

  3. Fold alternate points to the centre and fix on a stick.

Lesson Point: The Spirit came like a mighty wind.

Game 1: Torch Power

How to Play:

  1. Show a torch without batteries and try to turn it on.

  2. Add the batteries and switch it on.

  3. Talk about the Spirit giving us power.

Lesson Point: We need the Holy Spirit’s power.

Game 2: Hello in Many Languages

How to Play:

  1. Teach the class “hello” in two or three languages.

  2. Children greet each other in those languages.

  3. Say: “Jesus loves you” in your own language.

Lesson Point: The good news is for all people.
