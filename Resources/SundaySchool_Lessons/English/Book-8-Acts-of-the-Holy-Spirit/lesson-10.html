Peter Visits Cornelius’s House

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 8: Acts of the Holy Spirit

Aim

To teach the children that all people are of equal importance to God.

Scripture Reading

Acts 10:1-48

Memory Verse

Galatians 3:28

[Y]ou are all one in Christ Jesus.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Imagine an important soldier comes to visit your house – a lieutenant or even a general. How will you entertain him? Give the class the opportunity to make suggestions.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Today’s story is about a retired Roman army officer called Cornelius. In those days, the Romans ruled over Palestine, the country where the Jews lived. Jesus and his disciples were Jews. The Jews call everybody who is not a Jew a “Gentile”. Describe Cornelius as a person. Cornelius did not worship the false Roman idols, but followed the God of Israel, even though he was a Gentile (Acts 10:1-2).

2. Describe Cornelius’s vision. God had heard his prayer. An angel told him that a man called Simon Peter would come to help him. He was to send messengers to Joppa to find him (10:3-6).

3. Cornelius sent his servants to find Peter. He even sent one of his personal attendants along (10:7-8).

4. They were already on their way when Peter had his vision up on the roof. Show Picture 9 and review last week’s memory verse and God’s message to Peter – that the Gospel is for all people. Peter was going to be tested. Would he remember the lesson God had taught him? Peter received the messengers. He listened to what they said, and the next day they all went to Cornelius’s house (10:19-23).

5. Describe how Cornelius welcomed Peter (10:25). Show Picture 10. This is not the usual way a soldier greets anyone! Peter stopped Cornelius from worshipping him, as he was only a human being, like Cornelius himself (10:26).

6. Peter went into Cornelius’s house. A Jew would never have entered a Gentile’s house, but Peter was remembering the lesson from his vision. Many people were waiting here to hear what Peter had to say to them (10:24, 27-28).

7. Cornelius told Peter why he had sent for him (10:29-33).

8. Peter told Cornelius who Jesus of Nazareth was and what he had come to do (10:36-43). God showed that the Gentiles are part of his church, too, by sending the Holy Spirit upon all who were gathered there (10:44-48).

Our Response to the Story

Follow up on the thoughts mentioned under this section for last week. Have we reached out to anyone beyond our church or small community with the good news about Jesus?

Lesson - 10: PETER VISITS CORNELIUS’S HOUSE

Scripture Reading: Acts 10:1-48

1. Who Am I?

Choose from: Cornelius, Peter, angel

  1. I was a Roman army officer who prayed to God. I am ____________. __________ 👉 Cornelius

  2. I told Cornelius to send for Peter. I am the ____________. __________ 👉 angel

  3. I went to a Gentile’s house to tell about Jesus. I am ____________. __________ 👉 Peter

2. Circle the Right Word

Circle the correct word:

  1. Cornelius knelt before Peter, but Peter said, “Stand up, I am only a (man / king).” Right word: 👉 man

  2. The (Holy Spirit / soldier) came upon those who listened. Right word: 👉 Holy Spirit

  3. Peter went into the (Gentile’s / temple) house. Right word: 👉 Gentile’s

3. Put the Story in Order

a) Cornelius sent men to find Peter.
b) The Holy Spirit came on everyone who listened.
c) Cornelius prayed and saw an angel.
d) Peter went to Cornelius’s house.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. Cornelius prayed and saw an angel.

  2. Cornelius sent men to find Peter.

  3. Peter went to Cornelius’s house.

  4. The Holy Spirit came on everyone who listened.

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why was it special that Peter went to the house?

    Sample answer: Jews did not usually enter a Gentile’s home.

  2. What did God show by sending His Spirit?

    Sample answer: That He loves all people the same.

  3. How can you welcome someone who is different?

    Sample answer: Greet them, play with them and share Jesus’ love.

5. Memory Verse:

Galatians 3:28 — “You are all one in Christ Jesus.”

John 3:16 — “For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son.”

Craft 1: Welcome Door

Materials: Card, crayons, scissors

Instructions:

  1. Fold a card to make a door that opens.

  2. Draw Peter and Cornelius inside.

  3. Write: “All are welcome.”

Lesson Point: All people are important to God.

Craft 2: One in Christ Chain

Materials: Paper strips in different colours, glue

Instructions:

  1. Write a name on each paper strip.

  2. Glue the strips into links.

  3. Hang the chain in the room.

Lesson Point: In Christ we are all one family.

Game 1: Honoured Guest

How to Play:

  1. One child is the visitor.

  2. The others welcome the guest politely.

  3. Swap until all children have been guests.

Lesson Point: We welcome everyone kindly.

Game 2: Same in God’s Eyes

How to Play:

  1. Show different pictures of children.

  2. After each one say: “Loved by God!”

  3. Children repeat each time.

Lesson Point: God loves all children equally.
