Peter Begins to Preach

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 8: Acts of the Holy Spirit

Aim

To show the children how the power of the Holy Spirit worked in Peter.

Scripture Reading

Mark 14:66-72; Acts 2:14-41

Memory Verse

Acts 2:38

“Repent and be baptised … in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of your sins. And you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Have you ever seen a car with a loudspeaker on the roof that goes around big towns like Juba to make important announcements? People come out of their houses to listen. Children follow the car and a crowd collects. Give other examples of how crowds gather in your town. People have always been like that; they want to hear the news, to find out what everybody is talking about.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Can you remember whom Jesus sent to help the apostles carry on God’s work? That is right: the Holy Spirit. His power would help the apostles tell everybody in Jerusalem about Jesus. Say the memory verse of Lesson 2, John 14:26, together.

2. The apostles did as Jesus asked. They went out into the streets to talk about him. There were many visitors in Jerusalem at that time of year, and they came from many different countries. Show Picture 3, pointing out the people from different countries. Even though they spoke different languages, they all could hear the apostles speaking in their own language. They could not explain it. Some even thought the apostles were drunk (Acts 2:12-13). Then Peter stood up and spoke to them (2:14). Show Peter in the picture.

3. Put down the picture and remind the children of how frightened Peter was at Jesus’ trial. He even said he did not know Jesus – three times! Tell the story of Mark 14:66-72 briefly. What brought about this difference in Peter? How could he now stand up and talk about Jesus in front of a big crowd? The answer is: He had received the gift of the Holy Spirit! Show Picture 3 again.

4. Peter explained that they were not drunk. God had given them his Holy Spirit so they could tell everyone about Jesus, the Saviour of all mankind (2:15-17a, 21). Peter told the crowd who Jesus was. One way in which Jesus showed he was the Son of God was by his miracles (2:22). Ask the class to give some examples. Jesus allowed mere human beings to put him to death so that he could save them from sin and death. After three days God resurrected Jesus (Acts 2:23-24).

5. The memory verse tells us how we should react to this message. This is what Peter told the people that day, and 3 000 people accepted Jesus as their Saviour (Acts 2:38, 41).

Our Response to the Story

Peter received God’s Holy Spirit and witnessed fearlessly about Jesus. All of us who believe in Jesus have received the Holy Spirit (2:17a). Therefore, we can all witness about Jesus. Try to confirm this with an example from your own life.

End with a prayer of thanks for the gift of the Holy Spirit.

Lesson - 3: PETER BEGINS TO PREACH

Scripture Reading: Mark 14:66-72; Acts 2:14-41

1. Peter or Crowd? P/C

Write P if Peter did it, C if the crowd did it:

  1. Stood up and spoke boldly about Jesus. ( ___ ) 👉 P

  2. Heard the apostles speaking in their own language. ( ___ ) 👉 C

  3. Once said three times, “I do not know Him.” ( ___ ) 👉 P

2. Circle the Right Word

Circle the correct word:

  1. Peter was (afraid / fearless) when he preached at Pentecost. Right word: 👉 fearless

  2. About (30 / 3000) people believed that day. Right word: 👉 3000

  3. Peter told them to (repent / run) and be baptised. Right word: 👉 repent

3. Match the Following

A B
Peter’s fear At Jesus’ trial
Peter’s boldness After the Holy Spirit came
The crowd Gathered to listen
The gift The Holy Spirit

Answers:
Peter’s fear → At Jesus’ trial
Peter’s boldness → After the Holy Spirit came
The crowd → Gathered to listen
The gift → The Holy Spirit

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why was Peter so brave?

    Sample answer: The Holy Spirit gave him courage and power.

  2. What did Peter tell the people about Jesus?

    Sample answer: Jesus is the Saviour who died and rose again.

  3. How can you be brave for Jesus?

    Sample answer: Ask the Holy Spirit to help you speak about Him.

5. Memory Verse:

Acts 2:38 — “Repent and be baptised, every one of you, in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of your sins. And you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.”

2 Timothy 1:7 — “God gave us a spirit not of fear but of power, love and self-discipline.”

Craft 1: Speech Bubble Peter

Materials: Paper, crayons, scissors, glue

Instructions:

  1. Draw Peter standing tall.

  2. Cut out a big speech bubble.

  3. Write in it: “Jesus is Lord!” and glue it near him.

Lesson Point: The Holy Spirit made Peter brave.

Craft 2: Crowd Paper Chain

Materials: Paper strips, glue, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Draw a face on each paper strip.

  2. Glue the strips into a chain of people.

  3. Write on the first one: 3000 believed.

Lesson Point: Many people believed when Peter shared Jesus.

Game 1: Gather the Crowd

How to Play:

  1. Children walk around the room.

  2. When the teacher says “Listen!”, all gather around one child.

  3. That child says the memory verse and the crowd claps.

Lesson Point: People gather to hear the good news.

Game 2: Brave Voice

How to Play:

  1. Children whisper “Jesus is Lord”.

  2. Then say it a little louder, then loud and clear.

  3. Talk about being brave to share about Jesus.

Lesson Point: The Spirit helps us be bold.
