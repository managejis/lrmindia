Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 8: Acts of the Holy Spirit

Aim

To teach the children how Paul and Barnabas witnessed about Jesus Christ.

Scripture Reading

Acts 13:14-52

Memory Verse

Acts 13:38

… I want you to know that through Jesus the forgiveness of sins is proclaimed to you.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Sometimes when people talk to us we are so eager to hear what they say, that we listen very carefully. At other times we are not interested, so we shut our ears to their words; we hear them, but we do not really listen.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Show Picture 16. Paul and Barnabas were sent by their local church to travel all over and tell people about Jesus. Whenever they arrived in a new town, they would first of all go to the synagogue (13:14). A synagogue is the place of worship and prayer for people of the Jewish faith, remember?

2. One of the cities they reached was also called Antioch, but it was in another part of the world from the Antioch from which they had set out on their missionary journey. As a visitor, Paul was invited to speak to the people who had come to the weekly meeting at the synagogue. There were Jews as well as Gentiles who had accepted the Jewish faith (13:15-16).

3. Paul taught the people like this: The God of Israel chose our fathers (Abraham, Isaac and Jacob) and made them into a great people. The prophets and teachers among them spoke for many years about someone who would come and save them. The wait is over, the Saviour has come. He is the Lord Jesus Christ. But the Jews did not recognise who Jesus is (13:17-27).

4. Paul then told the good news about Jesus. Jesus had done no wrong, but he was killed. However, God raised him from the dead. He is alive and will never die again. All who put their trust in Jesus Christ are declared innocent before God, but those who laugh at this truth will bear the punishment of their sins (13:28, 30, 37-41).

5. Look at Picture 16 again. Some people accepted Paul’s message. Others were angry. Point out the different attitudes in the picture.

6. Paul and Barnabas were invited back to speak again the next week. Some people went home with them, as they could not wait to hear more (13:42-43).

7. The next week the synagogue was full. Many Jews were angry that people from so many different races had come. They wanted to keep on thinking that God loved only the people of Israel. They were also jealous of Paul and Barnabas because almost everyone in the city had come to hear them speak. They did not want to share or hear the Gospel (13:44-46).

8. Those who accepted the Christian faith formed a church in Antioch, too (13:48-49). Unfortunately, Paul and Barnabas were forced to leave because of the Jews’ anger. They took the Gospel to more places (13:50-52).

Our Response to the Story

God asks us to take the Gospel to people who have not heard the message. Not everybody will listen and believe, but everybody must be given the opportunity to hear the good news of Jesus Christ.

Let us continue to pray for the unreached tribes or people we spoke about at the end of lesson 15.

Lesson - 16: THE MISSIONARY MESSAGE

Scripture Reading: Acts 13:14-52

1. Choose the Right Answer

Circle a), b) or c):

  1. Where did Paul and Barnabas go first in each town?
    a) The market b) The synagogue c) The river 👉 b) The synagogue

  2. What is the good news Paul told?
    a) Jesus is alive b) The king is coming c) The ship is ready 👉 a) Jesus is alive

2. Who Am I?

Choose from: Paul, Barnabas, Gentiles

  1. I spoke to the people in the synagogue. I am ____________. __________ 👉 Paul

  2. I travelled with Paul. I am ____________. __________ 👉 Barnabas

  3. We were not Jews but wanted to hear God’s word. We are the ____________. __________ 👉 Gentiles

3. Listen or Not? L/N

Write L if they listened, N if they did not:

  1. Some people believed and were glad. ( ___ ) 👉 L

  2. Some people were jealous and angry. ( ___ ) 👉 N

  3. The new believers formed a church in Antioch. ( ___ ) 👉 L

4. Discussion Questions

  1. What is the missionary message?

    Sample answer: Jesus died, rose again and forgives our sins.

  2. Why did some people not listen?

    Sample answer: They were jealous or did not want to share God’s love.

  3. How can we listen well?

    Sample answer: By being quiet, thinking and asking questions.

5. Memory Verse:

Acts 13:38 — “Therefore, my brothers, I want you to know that through Jesus the forgiveness of sins is proclaimed to you.”

Psalm 96:3 — “Declare his glory among the nations, his marvellous deeds among all peoples.”

Craft 1: Listening Ears

Materials: Paper, crayons, scissors, string

Instructions:

  1. Draw and cut out two big ears.

  2. Colour them and write “Listen”.

  3. Tie or glue them to a headband.

Lesson Point: We should listen to God’s message.

Craft 2: Good News Scroll

Materials: Paper, stick, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Write “Jesus forgives” on a strip of paper.

  2. Roll it onto a stick.

  3. Tie with a ribbon.

Lesson Point: Forgiveness is found in Jesus.

Game 1: Listen Carefully

How to Play:

  1. The teacher whispers a short Bible sentence.

  2. Children repeat it quietly.

  3. Talk about why careful listening matters.

Lesson Point: We should listen carefully to the Gospel.

Game 2: Tell the News

How to Play:

  1. Children stand in two lines.

  2. One line asks “What is the good news?”

  3. The other line answers: “Jesus forgives us!”

Lesson Point: Jesus gives forgiveness to all who believe.
