Giving to God

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 8: Acts of the Holy Spirit

Aim

To teach the children that we must be honest and sincere when we give to God.

Scripture Reading

Acts 5:1-11

Memory Verse

2 Corinthians 9:7a

Each man should give what he has decided in his heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion …

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Make up a story about a child whose mother sent her to buy a loaf of bread. The mother gave her Ls 5 to pay for it. The bread cost only Ls 4, so she had Ls 1 change. (Make the price the average one in your area, and replace the currency with the one in use in your country.) When her mother asked her what the bread had cost, she lied. She said it had cost Ls 5, and she kept the change for herself. Then her sister saw the extra money and told their mother. The mother was angry.

Why was the mother angry? Answer: Her daughter had lied to her.

Main Points of the Lesson

Tell the story of Acts 5:1-11 in your own words.

1. Ananias and Sapphira were husband and wife. They had heard Peter preach and they had decided to follow Jesus. They belonged to the first church, where people shared all their belongings with one another. They saw that many people sold their possessions and gave the money to the church to give to those in need (4:32, 34-35). Then Ananias and Sapphira sold a piece of their land (5:1).

2. They only gave part of the money they received for their land to the church. However, they said that it was the whole sum. They lied about keeping a portion for themselves (5:2, 8).

Give an example, using the currency the children are familiar with: Land sold for Ls 400 Sum given to the church Ls 200 Ananias and Sapphira kept Ls 200

3. Peter talked to Ananias, giving him the opportunity to be honest and confess (5:3-4). However, he kept on lying. He fell down and died. Everyone who heard about it was filled with fear and respect for God’s power (5:5).

4. Later on, Sapphira arrived there. She had not heard what had happened to Ananias. She also refused to be honest before God and was struck down, too (5:7-10a). Show Picture 6. That taught the members of the church that God expects us to be honest and sincere when we bring him an offering. To God, what goes on in our hearts is more important than what we do to impress others.

Our Response to the Story

Think of situations that test our honesty in this way. Examples: when we behave very well at school, but disobey our parents at home, or when we are friendly with people only because we want something from them. Remember: you can’t deceive God. He sees inside your heart, and that is what counts for him.

Lesson - 6: GIVING TO GOD

Scripture Reading: Acts 5:1-11

1. Honest or Not? H/N

Write H if honest, N if not honest:

  1. Ananias and Sapphira said they gave everything but kept some. ( ___ ) 👉 N

  2. Peter gave them a chance to tell the truth. ( ___ ) 👉 H

  3. A child tells Mum the true price of the bread. ( ___ ) 👉 H

2. Complete the Sentences

Word box: God, lied, heart

  1. Ananias and Sapphira ____________ about their gift. __________ 👉 lied

  2. We cannot fool ____________. __________ 👉 God

  3. God sees what is in our ____________. __________ 👉 heart

3. Match the Following

A B
Ananias and Sapphira Sold some land
Peter Asked for the truth
The church Learnt to be honest with God
A cheerful giver Gives from the heart

Answers:
Ananias and Sapphira → Sold some land
Peter → Asked for the truth
The church → Learnt to be honest with God
A cheerful giver → Gives from the heart

4. Discussion Questions

  1. What was wrong with what they did?

    Sample answer: They pretended to give everything but did not tell the truth.

  2. What matters most when we give to God?

    Sample answer: A loving and honest heart.

  3. When is it hard to be honest?

    Sample answer: When we want to look good or keep something for ourselves.

5. Memory Verse:

2 Corinthians 9:7a — “Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion.”

Proverbs 12:22 — “The Lord detests lying lips, but he delights in people who are trustworthy.”

Craft 1: Honest Heart Offering Box

Materials: Small box or paper, crayons, glue, red paper

Instructions:

  1. Cover a small box with paper.

  2. Glue a red heart on the front.

  3. Write: “I give with a happy, honest heart.”

Lesson Point: God loves honest, cheerful givers.

Craft 2: Truth Bookmark

Materials: Card strip, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Colour a long strip of card.

  2. Write: “Tell the truth.”

  3. Decorate with hearts and stars.

Lesson Point: God wants us to be honest.

Game 1: Tell the Truth

How to Play:

  1. The teacher reads short situations, such as “You broke a cup”.

  2. Children say what an honest child would do.

  3. Everyone claps for honest answers.

Lesson Point: We should always tell the truth.

Game 2: Cheerful Givers

How to Play:

  1. Pass a “coin” (paper circle) around a circle.

  2. Each child says one thing they can give happily to God.

  3. Pray and thank God for loving givers.

Lesson Point: God loves a cheerful giver.
