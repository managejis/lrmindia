Peter in Prison

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 8: Acts of the Holy Spirit

Aim

To teach the children that the Lord protected and cared for Peter in prison.

Scripture Reading

Acts 12:1-11

Memory Verse

Psalm 32:7

You are my hiding-place; you will protect me from trouble …

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Have you ever noticed what a mother hen does with her chicks when she sees a big bird of prey, like a hawk, flying overhead? She spreads out her wings and gathers her chicks underneath them. In this way, she protects and hides them from danger.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Jesus’ first disciples, who now were the leaders of the early church, faced many dangers and persecution from the authorities. Some were put to death because of their faith (Acts 12:1-2).

2. Peter was one of those who were arrested. He was put in prison and guarded very carefully by four soldiers. The guards were changed regularly, so that they would not become tired and could always watch Peter closely (12:3-4).

3. The Christians in Jerusalem prayed continually for Peter (12:5).

4. The night before Peter was to go to court he slept well, even though he was chained to his guards (12:6). He must have known that he was safe in God’s hands, if he could sleep under those circumstances!

5. Suddenly an angel came into the prison. He had to shake Peter to wake him up (12:7a). Show Picture 11.

6. The angel gave Peter instructions. Explain what they were (12:7b-8). Peter thought he was still dreaming, that he was having another vision (12:9).

7. But it was really happening. The angel led Peter out of prison, past all the guards and through all the different gates. When they were safely among the city streets, the angel left Peter just as suddenly as he had appeared (12:10).

8. Only then did Peter realise that God had rescued him with a miracle (12:11).

Our Response to the Story

Refer to the introductory story again. As a mother hen protects her chicks, so God cares for and protects his children.

During the shelling of her hometown a woman named Mary was alone with her five young children. Her husband was away and the children were very frightened. As Mary gathered the children around her she thought, “Now we will see the proof that God cares for us.” God’s promises are not just words. He kept Mary and her children safe.

We have to remember, however, that God’s children are not automatically protected against all bad things like an umbrella protects a person against the sun or rain. God saved Peter from prison that time, but later in his life Peter was put to death for his faith. Like Jesus in the garden of Gethsemane on the night before he died, we can only pray for God’s will to be done.

Lesson - 11: PETER IN PRISON

Scripture Reading: Acts 12:1-11

1. True or False

Write T for True or F for False:

  1. Peter slept calmly in prison. ( ___ ) 👉 T

  2. The church did not pray for Peter. ( ___ ) 👉 F

  3. An angel led Peter out through the gates. ( ___ ) 👉 T

2. Complete the Sentences

Word box: angel, chains, prayed

  1. The ____________ woke Peter up. __________ 👉 angel

  2. Peter’s ____________ fell off. __________ 👉 chains

  3. The Christians ____________ for Peter. __________ 👉 prayed

3. Choose the Right Answer

Circle a), b) or c):

  1. How many soldiers guarded Peter at a time?
    a) 4 b) 14 c) 40 👉 a) 4

  2. Who is like a mother hen with her chicks?
    a) Soldiers b) God c) The king 👉 b) God

  3. Peter first thought it was a dream. What was it really?
    a) A dream b) A real rescue c) A story 👉 b) A real rescue

4. Discussion Questions

  1. How did God look after Peter?

    Sample answer: He sent an angel to set him free.

  2. What did the church do while Peter was in prison?

    Sample answer: They prayed for him.

  3. When do you need God’s care?

    Sample answer: When I am afraid, sick or alone.

5. Memory Verse:

Psalm 32:7 — “You are my hiding place; you will protect me from trouble and surround me with songs of deliverance.”

Psalm 91:11 — “For he will command his angels concerning you to guard you in all your ways.”

Craft 1: Hen and Chicks

Materials: Paper, cotton wool, crayons, glue

Instructions:

  1. Draw a big hen and glue on cotton wool for feathers.

  2. Draw small chicks tucked under her wing.

  3. Write: “God protects me.”

Lesson Point: God protects His children like a hen protects her chicks.

Craft 2: Open Door Card

Materials: Card, crayons, scissors

Instructions:

  1. Fold card to make a door.

  2. Draw Peter and an angel on the inside.

  3. Write: “God sets us free.”

Lesson Point: God rescued Peter from prison.

Game 1: Under the Wings

How to Play:

  1. One child is the hen and others are chicks.

  2. When the teacher says “Danger!”, chicks run under the hen’s arms.

  3. Say: “God keeps us safe.”

Lesson Point: God protects us.

Game 2: Follow the Angel

How to Play:

  1. Children line up quietly behind a leader “angel”.

  2. The leader walks a safe path around the room.

  3. Stop and thank God for His care.

Lesson Point: God guided Peter safely out.
