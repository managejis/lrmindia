Deborah Acts as God’s Voice

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 3: Victory through God

Aim

To show the children how Deborah encouraged the Israelites to trust in God for victory over their enemies.

Scripture Reading

Judges 4:1-10

Memory Verse

Psalm 34:8

Taste and see that the Lord is good; blessed is the man who takes refuge in him.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Make up a story or act a little play about two friends called Wani and Juma, who are going to buy a packet of biscuits. Wani, who has the money, promises to share the biscuits with Juma. But Wani then buys the biscuits and eats them all very quickly while Juma is not looking. Wani has broken his promise and Juma is upset.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. The Israelites broke their promise to Joshua. They said they would obey God’s commandments, but they turned their hearts from God and followed the wicked ways of the tribes around them. Because of this God allowed them to be conquered by cruel tribes who treated the Israelites very badly (Josh 23:14-16; Judg 4:1-3).

2. Deborah was an Israelite woman who faithfully followed God. She also was a prophetess; that is, someone who taught the people God’s will and brought them God’s messages. Describe how she sat under a palm tree between two big towns so that people could come to her with their problems (Judg 4:5-6). Show Picture 9.

3. One day Deborah sent a message to Barak, an Israelite soldier, to come and see her. The Israelites had turned back to God and asked him to save them from their enemies. Barak was to lead the army, Deborah told him (Judg 4:3, 67). Show Picture 9. Barak is the one dressed in green in the picture.

4. But Barak was afraid. The commander of the enemy army, Sisera, had many soldiers and chariots (fast carts on wheels), which Israel did not have. Barak agreed to lead the Israelite soldiers only if Deborah would go with them (Judg 4:7-9).

5. Deborah agreed to go with the army, but warned Barak that the honour of the victory over Sisera would not go to him; people would say that the Lord handed Sisera over to a woman (Judg 4:9-10).

Our Response to the Story

Barak had an important and difficult job to do, but his faith in God was weak. Deborah encouraged him. It is very important to encourage others in their journey with God. Ask God to give you a Christian friend if you don’t have one, so you can help each other stay obedient to God.

Lesson - 9: DEBORAH ACTS AS GOD’S VOICE

Scripture Reading: Judges 4:1-10

1. Who Am I?

Choose from: Deborah, Barak, Sisera

  1. I sat under a palm tree and told people what God said. Who am I? __________ 👉 Deborah

  2. I led Israel’s army. Who am I? __________ 👉 Barak

  3. I was the enemy’s commander. Who am I? __________ 👉 Sisera

2. Choose the Right Answer

Circle a), b) or c):

  1. What does a prophetess do?
    a) Fights battles b) Tells God’s messages c) Sells fruit 👉 b) Tells God’s messages

  2. Why was Barak afraid?
    a) The enemy had many chariots b) It was dark c) He was tired 👉 a) The enemy had many chariots

3. Encourage or Discourage? E/D

Write E for encourage or D for discourage:

  1. “Do not give up, God is with you!” ( ___ ) 👉 E

  2. “You will never do it.” ( ___ ) 👉 D

  3. Praying with a friend who is scared. ( ___ ) 👉 E

4. Discussion Questions

  1. How did Deborah help Barak?

    Sample answer: She told him God’s message and went with him.

  2. How did Wani break his promise to Juma?

    Sample answer: He ate all the biscuits he promised to share.

  3. Who can you encourage this week?

    Sample answer: A friend who feels afraid or sad.

5. Memory Verse:

Psalm 34:8 — “Taste and see that the Lord is good; blessed is the one who takes refuge in him.”

1 Thessalonians 5:11 — “Therefore encourage one another and build each other up.”

Craft 1: Palm Tree

Materials: Brown and green paper, glue, scissors

Instructions:

  1. Cut a brown trunk.

  2. Cut long green leaves.

  3. Glue the leaves on top.

  4. Write: “Deborah listened to God.”

Lesson Point: Deborah helped people hear God.

Craft 2: Encouragement Card

Materials: Folded paper, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Draw a smiling face on the front.

  2. Inside write: “God is with you.”

  3. Give it to a friend.

Lesson Point: We should encourage each other.

Game 1: Cheer Up

How to Play:

  1. One child pretends to be afraid.

  2. The others say kind, brave words.

  3. Take turns.

Lesson Point: Encouragement gives courage.

Game 2: Promise Keeper

How to Play:

  1. Make a simple promise, such as “I will share my pencil”.

  2. Children act out keeping it.

  3. Talk about why promises matter.

Lesson Point: God’s people keep their promises.
