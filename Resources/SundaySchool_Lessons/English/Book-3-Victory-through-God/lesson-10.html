God Lets the Israelites Defeat Sisera

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 3: Victory through God

Aim

To show the children that obedience to God is a stronger weapon than violence and might; it can overcome even the strongest enemy.

Scripture Reading

Judges 4:10-16; 5:19-22

Memory Verse

Psalm 62:6

[The Lord] alone is my rock and my salvation; he is my fortress, I shall not be shaken.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Describe how we feel when we have something difficult to do and we are very nervous. Your mouth goes dry, you have an uncomfortable feeling in your stomach and you perspire. Perhaps you find it difficult to speak.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Barak, the leader of the Israelite army, must have felt like this when he led his small group of soldiers to Mount Tabor to battle with the enemy under Sisera’s command (4:7). The Canaanite enemy had many more soldiers than Israel had, as well as 900 chariots. The only weapons the Israelites had were swords.

2. Nevertheless Barak and Deborah the prophetess obeyed God’s instructions to fight this mighty army. Deborah went along to encourage Barak and his soldiers (4:12-14).

3. Sisera and the Canaanites rode to the valley of the Kishon River near Mount Tabor in their chariots, but there the chariots’ wheels could not go over the rough paths of the hills. Then God sent a rainstorm. The river flooded and some of the chariots were washed away; others got stuck in the mud (4:10; 5:21). Show Picture 10. Do you see Deborah up on the hill, praying and encouraging the Israelites?

4. God gave the Israelites the victory over the Canaanites, who had more soldiers and better weapons. Only Sisera, the leader, escaped and ran away on foot (4:15-16).

Our Response to the Story

When the Israelites turned back to God and listened to what God told them through Deborah, the Lord gave them victory over their enemies. Our enemy is Satan. When we are tempted to do wrong or to ignore God, we should ask God for his help and power to resist temptation. With God’s power we can defeat Satan and overcome temptations, no matter how strong they seem.

Lesson - 10: GOD LETS THE ISRAELITES DEFEAT SISERA

Scripture Reading: Judges 4:10-16; 5:19-22

1. Put the Story in Order

a) Israel won the victory.
b) Barak led his soldiers to Mount Tabor.
c) God sent a rainstorm.
d) The enemy came with 900 chariots.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. Barak led his soldiers to Mount Tabor.

  2. The enemy came with 900 chariots.

  3. God sent a rainstorm.

  4. Israel won the victory.

2. True or False? T/F

Write T for true or F for false:

  1. Israel had more soldiers than the enemy. ( ___ ) 👉 F

  2. The chariots got stuck in the mud. ( ___ ) 👉 T

  3. Deborah went along to encourage Barak. ( ___ ) 👉 T

3. Missing Letters

Fill in the missing letters:

  1. K _ s h o n (the river that flooded) __________ 👉 Kishon

  2. C h _ r _ o t s (fast carts) __________ 👉 Chariots

  3. F _ r t r _ s s (a safe, strong place) __________ 👉 Fortress

4. Discussion Questions

  1. How did God help Israel?

    Sample answer: He sent rain, and the chariots got stuck.

  2. Who is our enemy?

    Sample answer: Satan, who tempts us to do wrong.

  3. How can we win over temptation?

    Sample answer: By asking God for his help and power.

5. Memory Verse:

Psalm 62:6 — “Truly he is my rock and my salvation; he is my fortress, I will not be shaken.”

James 4:7 — “Submit yourselves, then, to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.”

Craft 1: Rock and Fortress

Materials: Grey paper, glue, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Cut a big rock shape from grey paper.

  2. Glue paper bricks on it like a fortress.

  3. Write: “God is my rock.”

Lesson Point: God is strong and keeps us safe.

Craft 2: Muddy Chariot Picture

Materials: Brown paper, crayons, glue

Instructions:

  1. Draw a chariot on paper.

  2. Glue brown scraps below for mud.

  3. Write: “God is stronger.”

Lesson Point: God is stronger than any army.

Game 1: Stuck in the Mud

How to Play:

  1. Lay paper “mud” patches on the floor.

  2. Children step round the patches.

  3. If they touch one, they say: “God is my rock.”

Lesson Point: God helps us in hard places.

Game 2: Say No to Temptation

How to Play:

  1. Call out a temptation, like “Take your friend’s sweet.”

  2. Children say: “No! God, help me!”

  3. Then give a thumbs-up.

Lesson Point: With God’s help we can say no.
