Samson Destroys the Philistines

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 3: Victory through God

Aim

To show the children that it never is too late to turn to God. However, it is very sad to realise that you have wasted many chances to serve God.

Scripture Reading

Judges 16:21-30

Memory Verse

Psalm 51:1

Have mercy on me, O God, according to your unfailing love …

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

If you watch a group of dogs or chickens you will notice that they all attack the weakest one. People often are like that: they make fun of those who cannot fight back.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Samson used to be a very strong man. Show Picture 19 and briefly review the story. He had lost his God-given strength because of his disobedience and ungodly way of life. Now the Philistines held him prisoner.

2. The Philistine leaders put out his eyes and made him work in a grinding mill, walking round and round to turn the big stones (16:21).

3. The Philistine leaders were celebrating Samson’s defeat by holding a feast in honour of their idol Dagon. They sent for Samson so they could make fun of him (16:23-27).

4. Samson had come to realise that God was the true source of his strength, so he turned to God and asked him to make him strong one last time (16:28).

5. God heard his prayer. Samson leaned on the pillars holding up the roof of Dagon’s temple and pulled the building down, killing everybody there (16:29-30). Show Picture 20.

6. Samson died with the Philistine leaders. He had wasted the greatest part of his life as well as his precious gift by disobeying God. That was very sad. However, when he turned back to God, God did not reject him.

Our Response to the Story

The story of Samson’s life is a warning to us. God gave him an extraordinary gift but it brought Samson no joy in the end, because he misused it for his own purposes. Let us thank Jesus Christ for coming to show us the way to everlasting victory over disobedience and selfishness.

Lesson - 20: SAMSON DESTROYS THE PHILISTINES

Scripture Reading: Judges 16:21-30

1. Yes or No? Y/N

Write Y for yes or N for no:

  1. Did Samson turn back to God in prison? ( ___ ) 👉 Y

  2. Did God turn Samson away when he prayed? ( ___ ) 👉 N

  3. Did Samson waste many chances to serve God? ( ___ ) 👉 Y

2. Complete the Sentences

Word box: prayed, sorry, mercy

  1. Samson ____________ to God for help. __________ 👉 prayed

  2. God shows ____________ to those who ask. __________ 👉 mercy

  3. We should say ____________ when we do wrong. __________ 👉 sorry

3. Choose the Right Answer

Circle a), b) or c):

  1. Who laughed at the weak and helpless?
    a) The Philistine leaders b) Deborah c) Gideon 👉 a) The Philistine leaders

  2. What do we learn about God?
    a) He gives up on us b) It is never too late to turn to him c) He is slow 👉 b) It is never too late to turn to him

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Is it ever too late to turn to God?

    Sample answer: No. He forgives those who are truly sorry.

  2. Why is it sad to waste chances?

    Sample answer: We miss the good things God wants us to do.

  3. How can we help someone who is weak?

    Sample answer: Be kind and never laugh at them.

5. Memory Verse:

Psalm 51:1 — “Have mercy on me, O God, according to your unfailing love; according to your great compassion blot out my transgressions.”

Psalm 86:5 — “You, Lord, are forgiving and good, abounding in love to all who call to you.”

Craft 1: Open Door Card

Materials: Folded card, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Draw a door on the front.

  2. Inside write: “God welcomes me.”

  3. Decorate the card.

Lesson Point: God’s door is always open.

Craft 2: Sorry Heart

Materials: Red paper, crayons, glue

Instructions:

  1. Cut a heart in two parts.

  2. Write “sorry” on one half.

  3. Glue the halves together and write: “Forgiven.”

Lesson Point: God forgives and mends our hearts.

Game 1: Come Back

How to Play:

  1. One child walks away from the group.

  2. The group calls, “Come back!”

  3. The child returns and is welcomed.

Lesson Point: God welcomes us when we turn back.

Game 2: Kind or Unkind?

How to Play:

  1. Read short examples.

  2. Children show a happy or sad face.

  3. Talk about being kind to the weak.

Lesson Point: We should be kind to everyone.
