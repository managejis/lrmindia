300 Men Defeat a Multitude

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 3: Victory through God

Aim

To show the children that we are not saved by our own effort, but by the grace of God.

Scripture Reading

Judges 7:8-22

Memory Verse

Judges 7:15

“The Lord has given the Midianite camp into your hands.”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Ask the class what equipment they think an army needs when it goes into battle. Examples: It will need well-trained soldiers, guns, tanks and ammunition, as well as radios to keep in contact with the commanders.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Gideon was leading the Israelites against the large army of the Midianites. They had more men and camels than anyone could count (7:12). Gideon only had 300 men, but he also had God’s guidance (7:8-9).

2. God encouraged Gideon by telling him to go in secret with his servant, Purah, down to the Midianite camp and listen to what they were saying about the Israelites (7:10-11).

3. Describe how Gideon and Purah went and overheard the Midianite soldiers. They discovered that the Midianites were very frightened of Gideon and his little group of soldiers because they knew God was with them (7:13-15).

4. Gideon was encouraged by what he had heard, but he gave his 300 soldiers some unusual instructions and weapons. Describe how the soldiers were divided into three groups of 100. Apart from a sword, every soldier was given a trumpet and a light inside a jar. Everyone had to watch Gideon and do just as he did (7:16-18).

5. Gideon led his soldiers to the Midianite camp. Describe what happens in 7:19-20. All the children can act this out as you tell the story. Everyone can blow through their hands to make the sound of the trumpets, and clap their hands to represent the jars breaking. You could use a regular torch to shine the light, and then everyone can shout, “A sword for the Lord and for Gideon!”

6. We have now made a loud noise, but Gideon’s 300 men made a greater one. It frightened and confused the Midianites so much that some started fighting each other, while the rest ran away (7:21-22). Show Picture 16. Israel was saved, thanks to God.

Our Response to the Story

Cici was a seven-year-old girl from a poor home in an Asian town. She liked to go to a Bible Club called Happy Wednesday, with about 70 others. One day she arrived there when it was almost dark, long after the meeting was over. “I’m sorry I’m late,” she said, “but when it was time to come my dad locked me up in a room. He doesn’t like me coming here. But I love hearing about Jesus. I have prayed about this. What shall I do?” The leader and Cici prayed together, and then they decided that she could come to the Club on another day. God helped a little girl triumph over circumstances that seemed much stronger than she was.

Lesson - 16: 300 MEN DEFEAT A MULTITUDE

Scripture Reading: Judges 7:8-22

1. Put the Story in Order

a) They blew the trumpets and broke the jars.
b) Gideon heard the enemy was afraid.
c) The enemy ran away.
d) The soldiers got trumpets and jars with lights.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. Gideon heard the enemy was afraid.

  2. The soldiers got trumpets and jars with lights.

  3. They blew the trumpets and broke the jars.

  4. The enemy ran away.

2. Who Am I?

Choose from: Gideon, Purah, the Midianites

  1. I went with Gideon to the enemy camp. Who am I? __________ 👉 Purah

  2. We ran away in fear. Who are we? __________ 👉 the Midianites

  3. I told my men, “Watch me and do the same.” Who am I? __________ 👉 Gideon

3. Yes or No? Y/N

Write Y for yes or N for no:

  1. Did Gideon’s men carry swords only? ( ___ ) 👉 N

  2. Did God give them the victory? ( ___ ) 👉 Y

  3. Did a girl named Cici keep trusting God? ( ___ ) 👉 Y

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did the light in jars help?

    Sample answer: It made the enemy think a huge army was there.

  2. Who saved Israel?

    Sample answer: God did, by his grace.

  3. What did Cici do when her dad locked her in?

    Sample answer: She prayed and found another way to go.

5. Memory Verse:

Judges 7:15 — “Get up! The Lord has given the Midianite camp into your hands.”

Romans 8:31 — “If God is for us, who can be against us?”

Craft 1: Jar with Light

Materials: Clear cup, yellow paper, glue

Instructions:

  1. Cut a yellow flame from paper.

  2. Glue it inside a clear cup.

  3. Write: “God gives light.”

Lesson Point: Gideon’s men held lights in jars.

Craft 2: Trumpet Picture

Materials: Paper, crayons, glue

Instructions:

  1. Draw a trumpet.

  2. Colour it gold.

  3. Add music notes around.

Lesson Point: God’s plan was simple but powerful.

Game 1: Shout and Shine

How to Play:

  1. Everyone blows through their hands.

  2. Clap for the breaking jars.

  3. Shout together: “A sword for the Lord and for Gideon!”

Lesson Point: God gave Gideon victory.

Game 2: Torch Tag

How to Play:

  1. One child holds a torch.

  2. The others freeze when the light finds them.

  3. Take turns.

Lesson Point: God’s light shows the way.
