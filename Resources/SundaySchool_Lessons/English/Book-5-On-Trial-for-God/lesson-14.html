Daniel and the King of Babylon

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 5: On Trial for God

Aim

To show the children how Daniel and his friends walked close to God in a hostile situation, and God gave them great power and responsibility.

Scripture Reading

Daniel 2:1-49

Memory Verse

Exodus 2:11

(the Ten Commandments) “For in six days the Lord made the heavens and the earth, the sea, and all that is in them, but he rested on the seventh day.”
NOTE: This passage is too long to tell the children all the details; it is enough if they know what the dreams were about.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

What does it feel like if you are worried about something? Give the children time to respond. You probably can’t sleep, and you may not want to eat. It’s a good idea to go and talk to someone about what is worrying you.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Nebuchadnezzar, the powerful king of Babylon, was very worried. He had had upsetting dreams that he could not understand. He sent for all his magicians and stargazers, and decided to test their abilities. They had to tell him not only what his dreams meant, but also what he had dreamed about (Dan 1:1-9)! Nebuchadnezzar was very angry when they could not do it and ordered all his wise men to be killed. This included Daniel and his three friends (2:10-13).

2. Daniel and his friends prayed to God, who revealed the dream to Daniel (2:14-19).

3. Daniel explained to King Nebuchadnezzar that it was only the God of heaven who could reveal what the dreams meant that he had sent to the king (2:24-29). Show Picture 14. Notice how very humble Daniel was, but what courage God gave him to speak out to a powerful earthly king.

4. God was telling Nebuchadnezzar about the future. Many kingdoms would come and go in the history of the world, but a greater kingdom was coming that would destroy them all. This was the kingdom of God, which would last for ever (2:36-39, 44-45).

5. The king was pleased with Daniel. He spared all the other wise men and made Daniel governor over all of Babylon (2:46-49).

Our Response to the Story

The president and government of Albania, a country in Europe, said, “There is no God.” They closed the churches and forbade the Albanian Christians even to pray. But people in the rest of the world prayed for that country. In 1991 their prayers were answered and that government fell. Now churches are opening again and thousands of people are asking for Bibles and Christian literature in this very poor country. Our God is the supreme Ruler over the whole world, even if earthly rulers think they can ignore him.

Instil the Memory Verse

This verse shows the power of God as Creator of the whole world.

Recite all the commandments learnt so far.

Lesson - 14: DANIEL AND THE KING OF BABYLON

Scripture Reading: Daniel 2:1-49

1. Put the Story in Order

a) God showed Daniel the dream.
b) The king had a dream no one could explain.
c) The king made Daniel a governor.
d) Daniel and his friends prayed.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. The king had a dream no one could explain.

  2. Daniel and his friends prayed.

  3. God showed Daniel the dream.

  4. The king made Daniel a governor.

2. Match the Following

A B
The king Was worried by his dream
The wise men Could not explain the dream
Daniel Prayed for God’s help
God’s kingdom Will last for ever

Answers:
The king → Was worried by his dream
The wise men → Could not explain the dream
Daniel → Prayed for God’s help
God’s kingdom → Will last for ever

3. Circle the Right Words

Circle the correct word:

  1. Daniel was very (proud / humble). 👉 humble

  2. Only (God / the king) can reveal secrets. 👉 God

  3. God made the heavens and the (earth / moon). 👉 earth

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Who showed the king’s dream to Daniel?

    Sample answer: God showed it to him when he and his friends prayed.

  2. What did Daniel do when he spoke to the king?

    Sample answer: He gave God the credit and spoke with courage.

  3. Who rules the world?

    Sample answer: God does.

5. Memory Verse:

Exodus 20:11 — “For in six days the Lord made the heavens and the earth, the sea, and all that is in them, but he rested on the seventh day.”

Daniel 2:20 — “Praise be to the name of God for ever and ever.”

Craft 1: Dream Cloud

Materials: Blue paper, cotton wool, glue, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Cut a cloud shape from blue paper.

  2. Glue cotton wool on it.

  3. Draw the statue from the dream.

  4. Write: “God knows all things.”

Lesson Point: God reveals what is hidden.

Craft 2: Prayer Hands

Materials: Paper, crayons, scissors

Instructions:

  1. Trace both hands and cut them out.

  2. Glue them together at the wrist like praying hands.

  3. Write: “Daniel prayed.”

Lesson Point: When we are worried we can pray.

Game 1: Worry Box

How to Play:

  1. Children write or draw a worry on paper.

  2. Put the papers in a box and pray together.

  3. Say: “God knows all about it.”

Lesson Point: We can give our worries to God.

Game 2: Whisper Dream

How to Play:

  1. One child whispers a made-up “dream” to another.

  2. The second child says only “God knows!”

  3. Take turns.

Lesson Point: Only God truly knows everything.
