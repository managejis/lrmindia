Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 5: On Trial for God

Aim

To show the children how Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego showed that faith in God is better than fear of people in power.

Scripture Reading

Daniel 3:1-18

Memory Verse

Exodus 2:11

(the Ten Commandments) “For in six days the Lord made the heavens and the earth, the sea, and all that is in them, but he rested on the seventh day. Therefore the Lord blessed the Sabbath day and made it holy.”
NOTE: Review last week’s memory verse and complete verse 11 this week.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Take a torch along to class and shine the light on the children. They probably will shield their eyes against the bright light.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon had an enormous statue made of gold. It was set up on a plain so it could be seen from a long way off, shining brightly in the sun like a torch (Dan 3:1). You and your class could measure out the size on the ground. It was almost 30 metres high and almost three metres wide.

2. Nebuchadnezzar had become very proud. He ruled over a very large kingdom and it was the custom for rulers in those days to put up large statues of themselves. Perhaps King Nebuchadnezzar even thought of himself as a god and wanted the people to bow down to him, showing how great and clever he was. There was a great opening ceremony, where everyone had to bow before the king’s statue (3:2-5). Show Picture 15. There was a terrible punishment for anyone who would not obey, so everybody obeyed (3:6-7).

3. Everybody but Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego. They were the three friends of Daniel who came with him as Jewish prisoners to Babylon. They were still slaves, but had been given great responsibility in the government and some Babylonians were jealous of them. They plotted to get rid of them, and now they saw their chance. They told Nebuchadnezzar about these three foreigners who disobeyed the king’s orders. They were Jews, who worshipped only one God (3:8-12).

Let the class recite the relevant part of the Ten Commandments: Exodus 20:1-6.

4. The king was very angry when he heard about Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego’s disobedience to his command. He sent for them and questioned them. There was no god who could rescue them from the powerful king of Babylon, Nebuchadnezzar thought (3:13-15).

5. The three men’s answer is very important, because it shows their faith in God as well as their commitment to God, no matter what. They replied, “The God we serve is able to save us! But even if he doesn’t, we will not worship the golden statue or any other god” (3:16-18).

Our Response to the Story

Obedience to God’s commandments comes before anything else. Everybody who was at that ceremony saw Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego’s witness. These young men did not hide their faith or their God.

In January 1994 a bishop was killed for his faith in God. Just before he died he said, “I am ready to die for the cause of the church so that others will be able to worship their God peacefully and without so much fear. I will not die a silent death.” Pray for Christians who are suffering for their Christian witness today.

Like Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego, we have to accept that God does not always take away our suffering. But we can hold on to our faith and be witnesses for the Lord, no matter what the circumstances or the outcome.

Lesson - 15: THE IMAGE OF GOLD

Scripture Reading: Daniel 3:1-18

1. Choose the Right Answer

Circle a), b) or c):

  1. What did King Nebuchadnezzar build?
    a) A golden statue b) A boat c) A tower of wood 👉 a) A golden statue

  2. What did the three friends refuse to do?
    a) Eat bread b) Bow to the statue c) Sing 👉 b) Bow to the statue

2. Right or Wrong?

Write R for right or W for wrong:

  1. Worshipping only the living God. ( ___ ) 👉 R

  2. Bowing to a statue because everyone else does. ( ___ ) 👉 W

  3. Telling the truth about my faith. ( ___ ) 👉 R

3. Who Am I?

Choose from: Shadrach, the king, a jealous man

  1. I was angry and sent for the three friends. Who am I? __________ 👉 the king

  2. I told the king about the three Jews. Who am I? __________ 👉 a jealous man

  3. I said, “The God we serve is able to save us.” Who am I? __________ 👉 Shadrach

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did the three friends not bow down?

    Sample answer: They worshipped only God.

  2. What did they say about God’s power?

    Sample answer: He is able to save them, but even if he does not, they will not worship the statue.

  3. Do we always have to bow to what people tell us?

    Sample answer: No. We must obey God first.

5. Memory Verse:

Exodus 20:11 — “For in six days the Lord made the heavens and the earth, the sea, and all that is in them, but he rested on the seventh day. Therefore the Lord blessed the Sabbath day and made it holy.”

Matthew 4:10 — “Worship the Lord your God, and serve him only.”

Craft 1: Golden Statue Picture

Materials: Yellow paper, foil, crayons, glue

Instructions:

  1. Cut a tall statue from yellow paper.

  2. Cover it with foil.

  3. Draw people standing at its feet.

  4. Write: “Worship God alone.”

Lesson Point: No statue or king is greater than God.

Craft 2: Three Friends Standing Tall

Materials: Paper strips, crayons, scissors

Instructions:

  1. Cut three people from folded paper.

  2. Colour each differently.

  3. Write: “We will not bow.”

Lesson Point: We stay faithful to God, no matter what.

Game 1: Statue Freeze

How to Play:

  1. Play soft music and children dance.

  2. When it stops they freeze like statues.

  3. Say: “Only God is worth worshipping.”

Lesson Point: We worship God, not statues.

Game 2: Stand Firm

How to Play:

  1. Children stand in a line.

  2. The teacher calls “Bow!” and only the teacher bows.

  3. Children say “We worship God!”

Lesson Point: We stand firm for God.
