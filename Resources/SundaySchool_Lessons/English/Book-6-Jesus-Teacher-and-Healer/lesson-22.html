A Deaf and Dumb Man Hears and Speaks

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 6: Jesus Teacher and Healer

Aim

To teach the children that Jesus used his power over illness and suffering to show people that he really was the Son of God.

Scripture Reading

Mark 7:31-37

Memory Verse

Mark 7:37

“[Jesus] has done every- thing well … He even makes the deaf hear and the mute speak.”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Ask a few children in the class to put their hands over their ears. Then in your normal voice ask them to do something simple. For example: “Fetch a book from the table” or “Stand up.” They will not hear you very well and the other children may either shout the request at them or make signs to convey your meaning to them.

This will probably cause some confusion! However, this is exactly the point you want to make. Deaf people find life very difficult because they cannot understand what is going on around them. It is bad enough to lose one’s hearing, but those who are born deaf often are dumb, because people learn to speak by listening to those around them and repeating the sounds and words they hear others use.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. One day a deaf and dumb man’s friends brought him to Jesus. They asked for Jesus’ help (7:32).

2. Jesus took the man aside so that the man would not be frightened by the crowd that was always present wherever Jesus went. The man probably did not even know who Jesus was! As the man could not hear him, Jesus used signs to show the man what he was going to do. Putting his fingers in his ears was a sign that Jesus would cure his deafness. Touching his tongue meant Jesus would make him speak. Looking up was a sign of prayer (7:33-34a).

3. Jesus prayed, saying the words, “Open up” (7:34). Show Picture 22.

Immediately the man could hear and speak (7:35).

4. Everyone who heard the man - who used to be deaf and dumb - speak, was amazed. They could not stop talking about the wonderful miracles Jesus performed.

Instil the Memory Verse

Our Response to the Lesson

Everyone who saw those miracles was amazed. The people in the story said Jesus did everything well. They were right.

Jesus is perfect because he is God. But very few people trusted Jesus and loved him when he was on earth. He asks us to trust and love him because he is able to love and help us perfectly.

Lesson - 22: A DEAF AND DUMB MAN HEARS AND SPEAKS

Scripture Reading: Mark 7:31-37

1. Match the Following

A B
The friends Brought the man to Jesus
Jesus Took him aside and healed him
The man Could hear and speak
The crowd Was amazed

Answers:
The friends → Brought the man to Jesus
Jesus → Took him aside and healed him
The man → Could hear and speak
The crowd → Was amazed

2. Missing Letters

Fill in the missing letters:

  1. H _ A R __________ 👉 HEAR

  2. S P _ A _ __________ 👉 SPEAK

  3. A M _ Z _ D __________ 👉 AMAZED

3. True or False

Write True or False:

  1. Jesus took the man away from the crowd. ( ___ ) 👉 True

  2. Jesus said, “Open up!” ( ___ ) 👉 True

  3. The people kept quiet about the miracle. ( ___ ) 👉 False

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did Jesus take the man aside?

    Sample answer: So he would not be frightened by the crowd.

  2. What did the people say about Jesus?

    Sample answer: That He does everything well.

  3. How can we use our ears and mouths for Jesus?

    Sample answer: By listening to His Word and telling others about Him.

5. Memory Verse:

Mark 7:37 — “He has done everything well. He even makes the deaf hear and the mute speak.”

Isaiah 35:5 — “Then will the eyes of the blind be opened and the ears of the deaf unstopped.”

Craft 1: Hearing Ear

Materials: Card, crayons, scissors

Instructions:

  1. Cut a big ear shape from card.

  2. Colour it and write: “Jesus helps me hear.”

  3. Decorate with a small smiling mouth.

Lesson Point: Jesus cares for those who cannot hear or speak.

Craft 2: Sign Language Card

Materials: Card, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Draw a hand sign for “love” on a card.

  2. Colour the hand brightly.

  3. Write: “I love Jesus.”

Lesson Point: We can show love without words.

Game 1: Silent Message

How to Play:

  1. One child silently acts a simple action, such as eating or sleeping.

  2. The others guess the action.

  3. Talk about how deaf people use signs.

Lesson Point: Jesus used signs to help the deaf man understand.

Game 2: Open Up!

How to Play:

  1. Children cover their ears and stand quietly.

  2. The teacher says “Open up!” and they uncover their ears.

  3. They say, “Thank You, Jesus, for ears!”

Lesson Point: Jesus has power to heal.
