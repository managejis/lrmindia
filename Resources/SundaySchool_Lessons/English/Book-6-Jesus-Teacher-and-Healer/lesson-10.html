The Workers in the Vineyard

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 6: Jesus Teacher and Healer

Aim

To teach the children that God gives eternal life to all who believe in Jesus Christ, not only to “good people”. No-one can “earn” the gift of salvation.

Scripture Reading

Matthew 20:1-16

Memory Verse

1 John 5:11

God has given us eternal life, and this life is in his Son.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

What happens at home when it is time to eat? Often, not everyone eats at the same time. Some may be out at that moment; they will eat later. However, in the end, everyone is fed.

If this example isn’t suitable, use the example of a tea party: Guests are given refreshments when they arrive.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Jesus told a story about a man who owned a vineyard. When the grapes were ready to be harvested, he needed extra workers to help him. In the morning he went to the marketplace and hired some workers. They agreed on the wage beforehand: a silver coin for the day’s labour. The men started working (20:1-2).

2. Still, the work was too much for the number of workers. So the owner hired some more workers. He did this three more times during the day: at 9 o’clock, at 12 o’clock (midday) and at 5 o’clock (when the workday was almost over). They were hired at a fair wage. Describe what happened in (20:3-7).

3. At the end of the day all the workers were called to receive their pay. They were all paid the same amount! The men who had worked since early that morning complained. They said they should receive more because they worked longer and harder (20:8-12). Show Picture 10.

4. The owner replied that he had paid them what they had agreed upon when he had hired them, which was a fair wage for a full day’s work (20:13-15).

5. God is like the owner in this story. He gives us all eternal life because Jesus died for all of mankind. Before God all people are equal. Nobody “deserves” to be saved any more than someone else; God’s mercy is a gift.

Our Response to the Story

God says it does not matter when we come to follow him – when we have grown old or while we are still young. All who accept his invitation to come to him will receive eternal life. The only difference is: if we take this gift when we are young we have all of our lives to follow and honour Jesus Christ.

Lesson - 10: THE WORKERS IN THE VINEYARD

Scripture Reading: Matthew 20:1-16

1. Who Am I?

Write the answer:

  1. I owned the vineyard and hired workers. Who am I? __________ 👉 The vineyard owner

  2. We started work early in the morning and complained. Who are we? __________ 👉 The first workers

  3. We were hired at five o’clock. Who are we? __________ 👉 The last workers

2. True or False

Write True or False:

  1. The owner paid the last workers less. ( ___ ) 👉 False

  2. All the workers received the same pay. ( ___ ) 👉 True

  3. God’s gift of life is a gift, not a reward. ( ___ ) 👉 True

3. Count and Write

Write the answer:

  1. The workers were paid one silver ____________. __________ 👉 coin

  2. The owner went to the market ____ times in the day. __________ 👉 5

  3. Eternal life is a ____________ from God. __________ 👉 gift

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did the first workers complain?

    Sample answer: They thought they should get more for working longer.

  2. What is God like in this story?

    Sample answer: Kind and fair; He gives to all as a gift.

  3. Why is it good to follow Jesus while young?

    Sample answer: We can follow and honour Him all our lives.

5. Memory Verse:

1 John 5:11 — “God has given us eternal life, and this life is in his Son.”

Ephesians 2:8 — “It is by grace you have been saved, through faith ... it is the gift of God.”

Craft 1: Grape Bunch

Materials: Purple paper circles, green paper, glue, card

Instructions:

  1. Cut many small purple circles.

  2. Glue them in a bunch shape on a card.

  3. Add a green leaf and write: “God’s gift.”

Lesson Point: God’s gift of life is for everyone who believes.

Craft 2: Silver Coin Gift

Materials: Card, silver or grey paper, glue, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Cut a circle from silver paper and glue it on a card.

  2. Draw a ribbon and a bow around it.

  3. Write: “A gift, not a prize.”

Lesson Point: We cannot earn salvation; God gives it freely.

Game 1: Gift for All

How to Play:

  1. Hand out equal small gifts (a sticker each) to all children.

  2. Some children came early and some late; give the same to all.

  3. Talk about how it feels.

Lesson Point: God gives His gift to all who come.

Game 2: Vineyard Workers

How to Play:

  1. Children pretend to pick grapes and put them in a basket.

  2. The teacher calls “Work time!” at different times for different groups.

  3. At the end everyone gets a clap of thanks.

Lesson Point: Everyone is welcome to work for God.
