Jesus Calms a Storm

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 6: Jesus Teacher and Healer

Aim

To teach the children that Jesus’ power is greater than any other power in heaven or on earth. We can trust him to be with us and protect us.

Scripture Reading

Mark 4:35-41

Memory Verse

Matthew 28:18

Jesus came to them and said, “All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me.”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Describe a time you were very frightened because something you could do nothing about was happening. Example: When Juba, in the south of the Sudan, was shelled in February 1989 and in 1990.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. The disciples were very frightened. They were out in a boat when a storm came. Several of the disciples had been fishermen before they became Jesus’ disciples, but even they were frightened. Describe a storm at sea; think of the noise it makes. The water came right into the boat (Mk 4:37)!

2. Jesus was with them, but he was sleeping in the back of the boat. The disciples woke him up. They were angry with Jesus. They thought he did not care what happened to them (4:38).

3. Jesus stood up and stopped the storm. Describe what happened (4:39).

Show Picture 18.

4. Then Jesus spoke to his disciples. They had watched him perform many miracles before. If there is time, ask the class to tell you about some of the miracles the disciples had seen Jesus do.

In spite of living with Jesus, seeing and hearing everything he did and taught, the disciples still did not understand that Jesus is God himself. He created everything that exists and he can even control the forces of nature, like a storm. The disciples found this very difficult to understand (4:40-41).

Instil the Memory Verse

Our Response to the Story

Sometimes, when we believe we are in danger, we behave just like the disciples. They accused Jesus of not caring about them. We do the same sometimes. However, Jesus is with us in the midst of danger. No power is greater than his power, which is the power of God. We need to trust him. Read Romans 8:35, 37-39 to the children.

Lesson - 18: JESUS CALMS A STORM

Scripture Reading: Mark 4:35-41

1. Who Am I?

Write the answer:

  1. I was sleeping at the back of the boat. Who am I? __________ 👉 Jesus

  2. We were afraid in the storm and woke Jesus. Who are we? __________ 👉 The disciples

  3. I stopped when Jesus said, “Quiet! Be still!” What am I? __________ 👉 The storm

2. Trust or Worry? T/W

Write T for trust or W for worry:

  1. Jesus is with me in the dark. ( ___ ) 👉 T

  2. I am sure no one cares about me. ( ___ ) 👉 W

  3. I pray when I am scared. ( ___ ) 👉 T

3. Match the Following

A B
The storm Waves filled the boat
The disciples Were very afraid
Jesus Stopped the wind
The sea Became calm

Answers:
The storm → Waves filled the boat
The disciples → Were very afraid
Jesus → Stopped the wind
The sea → Became calm

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why were the disciples afraid even though they were fishermen?

    Sample answer: The storm was very big and the boat was filling with water.

  2. What did Jesus’ power over the storm show?

    Sample answer: That He is God and in charge of everything.

  3. What can you do when you are scared?

    Sample answer: Trust Jesus and pray to Him.

5. Memory Verse:

Matthew 28:18 — “Jesus came to them and said, “All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me.””

Psalm 56:3 — “When I am afraid, I put my trust in you.”

Craft 1: Boat in the Storm

Materials: Blue paper waves, brown paper boat, glue, card

Instructions:

  1. Glue wavy blue strips across a card.

  2. Glue a brown boat on the waves.

  3. Draw Jesus in the boat and write: “Peace, be still.”

Lesson Point: Jesus is with us in every storm.

Craft 2: Calm Sea Picture

Materials: Light blue paper, crayons, glue

Instructions:

  1. Draw straight, smooth lines of blue across a page.

  2. Add a small boat and a smiling sun.

  3. Write: “Jesus is greater.”

Lesson Point: Jesus calms what frightens us.

Game 1: Storm and Calm

How to Play:

  1. Children make storm sounds by tapping and stamping.

  2. The teacher says “Peace! Be still!” and everyone freezes.

  3. Repeat with a different child as “Jesus”.

Lesson Point: Jesus’ voice brings calm.

Game 2: Row the Boat

How to Play:

  1. Children sit in rows as a boat and “row” together.

  2. The teacher shouts “Storm!” and they row harder.

  3. At “Jesus!” they stop and cheer.

Lesson Point: Jesus is stronger than any storm.
