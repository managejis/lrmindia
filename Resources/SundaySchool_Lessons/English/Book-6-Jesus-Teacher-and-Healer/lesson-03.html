A Light Should Not Be Hidden

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 6: Jesus Teacher and Healer

Aim

To show the children that our lives are to be like lights showing people the way to God.

Scripture Reading

Matthew 5:14-16; John 8:12; Mark 4:21-23; 1 Kings 17:8-24

Memory Verse

Matthew 5:16

“[L]et your light shine before men, that they may see your good deeds and praise your Father in heaven.”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Show the class a candle or a torch. Do you know what this is used for? Light the candle or switch on the torch and put it behind a desk or somewhere where it can’t be seen. Do you think this is a good place to put it? Does this light help us now?

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Jesus talked about putting a light where it is useful.

Show Picture 3. What are the people in the picture doing with the lights?

2. Jesus said, “I am the light of the world.” He meant that he shows us the right path to follow. Without Jesus, we live in darkness because we are sinners. Jesus said those of us who follow him must be like lights that show other people the right path to follow. If we hide our lights they will not find the way.

3. Say the memory verse. These are Jesus’ words.

The truth of this verse can be made clear by telling the class the story of Elijah and the widow in the town of Zarephath:

4. During a time of great famine, the prophet Elijah visited a certain town where a very poor widow lived with her only son. He asked the widow for food. She had almost nothing left (1 K 17:812). However, she agreed to feed him.

5. Elijah promised the widow that God would not let their food run out - and it didn’t! (17:1316).

6. However, some other disaster struck the poor widow. Her son fell ill and died. She was very upset. By God’s power Elijah healed the boy (17:18-23).

7. The widow knew Elijah was a prophet of God, and in the end she, too, worshipped Elijah’s God (17:24).

Instil the Memory Verse

Our Response to the Story

We must think: How can you show in your home and everywhere you go that you are a light that shows the way to Jesus? Make a few suggestions to get the class talking, eg: What about doing your household tasks without grumbling?

End the lesson with a prayer asking God to help the class shine their light.

Lesson - 3: A LIGHT SHOULD NOT BE HIDDEN

Scripture Reading: Matthew 5:14-16; John 8:12; Mark 4:21-23; 1 Kings 17:8-24

1. Who Am I?

Write the answer:

  1. I said, “I am the light of the world.” Who am I? __________ 👉 Jesus

  2. I was a prophet who asked a poor widow for food. Who am I? __________ 👉 Elijah

  3. I lived in Zarephath with my only son. Who am I? __________ 👉 The widow

2. Odd One Out

Circle the odd one out:

  1. candle – torch – lamp – stone 👉 stone

  2. sharing – helping – grumbling – smiling 👉 grumbling

  3. bright – shine – glow – hide 👉 hide

3. Complete the Sentences

Word box: shine, food, light

  1. We should let our ____________ shine. __________ 👉 light

  2. The widow shared her last ____________. __________ 👉 food

  3. Jesus wants us to ____________ for Him. __________ 👉 shine

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Where should we put a lamp?

    Sample answer: Where it can give light to everyone.

  2. How can you be a light at home?

    Sample answer: By helping, being kind and not grumbling.

  3. What happened to the widow’s food?

    Sample answer: God did not let it run out.

5. Memory Verse:

Matthew 5:16 — “Let your light shine before men, that they may see your good deeds and praise your Father in heaven.”

John 8:12 — “Jesus said, “I am the light of the world.””

Craft 1: Paper Candle

Materials: A cardboard tube, yellow and orange paper, glue

Instructions:

  1. Cover the tube with white paper.

  2. Cut a yellow and orange flame.

  3. Glue the flame on top and write: “Shine!”

Lesson Point: We shine for Jesus when we do good things.

Craft 2: Shining Star Card

Materials: Yellow card, foil or glitter paper, glue

Instructions:

  1. Cut a big star from yellow card.

  2. Decorate it with foil or glitter paper.

  3. Write one kind thing you can do this week.

Lesson Point: Our good deeds show people the way to God.

Game 1: Hide and Show the Light

How to Play:

  1. Switch off the lights or close the curtains a little.

  2. Hide a torch under a box and ask who can see.

  3. Then put it on a table and see how it lights the room.

Lesson Point: A light is useful only when it is not hidden.

Game 2: Pass the Light

How to Play:

  1. Sit in a circle with one torch.

  2. Pass it on while saying one kind thing to do at home.

  3. The last child switches it on and everyone says the memory verse.

Lesson Point: We pass Jesus’ light to others by what we do.
