Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 6: Jesus Teacher and Healer

Aim

To teach the children that Jesus does not want anyone to be lost.

Scripture Reading

Matthew 18:10-14; Luke 15:17; John 10:11

Memory Verse

John 10:11

“I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep.”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

What happens when you lose something? You look for it. You ask others to help you look. You are not very happy. And when you find it, you tell everyone. Give an example, such as losing the key to your bicycle.

Main Points of the Lesson

This is a beautiful story. Use Luke’s account and tell it in your own words.

1. There was a shepherd who looked after 100 sheep. Describe what he did for them, how well he cared for them.

2. One day, one of the sheep went missing. The shepherd looked for it everywhere.

3. Describe how he found it (Lk 15:5). He carefully carried the sheep home.

Show Picture 8.

4. The shepherd was so happy that he called his friends to celebrate!

Instil the Memory Verse

Our Response to the Story

Explain that Jesus is the good shepherd, who is willing to give his life for his sheep. He saved us by dying for us on the cross. He rejoices like the shepherd in the story, when we are safe in his arms instead of running away.

Lesson - 8: THE LOST SHEEP

Scripture Reading: Matthew 18:10-14; Luke 15:1-7; John 10:11

1. Put the Story in Order

a) One sheep went missing.
b) He called his friends to celebrate.
c) The shepherd had 100 sheep.
d) The shepherd found it and carried it home.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. The shepherd had 100 sheep.

  2. One sheep went missing.

  3. The shepherd found it and carried it home.

  4. He called his friends to celebrate.

2. Count and Write

Write the answer:

  1. The shepherd had ____ sheep at first. __________ 👉 100

  2. He went to look for ____ sheep. __________ 👉 one

  3. He left ____ sheep safe in the field. __________ 👉 99

3. Circle the Right Words

Circle the right word:

  1. Jesus is the good (shepherd / farmer). 👉 shepherd

  2. He is happy when the lost one is (found / forgotten). 👉 found

  3. Jesus gave His life for His (sheep / house). 👉 sheep

4. Discussion Questions

  1. How did the shepherd feel when he found the sheep?

    Sample answer: Very happy; he celebrated.

  2. Who is the good shepherd?

    Sample answer: Jesus.

  3. How does Jesus show He loves us?

    Sample answer: He died on the cross for us.

5. Memory Verse:

John 10:11 — “I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep.”

Luke 19:10 — “For the Son of Man came to seek and to save the lost.”

Craft 1: Cotton Wool Sheep

Materials: Card, cotton wool, black paper, glue

Instructions:

  1. Draw a sheep body on card.

  2. Glue cotton wool over it.

  3. Add a black paper face and legs.

Lesson Point: Jesus cares for each of His sheep.

Craft 2: Shepherd’s Staff

Materials: A long strip of brown card, tape, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Roll the brown card into a long tube.

  2. Bend the top into a hook and tape it.

  3. Colour it and write: “Jesus is my shepherd.”

Lesson Point: Jesus guides and protects us.

Game 1: Find the Lost Sheep

How to Play:

  1. Hide a toy or paper sheep in the room.

  2. One child, the shepherd, looks for it while the class says “warm” or “cold”.

  3. Celebrate when it is found.

Lesson Point: Jesus searches for us and rejoices when we are found.

Game 2: Shepherd and Sheep

How to Play:

  1. One child is the shepherd and the others are sheep.

  2. The shepherd says “Come!” and the sheep follow him in a line.

  3. Count the sheep to make sure none is lost.

Lesson Point: The good shepherd takes care of every sheep.
