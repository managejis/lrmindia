Jesus’ Power Over Evil

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 6: Jesus Teacher and Healer

Aim

To teach the children that Jesus has power over evil … and that he empowers his followers to overcome evil, too. In the Name and the power of Jesus, we can defeat evil.

Scripture Reading

Mark 9:14-29

Memory Verse

Mark 9:23

“Everything is possible for him who believes.”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Think of some things you have found very hard to do. Examples: finding money to pay for school fees, talking to your friends or family about the Lord Jesus. Give some examples from your personal experience, too.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Today’s story is about a man who had a very big problem. His son was very ill and no-one was able to cure him.

2. Describe the son’s illness. It must have been heartbreaking and very frightening to see a boy controlled by an evil spirit (9:17-18a).

3. The father first went to Jesus’ disciples for help. They were not able to do anything (9:18b).

4. When the boy came near Jesus the evil spirit recognised him as the Son of God and treated the boy very roughly (9:20).

Jesus’ questions showed his concern for the boy. The father did not have much hope that even Jesus could help. Still, he believed that if anyone could help them, Jesus was the One (9:21-22). Show Picture 24. Jesus said to him, “Everything is possible for him who believes” (9:23).

5. Immediately, the father reacted by asking Jesus to help his son. The crowd came running to see what would happen. Jesus healed the boy when everyone thought the child was dead (9:24-27).

6. Afterwards Jesus’ disciples asked him why they couldn’t succeed in healing the boy. Jesus told them they needed to learn to use the power of prayer (9:28-29).

Our Response to the Story

Jesus has defeated all evil. When we pray he gives us the power to do this too. Today’s memory verse contains a wonderful promise. However, it doesn’t mean we may pray irresponsibly or selfishly. The power of prayer must be used in accordance with God’s will (1 Jn 5:14).

Lesson - 24: JESUS’ POWER OVER EVIL

Scripture Reading: Mark 9:14-29

1. Who Am I?

Write the answer:

  1. I asked Jesus to help my sick son. Who am I? __________ 👉 The boy’s father

  2. We could not heal the boy and asked Jesus why. Who are we? __________ 👉 The disciples

  3. I have power over all evil. Who am I? __________ 👉 Jesus

2. Choose the Right Answer

Circle a), b) or c):

  1. Who first tried to help the boy?
    a) The disciples b) Peter c) The king 👉 a) The disciples

  2. What did Jesus say is possible?
    a) Nothing b) Everything for one who believes c) Only small things 👉 b) Everything for one who believes

  3. What did Jesus say the disciples needed?
    a) Money b) Prayer c) Food 👉 b) Prayer

3. Believe or Doubt? B/D

Write B for believe or D for doubt:

  1. I ask Jesus for help because He is strong. ( ___ ) 👉 B

  2. I think my prayers do not matter. ( ___ ) 👉 D

  3. I trust that Jesus can do anything. ( ___ ) 👉 B

4. Discussion Questions

  1. What did the father say to Jesus?

    Sample answer: “I do believe; help me overcome my unbelief!”

  2. Why could Jesus heal the boy?

    Sample answer: Because He has power over all evil.

  3. How can we get God’s help?

    Sample answer: By praying and believing in Jesus.

5. Memory Verse:

Mark 9:23 — “Everything is possible for one who believes.”

Philippians 4:13 — “I can do all this through him who gives me strength.”

Craft 1: Power Shield

Materials: Card, silver paper, crayons, glue

Instructions:

Materials: Card, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Fold a card in half.

  2. Draw praying hands on the front.

  3. Write inside: “Everything is possible.”

Game 1: Believe It!

How to Play:

  1. The teacher says a true statement about Jesus or a false one.

  2. Children stand up if true and sit if false.

  3. Praise those who answer correctly.

Lesson Point: We believe Jesus’ promises.

Game 2: Help Me Believe

How to Play:

  1. Sit in a circle.

  2. Each child completes: “Jesus, help me believe that ...”.

  3. End with a short prayer together.

Lesson Point: Jesus helps us when we ask Him in faith.
