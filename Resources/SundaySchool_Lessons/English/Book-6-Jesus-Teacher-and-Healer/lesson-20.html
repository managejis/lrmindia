A Dead Child Lives Again

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 6: Jesus Teacher and Healer

Aim

To teach the children that Jesus has power over life and death. Those who follow him need not fear death.

Scripture Reading

Mark 5:22-24, 35-43

Memory Verse

Mark 5:36

“Don’t be afraid; just believe.”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Has anyone of you been ill lately? What did it feel like? Did it make you feel frightened? When people are very ill they are sometimes very afraid. They are afraid of dying.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. A little girl was very ill. Her father, Jairus, was an important man, but all his wealth could not do anything for her. Then he heard that Jesus, who had healed so many people, was nearby. Jairus went out to find Jesus.

2. Jairus pleaded with Jesus to come to his house and help his daughter. Jairus was an important man, but he humbled himself before Jesus (Mk 5:22-23).

3. Jesus began to follow Jairus to his house, but on the way there he was delayed by all the people who wanted to see him (5:24). Before they reached Jairus’s house, the message came that his daughter had died (5:35).

4. Jesus told Jairus not to give up hope but to trust him (5:36).

5. Jesus went to Jairus’s house, where the funeral preparations had already begun. He told everyone to stop crying, since the little girl was not dead; she was only sleeping. Everyone laughed at Jesus in disbelief (5:38-40a).

6. Inside the house, with only the girl’s parents and Peter, James and John present, Jesus restored the little girl to life (5:37, 40-43). Show Picture 20.

Jesus has power over death because he is God. He can raise people from the dead.

Instil the Memory Verse

Our Response to the Story

Find out what the children are frightened of. It may be going to the clinic or hospital, or just being ill. Jesus told Jairus to believe in him and not be afraid. We, too, can put our trust in Jesus when we are afraid.

Lesson - 20: A DEAD CHILD LIVES AGAIN

Scripture Reading: Mark 5:22-24, 35-43

1. Match the Following

A B
Jairus Asked Jesus to help his daughter
The little girl Was raised to life
Peter, James and John Went in with Jesus
Jesus Said, “Don’t be afraid”

Answers:
Jairus → Asked Jesus to help his daughter
The little girl → Was raised to life
Peter, James and John → Went in with Jesus
Jesus → Said, “Don’t be afraid”

2. Circle the Right Word

Circle the word that is right:

  1. Jairus’ daughter was (twelve / one hundred) years old. 👉 twelve

  2. Jesus said, “Do not be (afraid / lazy); just believe.” 👉 afraid

  3. Jesus has power over (death / nothing). 👉 death

3. Yes or No

Write Yes or No:

  1. Was Jairus an important man? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

  2. Did Jesus say she was only sleeping? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

  3. Does Jesus care when we are afraid or ill? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

4. Discussion Questions

  1. What did Jairus do when his daughter was ill?

    Sample answer: He went to find Jesus and begged for help.

  2. What did Jesus tell Jairus?

    Sample answer: Do not be afraid, just believe.

  3. What makes you afraid, and what can you do?

    Sample answer: Answers will vary; we can trust Jesus and pray.

5. Memory Verse:

Mark 5:36 — “Don’t be afraid; just believe.”

John 11:25 — “Jesus said, “I am the resurrection and the life.””

Craft 1: Little Bed

Materials: A small box, cloth scraps, a paper doll

Instructions:

  1. Make a doll from paper.

  2. Make a small bed in the box with cloth.

  3. Lay the doll in it and write: “Get up!”

Lesson Point: Jesus has power over life and death.

Craft 2: Fear Away Card

Materials: Card, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Draw something that scares you on one half.

  2. Draw Jesus holding your hand on the other half.

  3. Write: “Don’t be afraid.”

Lesson Point: We can trust Jesus when we are afraid.

Game 1: Wake Up!

How to Play:

  1. Children pretend to sleep on the floor.

  2. The teacher says “Jesus says, wake up!” and they stand and cheer.

  3. Repeat with another “Jesus”.

Lesson Point: Jesus has power to give life.

Game 2: Believe Walk

How to Play:

  1. Place cards along a path with words like “Trust”, “Believe”, “Love”.

  2. Children walk along, saying the words on each card.

  3. End with the memory verse.

Lesson Point: Jesus asks us to trust Him and not fear.
