The Faith of a Foreigner

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 6: Jesus Teacher and Healer

Aim

To teach the children that Jesus loves people from all tribes and races. He wants us all to put our trust in him and be delivered from the power of evil.

Scripture Reading

Mark 7:25-30

Memory Verse

John 10:16

“I have other sheep that are not of this sheep pen … They too will listen to my voice …”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

What happens when someone from another tribe comes and lives in the compound next to yours? They speak a different language, they eat different food or prepare it differently, they have different customs. We often mistrust them because we don’t know or understand them.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. A foreign woman once came to see Jesus. She was not a Jew. The Jews called everyone who did not belong to their nation a Gentile.

The woman was very worried, as her daughter was possessed by an evil spirit. She pleaded with Jesus to help her (Mk 7:25-26).

2. Jesus answered her request with a story. Read 7:27 to the class. What Jesus meant was that he had come to help the Jews (the children in the story). What was theirs could not be given to others (the dogs in the story; Jews often called people from other nations dogs, because they looked down on them).

If he helped foreigners before helping the Jews, Jesus was saying, it would be like feeding the dogs before the children!

3. But the woman would not be put off that easily. “Yes, Lord,” she replied, “but even the dogs under the table eat the children’s crumbs” (7:28).

Show Picture 21. Notice the dog in the picture? The man on the left probably is the owner of the house where Jesus was staying (7:24).

4. Jesus rewarded this foreign woman’s faith and trust in him. He told her she would find her little daughter healed when she arrived back home (7:29-30).

Jesus loves people from all tribes and nations. He wants everybody in the whole world to have faith in him and be delivered from the power of evil.

Instil the Memory Verse

Our Response to the Story

We know Jesus loves people from all the tribes in the world. Therefore, how should we treat people from another tribe who come to live near us? Help the class think of different ways that will help foreigners be part of the community. When we share Jesus’ love with others, we are doing God’s work.

Lesson - 21: THE FAITH OF A FOREIGNER

Scripture Reading: Mark 7:25-30

1. Who Am I?

Write the answer:

  1. I was not a Jew but I begged Jesus to help my daughter. Who am I? __________ 👉 The foreign woman

  2. I was ill because of an evil spirit. Who am I? __________ 👉 The woman’s daughter

  3. I rewarded the woman’s faith. Who am I? __________ 👉 Jesus

2. Kind or Unkind? K/U

Write K for kind or U for unkind:

  1. I invite the new child from another tribe to play. ( ___ ) 👉 K

  2. I laugh at how a new child speaks. ( ___ ) 👉 U

  3. I share my food with a neighbour. ( ___ ) 👉 K

3. Complete the Sentences

Word box: crumbs, all, faith

  1. Jesus loves people from ____________ tribes. __________ 👉 all

  2. The woman said even dogs eat the ____________. __________ 👉 crumbs

  3. Jesus praised her ____________. __________ 👉 faith

4. Discussion Questions

  1. How did the woman show her faith?

    Sample answer: She kept asking Jesus and trusted Him.

  2. How does Jesus feel about all tribes?

    Sample answer: He loves them all.

  3. How can you welcome a newcomer?

    Sample answer: By greeting him and playing together.

5. Memory Verse:

John 10:16 — “I have other sheep that are not of this sheep pen ... They too will listen to my voice.”

John 3:16 — “God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son.”

Craft 1: Friends Around the World

Materials: Paper, crayons, glue

Instructions:

  1. Draw children with different clothes holding hands.

  2. Colour them in many colours.

  3. Write: “Jesus loves all.”

Lesson Point: Jesus loves people from every tribe and nation.

Craft 2: Welcome Card

Materials: Card, crayons, stickers

Instructions:

  1. Fold a card in half.

  2. Draw a smiling face on the front.

  3. Inside write: “Welcome! Jesus loves you.”

Lesson Point: We welcome others as Jesus does.

Game 1: Greetings Round

How to Play:

  1. Children greet each other in different languages or ways.

  2. Say “Welcome!” to each new friend.

  3. Talk about how it feels to be welcomed.

Lesson Point: We should treat everyone with kindness.

Game 2: Crumbs Under the Table

How to Play:

  1. Scatter small paper “crumbs” under a table.

  2. Children collect them in a basket.

  3. Explain that even small crumbs from Jesus are a blessing.

Lesson Point: Jesus’ love is big enough for everyone.
