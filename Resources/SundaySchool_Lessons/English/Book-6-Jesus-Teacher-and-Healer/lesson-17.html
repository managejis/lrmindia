A Shrivelled Hand and Hard Hearts

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 6: Jesus Teacher and Healer

Aim

To teach the children that our attitude towards other people shows what we think of God.

Scripture Reading

Mark 3:1-6

Memory Verse

Matthew 25:40

“[W]hatever you did for one of the least of these brothers of mine, you did for me.”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Before the class, arrange with one of the children to assist you by acting in a little play. (You could also use two of the children.) Act a scene in a house where the child is asked to help with some work. The child replies, “Oh no, I can’t do it now. I have to get ready for church choir practice. I’m too busy to help you.”

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Jesus was very busy. People followed him everywhere, wanting his help. However, Jesus never used that as an excuse not to go to the synagogue on the Sabbath to worship God. Explain that the people of the Jewish faith worship God on Saturday, not Sunday, and they call it the Sabbath day.

2. The religious leaders also went to the synagogue on the Sabbath. They watched Jesus closely. Would he obey all the religious laws? (3:1-2). God had set only 10 commandments, but there were over 300 of these rules they had made! It made the Sabbath a difficult day, because there were so many rules to keep in mind. Absolutely no work was to be done on the Sabbath; you were not even allowed to cook food. It was so important to the religious leaders to obey these laws that they had forgotten that loving and serving God is the most important rule of all.

3. A man with a shrivelled hand also came to the synagogue that day. Jesus knew the religious leaders would think healing a person was work, so you could not do it on the Sabbath. Therefore, Jesus asked them, “Is the Sabbath day to be spent doing good things for people or bad?” (3:3-4).

4. The religious leaders’ hearts were hard, not kind. They cared more about their rules than about people’s suffering, and they would not change. Jesus healed the man there and then (3:5). Show Picture 17.

Those leaders were very religious, but they did not want to know how to worship God the way he wanted them to. Jesus showed and taught us God’s will. The way we treat other people shows how we love God, because it is as if we are doing it to God himself.

Instil the Memory Verse

Our Response to the Story

Act the introductory play again, but this time let the child who is asked to help agree to do the work. He or she may either spend less time getting ready for choir practice and do the job before leaving for church, or agree to do it at another time.

Lesson - 17: A SHRIVELLED HAND AND HARD HEARTS

Scripture Reading: Mark 3:1-6

1. Kind or Hard Heart? K/H

Write K for kind or H for hard heart:

  1. Helping someone who needs help right now. ( ___ ) 👉 K

  2. Saying, “I am too busy to help.” ( ___ ) 👉 H

  3. Caring more about rules than about people. ( ___ ) 👉 H

  4. Sharing my lunch with a hungry friend. ( ___ ) 👉 K

2. Choose the Right Answer

Circle a), b) or c):

  1. What was wrong with the man’s hand?
    a) It was shrivelled b) It was lost c) It was bandaged 👉 a) It was shrivelled

  2. On which day did Jesus heal him?
    a) Sunday b) The Sabbath c) Monday 👉 b) The Sabbath

  3. What did the leaders care about more?
    a) People b) Their rules c) The man 👉 b) Their rules

3. Complete the Sentences

Word box: love, hand, others

  1. Jesus healed the man’s ____________. __________ 👉 hand

  2. Loving God means we also love ____________. __________ 👉 others

  3. The most important rule is to ____________ God. __________ 👉 love

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why were the leaders watching Jesus?

    Sample answer: They wanted to see if He would break their rules.

  2. Is it right to do good on the Sabbath?

    Sample answer: Yes; God wants us to do good every day.

  3. How do we show love to God?

    Sample answer: By how we treat other people.

5. Memory Verse:

Matthew 25:40 — “Whatever you did for one of the least of these brothers and sisters of mine, you did for me.”

Matthew 22:39 — “Love your neighbor as yourself.”

Craft 1: Healing Hand

Materials: Paper, scissors, crayons, glue

Instructions:

  1. Draw a drooping hand on one paper.

  2. Draw a strong open hand on another.

  3. Glue them side by side with the title: “Before and After.”

Lesson Point: Jesus’ kindness is more important than any rule.

Craft 2: Helping Hands Chart

Materials: Card, crayons, stickers

Instructions:

  1. Draw a big hand in the middle of the card.

  2. Write five ways to help at home on the fingers.

  3. Add a sticker each time you help.

Lesson Point: We love God when we help others.

Game 1: I Can Help!

How to Play:

  1. One child acts being asked to help.

  2. The child says “Yes, I can help!”.

  3. Others clap for a cheerful helper.

Lesson Point: We should not use excuses to avoid helping.

Game 2: Open Hands

How to Play:

  1. Children make tight fists, then open their hands slowly.

  2. Each says one way to help someone this week.

  3. Finish with a short prayer.

Lesson Point: Kind hands do good on every day.
