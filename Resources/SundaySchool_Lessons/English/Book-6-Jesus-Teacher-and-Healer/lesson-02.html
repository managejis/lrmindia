Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 6: Jesus Teacher and Healer

Aim

To show the children that we need to listen to and obey Jesus’ words.

Scripture Reading

Matthew 7:24-27; Luke 6:46-49.

Memory Verse

Matthew 7:24

“[E]veryone who hears these words of mine and puts them into practice is like a wise man who built his house on the rock.”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Have you ever seen a house being built? How do the builders choose the right place for the house? Do you know what is a good foundation?

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Jesus talked to the people about building houses. Perhaps when he was teaching them this, they saw some houses being built.

Show Picture 1. Jesus told a story about two men who each decided to build a house.

2. Describe how one man built his house with a very good foundation (Matt 7:24). It will be easier for you to tell the story and for the children to follow if you give him a name, eg Mr Negiit.

3. Describe where the other man – let’s call him Mr Kaslaan – built his house (7:26). He did not choose a good place, now did he?

4. Describe the storm that came (7:25, 27). These houses were square, not round, so the wind hit them very hard. Show Picture 2 and talk about it. Get the children to tell you what happened by asking them questions. For example: What happened to the house at the bottom of the picture?

5. Jesus said that people who listen to him and obey him are like Mr Negiit, the wise builder. Those who don’t listen and don’t obey are like Mr Kaslaan, the foolish builder. Replace the builders’ names with those you have chosen.

Instil the Memory Verse

Our Response to the Story

Kani was eight years old. His parents had told him not to go to the river without his elder brother. One Sunday he and his friend decided to go to the river, as it was so hot. Their parents thought they had gone to Sunday school. The water looked so cool and inviting that they decided to go swimming. It was lovely. After a little while the friend looked around to see where Kani was. Kani had disappeared! He was never seen again. He had drowned.

This is a true story. We learn to obey Jesus first of all by listening to and obeying our parents’ instructions. At Sunday school we learn more about Jesus, and also how to obey him as we grow to know more about him.

Lesson - 2: THE TWO HOUSES

Scripture Reading: Matthew 7:24-27; Luke 6:46-49

1. Match the Following

A B
Wise builder Built on the rock
Foolish builder Built on the sand
The rain and wind Came to both houses
Jesus’ words Listen and do them

Answers:
Wise builder → Built on the rock
Foolish builder → Built on the sand
The rain and wind → Came to both houses
Jesus’ words → Listen and do them

2. Circle the Right Word

Circle the word that is right:

  1. The wise man built his house on the (rock / sand). 👉 rock

  2. The house on the sand (stood / fell) down. 👉 fell

  3. A wise person (hears and obeys / only hears) Jesus’ words. 👉 hears and obeys

3. Obey or Not Obey? O/N

Write O for obey or N for not obey:

  1. Mum says, “Come home now,” and I come at once. ( ___ ) 👉 O

  2. I go to the river when I was told not to. ( ___ ) 👉 N

  3. I listen to my Sunday school teacher. ( ___ ) 👉 O

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did one house stand and the other fall?

    Sample answer: One had a strong foundation of rock; the other was built on sand.

  2. What does it mean to build on Jesus’ words?

    Sample answer: To listen to Him and do what He says.

  3. Who are the first people we should obey?

    Sample answer: Our parents and carers.

5. Memory Verse:

Matthew 7:24 — “Everyone who hears these words of mine and puts them into practice is like a wise man who built his house on the rock.”

James 1:22 — “Do not merely listen to the word, and so deceive yourselves. Do what it says.”

Craft 1: Two Houses

Materials: Card, brown and yellow paper, blue crayon, glue

Instructions:

  1. Fold a card in half.

  2. On one side glue a house on a grey rock.

  3. On the other side glue a house on yellow sand and draw rain.

Lesson Point: Obeying Jesus makes our lives strong like the house on the rock.

Craft 2: Rock Paperweight

Materials: A smooth stone, paints or crayons

Instructions:

  1. Wash and dry a small stone.

  2. Paint or colour it.

  3. Write on it: “Jesus is my rock.”

Lesson Point: We build our lives on Jesus.

Game 1: Wise Builders

How to Play:

  1. Give each team some building blocks or paper cups.

  2. Teams build a tower on a flat strong “rock” (a book) and another on a soft cushion.

  3. Gently blow on both and see which one stands.

Lesson Point: A strong foundation keeps us safe.

Game 2: Listen and Do

How to Play:

  1. The teacher gives three small instructions in a row.

  2. Children must listen and do them in the right order.

  3. Clap for those who did everything.

Lesson Point: Wise people listen and then obey.
