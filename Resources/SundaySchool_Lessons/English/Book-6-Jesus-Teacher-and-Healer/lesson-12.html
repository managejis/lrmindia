Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 6: Jesus Teacher and Healer

Aim

To teach the children how we can be ready for Jesus’ return.

Scripture Reading

Matthew 25:14-30

Memory Verse

Matthew 25:21

“Well done, good and faithful servant! You have been faithful … Come and share your master’s happiness!”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Ask the children what they are good at and what they most like to do. Give them time to respond. Tell them what you like to do.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. One day, Jesus told a story about a man who gave his servants something to do before he left on a journey. Tell the events in 25:14-15 in your own words, how the money was shared out. In those days, there was no paper money, only coins. A talent was a coin that was worth a fair sum.

2. Tell how the first servant used the money that was entrusted to him and made twice as much (25:16). Perhaps he began a small business in his neighbourhood.

3. Repeat this with the second servant (25:17).

4. The third servant buried the money. He did not put it to good use (25:18).

5. The master returned after a long time. He was pleased with the first two servants and rewarded them (25:19-23).

6. The third servant returned the same amount he had been given and gave his excuses for not putting it to use (25:24-25). Show Picture 12. The master was angry with the lazy servant who did nothing with what he had been given. That servant lost the little he had to start with, too (25:26-28).

Instil the Memory Verse

Our Response to the Story

Explain that Jesus is like the master in the story and we are the servants. While he has gone back to heaven he has left us work to do and abilities to do it with. We do not receive money as in the parable. Every one of us has gifts or talents God has given us. We must put these talents to good use.

Let’s think of the things you mentioned at the beginning of the lesson when I asked you what you’re good at. Name some of the things to refresh the class’s memories. How can you use these talents for the glory of the Lord Jesus?

Examples Singing: We can sing songs about Jesus to teach others about him.

Telling stories: We can tell our family and friends the stories we hear about Jesus in Sunday school.

We do not need to wait until we are adults to work for Jesus. We can start now and keep up the good work until he comes again!

Lesson - 12: THE TALENTS

Scripture Reading: Matthew 25:14-30

1. Count and Write

Write the answer:

  1. The master gave five talents to the first servant, who made ____ more. __________ 👉 5

  2. The second servant was given ____ talents. __________ 👉 two

  3. The third servant was given ____ talent. __________ 👉 one

2. Choose the Right Answer

Circle a), b) or c):

  1. What did the third servant do with his talent?
    a) Used it well b) Buried it c) Lost it 👉 b) Buried it

  2. What did the master say to the good servants?
    a) “Well done!” b) “Go away!” c) “Be quiet!” 👉 a) “Well done!”

  3. What are our talents?
    a) Gifts God gives us b) Coins c) Houses 👉 a) Gifts God gives us

3. Use It or Hide It? U/H

Write U for use it or H for hide it:

  1. I sing songs about Jesus. ( ___ ) 👉 U

  2. I am good at drawing but never share it. ( ___ ) 👉 H

  3. I tell Bible stories to my friends. ( ___ ) 👉 U

4. Discussion Questions

  1. What is something you are good at?

    Sample answer: Answers will vary, such as singing or drawing.

  2. How can you use it for Jesus?

    Sample answer: By helping others and telling them about Him.

  3. Why was the master unhappy with the third servant?

    Sample answer: He did nothing with what he was given.

5. Memory Verse:

Matthew 25:21 — “Well done, good and faithful servant! You have been faithful with a few things ... Come and share your master’s happiness!”

Colossians 3:23 — “Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord.”

Craft 1: Talent Coins

Materials: Gold paper, scissors, string, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Cut out gold paper circles.

  2. On each one write or draw a talent you have.

  3. String them together as a chain.

Lesson Point: God gives each of us gifts to use.

Craft 2: My Talent Badge

Materials: Card, safety pin or tape, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Cut a round badge from card.

  2. Draw what you are good at.

  3. Write: “I will use it for Jesus.”

Lesson Point: We should use our gifts for God.

Game 1: Show Your Talent

How to Play:

  1. Each child shows or tells one thing they are good at.

  2. The class cheers and says one way to use it for Jesus.

  3. Everyone gets a turn.

Lesson Point: Every child has a talent from God.

Game 2: Dig It Up

How to Play:

  1. Hide paper coins under cups.

  2. Children find them and say how they would use the gift.

  3. Do not leave any coin hidden!

Lesson Point: We should use our talents, not bury them.
