Jesus is Baptised

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 6: Jesus Teacher and Healer

Aim

To teach the children that Jesus came to this world because it was God’s will. Jesus truly is the Son of God.

Scripture Reading

Mark 1:1-11; Luke 3:10-14

Memory Verse

Mark 1:11

And a voice came from heaven: “You are my Son, whom I love; with you I am well pleased.”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

How can we find out what is happening in our town or in the world? Possible answers: the radio, a newspaper, the drums, a messenger. In the past, in some countries there were people whose job it was to be messengers for the ruler. They had to go and spread the news in every town.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. God sent a certain person to be his special messenger, saying that God was going to send his only Son to this world (1:2). This messenger’s name was John the Baptist.

2. Tell the children where John lived and how he lived (1:4, 6).

3. Tell them what John did (1:5, 7). Now you know why he was called John the Baptist! He told people they had to change their evil ways (Mk 1:4; Lk 3:10-14). They had to prepare themselves for the coming of the Son of God (Mk 1:5, 6-7).

Explain how the people prepared themselves: They asked God to forgive them.

They were baptised.

Baptism is a sign of repentance, which means that you are sorry for the sins you have committed and that you are turning away from sin.

4. One day Jesus came to John. He asked John to baptise him. But Jesus was perfect. He had no sin. He did not need to repent. Therefore, he did not need to be baptised. However, Jesus wanted to be one with the people. He chose to be treated like one of us sinners. Show Picture 13 and tell what happened (1:9-11). God spoke. He said and he showed that Jesus truly is his Son.

Instil the Memory Verse

Our Response to the Story

After his baptism Jesus performed many miracles. We are going to hear about some of them in the coming weeks’ lessons.

Jesus performed these miracles to show people that he was the Son of God. He still is the Son of God. When we follow or obey Jesus, the Son of God, we too become part of God’s family: sons and daughters of our Heavenly Father.

Lesson - 13: JESUS IS BAPTISED

Scripture Reading: Mark 1:1-11; Luke 3:10-14

1. Who Am I?

Write the answer:

  1. I was God’s special messenger who prepared the way for Jesus. Who am I? __________ 👉 John the Baptist

  2. I was baptised in the Jordan, though I had no sin. Who am I? __________ 👉 Jesus

  3. I spoke from heaven: “You are my Son.” Who am I? __________ 👉 God the Father

2. Put the Story in Order

a) God said, “You are my Son.”
b) John told people to change their ways.
c) Jesus came to John to be baptised.
d) People asked God to forgive them and were baptised.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. John told people to change their ways.

  2. People asked God to forgive them and were baptised.

  3. Jesus came to John to be baptised.

  4. God said, “You are my Son.”

3. Complete the Sentences

Word box: sorry, Son, messenger

  1. John was God’s ____________. __________ 👉 messenger

  2. Baptism shows we are ____________ for our sins. __________ 👉 sorry

  3. Jesus is God’s ____________. __________ 👉 Son

4. Discussion Questions

  1. What did John tell the people to do?

    Sample answer: To turn away from sin and be sorry.

  2. Why was Jesus baptised?

    Sample answer: To be one with us, even though He had no sin.

  3. What did God say about Jesus?

    Sample answer: That He loves Him and is pleased with Him.

5. Memory Verse:

Mark 1:11 — “And a voice came from heaven: “You are my Son, whom I love; with you I am well pleased.””

Galatians 3:26 — “In Christ Jesus you are all children of God through faith.”

Craft 1: Dove and Cloud

Materials: White paper, cotton wool, blue card, glue

Instructions:

  1. Glue cotton wool clouds at the top of a blue card.

  2. Cut out a white dove and glue it below.

  3. Write: “You are my Son.”

Lesson Point: God showed that Jesus is His Son.

Craft 2: River Jordan

Materials: Blue and brown paper, glue, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Glue a wavy blue river across a sheet.

  2. Add brown banks on each side.

  3. Draw two small people standing in the water.

Lesson Point: Jesus was baptised in the Jordan River.

Game 1: Messenger Run

How to Play:

  1. Whisper a short message to the first child.

  2. Children pass it down the line, then the last says it aloud.

  3. Say that John passed on God’s message.

Lesson Point: John was God’s messenger.

Game 2: Say Sorry Prayer

How to Play:

  1. Sit quietly in a circle.

  2. The teacher leads a short prayer asking God’s forgiveness.

  3. Children say “Amen” together.

Lesson Point: God forgives when we are truly sorry.
