Jesus Walks on the Water

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 7: Jesus Lord and Saviour

Aim

To teach the children how Jesus showed his disciples that he has the power of God.

Scripture Reading

John 6:16-21

Memory Verse

No new memory verse

Review the passage so far.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Talk to the class about powerful machines people use to do their work. Perhaps they have seen road graders, large cargo planes taking off or tanks at work. These machines have so much power they can be quite frightening to watch! Unless you are a mechanic or an engineer you don’t really understand where all the power comes from. You just see what the power can do.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Jesus is the source of all power, because he is God himself. While he lived on earth he limited his divine power. At certain times he used special acts of power to teach the people something about God.

2. After Jesus showed his divine power by feeding the 5 000 with only two fish and five loaves, he told his disciples to go home while he went away by himself to speak to God the Father in prayer. The disciples got on their boat and set out on the lake. They were still waiting for Jesus to come back to them when a storm erupted (6:16-18).

3. Describe how terrified the disciples were when they saw Jesus walking towards them on the water. Show Picture 7. Jesus’ miraculous power frightened them. They did not understand it (6:19).

4. Jesus’ words immediately comforted them. They took him into the boat and at once they were out of the storm and at their destination (6:20-21)!

Our Response to the Story

The disciples saw Jesus’ awesome power and became afraid, but his words took away their fear and so they were brought to safety. Jesus has the power to change our lives and take away our fear, even though he does not always take away the danger we find ourselves in. A Zairian pastor who is nearly 90 years old and who has lived through many dangers like war, famine and imprisonment, often says, “The Lord is my helper.” He knows it is true, for he has experienced it himself.

Lesson - 7: JESUS WALKS ON THE WATER

Scripture Reading: John 6:16-21

1. Put the Story in Order

a) Jesus said, “Do not be afraid.”
b) Jesus went up the hill to pray.
c) A storm came and they saw Jesus on the water.
d) The disciples sailed across the lake.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. Jesus went up the hill to pray.

  2. The disciples sailed across the lake.

  3. A storm came and they saw Jesus on the water.

  4. Jesus said, “Do not be afraid.”

2. Complete the Sentences

Word box: storm, afraid, boat

  1. The disciples were in a ____________. __________ 👉 boat

  2. A strong ____________ blew on the lake. __________ 👉 storm

  3. Jesus said, “Do not be ____________.” __________ 👉 afraid

3. Brave or Scared? B/S

Write B if we trust Jesus or S if we feel scared:

  1. I remember that Jesus is with me in the dark. ( ___ ) 👉 B

  2. I think Jesus has forgotten me. ( ___ ) 👉 S

  3. I pray when I am afraid. ( ___ ) 👉 B

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why were the disciples afraid?

    Sample answer: There was a storm, and they saw Jesus walking on the water.

  2. What did Jesus say to them?

    Sample answer: “It is I; don’t be afraid.”

  3. What can you do when you feel afraid?

    Sample answer: Pray and remember that Jesus is with us.

5. Memory Verse:

John 1:1-2 — “In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God. He was with God in the beginning.”

John 6:20 — “It is I; don’t be afraid.”

Craft 1: Boat on the Waves

Materials: Paper, blue crayon, card, glue

Instructions:

  1. Fold a small paper boat.

  2. Colour wavy blue waves on card.

  3. Glue the boat on the waves.

  4. Write: “Jesus is with me.”

Lesson Point: Jesus is with us in every storm.

Craft 2: Calm Sea Bottle

Materials: Small bottle, water, blue colour, a little glitter

Instructions:

  1. Fill the bottle with water.

  2. Add blue colour and glitter.

  3. Close the lid and shake, then watch it settle.

Lesson Point: Jesus has power to bring calm.

Game 1: Stormy Sea

How to Play:

  1. Children sit in a “boat” made with a rope or tape.

  2. Teacher says, “The storm is coming!” and they sway gently.

  3. When the teacher says “Jesus is here!” all sit still and calm.

Lesson Point: Jesus’ words take our fear away.

Game 2: Do Not Be Afraid

How to Play:

  1. Name something scary (dark, thunder, dogs).

  2. After each one the class calls out, “Jesus is with me!”

  3. Finish with a short prayer.

Lesson Point: We can trust Jesus when we are afraid.
