Jesus Speaks to Nicodemus

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 7: Jesus Lord and Saviour

Aim

To let the children see how Jesus showed by his teaching that he is the Son of God.

Scripture Reading

John 3:1-17; Numbers 21:4-9

Memory Verse

John 1:2

[The Word] was with God in the beginning.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Suppose you wanted to visit someone but you didn’t want anyone else to see you. How would you do it? Give the children time to respond. You might cover yourself with a big coat or a towel, or choose to go out in the dark of night.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. One night an important Jewish religious leader named Nicodemus visited Jesus. He came secretly because he didn’t want the other religious leaders to know what he was doing. They hated Jesus, but Nicodemus had questions he wanted to ask. Because of what Jesus said and did Nicodemus realised that Jesus had come from God. However, Jesus was not the kind of person the Jews expected God’s messenger to be like (3:1-2).

2. Jesus told Nicodemus that in order to enter into the kingdom of God he needed to be born again. Naturally Nicodemus thought that was impossible! Show Picture 3. Here in the corner you can see a mother and child; this picture represents physical birth (3:3-4).

3. Jesus explained that he was talking about a spiritual birth. Everyone who receives eternal life has repented of their sin, which has been washed away. God then gives the gift of his Spirit to that person, so that he or she is born of the Spirit.That is“the second birth” (3:5-7). Just as we cannot control the movement of the wind, we have no control over our salvation; it is a gift from God (3:8).

4. Nicodemus found all of this very hard to understand, even though he was an important religious teacher! Jesus said, “If you can’t believe what I am telling you about present events how will you ever believe what I tell you about heaven, where no-one on earth has ever been except me?” (3:9-13).

5. Jesus was the best teacher ever. He knew an explanation works best if one uses an illustration – a word picture. The story about the snakes Jesus refers to here is told in Book 2, Lesson 20. It would be useful to show the accompanying picture and briefly review the story in Numbers 21:4-9. Jesus was going to be lifted up on a cross, as the bronze snake was lifted up in the desert. Accepting Jesus is the only way to salvation, as looking at the bronze snake was the only way to survive a snake bite in the desert (3:14-17).

Our Response to the Story

Jesus asked Nicodemus to put his trust in him. Jesus asks us, too, to put our trust in him and accept his gift of salvation.

Read John 3:16 aloud. Or, if the class has already learnt it as a memory verse, let the children say it.

Lesson - 3: JESUS SPEAKS TO NICODEMUS

Scripture Reading: John 3:1-17; Numbers 21:4-9

1. True or False?

Write T for true or F for false:

  1. Nicodemus came to Jesus at night. ( ___ ) 👉 T

  2. Nicodemus was a farmer. ( ___ ) 👉 F

  3. Jesus said we must be born again. ( ___ ) 👉 T

2. Who Am I?

Choose from: Nicodemus, Jesus, the wind

  1. I am a religious leader with many questions. Who am I? __________ 👉 Nicodemus

  2. You cannot see me, but you can hear me blow. Who am I? __________ 👉 the wind

  3. I will be lifted up so that people may have life. Who am I? __________ 👉 Jesus

3. Missing Letters

Fill in the missing letters:

  1. B _ R N A G _ I N (a new birth) __________ 👉 BORN AGAIN

  2. E T E R N _ L L I _ E (a gift from God) __________ 👉 ETERNAL LIFE

  3. S _ V I O U R (Jesus is our ...) __________ 👉 SAVIOUR

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did Nicodemus come at night?

    Sample answer: He did not want the other leaders to see him.

  2. What does being born again mean?

    Sample answer: God gives us new life by his Spirit when we trust Jesus.

  3. Who can trust Jesus and have eternal life?

    Sample answer: Everyone who believes in him.

5. Memory Verse:

John 1:2 — “[The Word] was with God in the beginning.”

John 3:36 — “Whoever believes in the Son has eternal life.”

Craft 1: Snake on a Pole

Materials: Brown paper strip, a stick or straw, glue, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Colour a long paper strip like a snake.

  2. Twist it round the stick.

  3. Write: “Look and live.”

Lesson Point: Jesus was lifted up so that whoever trusts him can live for ever.

Craft 2: Wind Spinner

Materials: Square paper, scissors, pin, pencil with eraser

Instructions:

  1. Cut a paper square from the corners to the middle.

  2. Fold every other corner to the centre.

  3. Pin it to the pencil and blow on it.

Lesson Point: We cannot see the wind, but we see what it does; God’s Spirit works like that.

Game 1: Night Visitor

How to Play:

  1. One child tiptoes to the teacher’s desk as “Nicodemus”.

  2. The teacher asks, “Why did you come?”

  3. The child asks a question about Jesus; the class helps with the answer.

Lesson Point: Jesus welcomes everyone who comes with questions.

Game 2: Wind Blows

How to Play:

  1. The teacher says, “The wind blows on everyone who …” (has a brother, likes rice, etc.).

  2. Those children swap seats.

  3. Say: we cannot see the wind but it is real, like God’s Spirit.

Lesson Point: God’s Spirit is real even though we cannot see him.
