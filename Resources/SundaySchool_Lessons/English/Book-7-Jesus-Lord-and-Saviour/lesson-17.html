The Beggar and the Rich Man

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 7: Jesus Lord and Saviour

Aim

To teach the children that the choices we make in this life determine our position in the next world.

Scripture Reading

Luke 16:19-31

Memory Verse

John 1:13

[C]hildren [of God are] born not of natural descent, nor of human decision or a husband’s will, but born of God.
NOTE: This is a long verse, but difficult to divide, so let the class spend two sessions learning it.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

What kind of life would you like to have if you were free to choose?

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Jesus told a story about another rich man who chose exactly how he wanted to live. Describe the kind of life he chose (Lk 16:19).

2. Lazarus was a very poor man who had to beg for a living at the entrance of the rich man’s property. He had a terrible life (16:20-21). Show Picture 17. The rich man cared nothing for poor Lazarus, although he had plenty to share.

3. When Lazarus died, everything changed for him. His life in the next world was wonderful; he was given a place of honour in heaven, at Abraham’s side. Remind the children who Abraham was. He was the father of the Jewish people (Gen 12:2) and the Bible calls him God’s friend (Jas 2:23). The rich man died too, and his circumstances changed just as dramatically. He found himself in hell, where he suffered terribly (16:22-23).

4. Describe how the rich man called out for help and the reply he received (16:23-26). In his life on earth he had been unwilling to help anyone and now no-one was able to help him. He was cut off from all kindness and care.

5. It was only then that the rich man began to think of other people. He desperately wanted to warn his brothers, who probably were living as carelessly as he had lived (16:27-28). Jesus wants us to learn from this story that God’s message is clear and available to all who will listen. Not even a messenger from the other side of the grave will make people who have closed their minds to God, listen (16:29-31).

Our Response to the Story

Parents and other grownups often warn children about dangerous things, for example: “Don’t touch that pot! It’s just come off the fire and is still very hot.” As a teacher you should be able to give many more examples. Sometimes we close our minds to their warnings and then suffer unnecessarily.

With this story Jesus wants to warn us that how we choose to live our lives in this world affects our position in the next world. If we fail to show love and mercy to God’s creatures, how can we expect love and mercy from our Creator? Therefore, we should not only do good wherever we can; we should also never repay people for their cruelty or unkindness.

God longs to reward us, because he loves us and wants only what is good for us. Loving God and sharing what he has given us is the way he means for us to live. It’s the best choice to make.

Lesson - 17: THE BEGGAR AND THE RICH MAN

Scripture Reading: Luke 16:19-31

1. True or False?

Write T for true or F for false:

  1. Lazarus was a poor beggar. ( ___ ) 👉 T

  2. The rich man shared his food with Lazarus. ( ___ ) 👉 F

  3. Lazarus went to be with Abraham in heaven. ( ___ ) 👉 T

2. Match the Following

A B
The rich man Lived only for himself
Lazarus Was poor and sick
Abraham Was God’s friend
The brothers Needed to listen to God’s Word

Answers:
The rich man → Lived only for himself
Lazarus → Was poor and sick
Abraham → Was God’s friend
The brothers → Needed to listen to God’s Word

3. Kind or Unkind? K/U

Write K for kind or U for unkind:

  1. I help a poor neighbour. ( ___ ) 👉 K

  2. I ignore someone who is hungry. ( ___ ) 👉 U

  3. I share my lunch. ( ___ ) 👉 K

4. Discussion Questions

  1. How did the rich man treat Lazarus?

    Sample answer: He did not help him at all.

  2. What does this story warn us about?

    Sample answer: The way we live now matters, so we should be kind and listen to God.

  3. How can we help someone in need?

    Sample answer: Share food, clothes and friendship.

5. Memory Verse:

John 1:13 — “... children born not of natural descent, nor of human decision or a husband’s will, but born of God.”

1 John 3:18 — “Dear children, let us not love with words or tongue but with actions and in truth.”

Craft 1: Helping Hands

Materials: Paper, crayons, scissors

Instructions:

  1. Trace both your hands on paper.

  2. Cut them out.

  3. Write: “I will help others.”

Lesson Point: God wants us to love people in need.

Craft 2: Kindness Cup

Materials: Paper cup, strips of paper, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Decorate a cup.

  2. Write kind things to do on paper strips.

  3. Put the strips in the cup and pick one each day.

Lesson Point: Our choices show our love for God.

Game 1: Kind or Not?

How to Play:

  1. Teacher says an action; children stand for kind, sit for unkind.

  2. Discuss a few.

  3. Praise the kind choices.

Lesson Point: We should choose kindness every day.

Game 2: Give It Away

How to Play:

  1. Each child has a paper “coin”.

  2. They give it to someone who has none.

  3. Count until all have some.

Lesson Point: Sharing helps everyone.
