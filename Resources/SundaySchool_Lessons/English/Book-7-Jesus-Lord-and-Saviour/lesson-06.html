Jesus Feeds 5000 People

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 7: Jesus Lord and Saviour

Aim

To show the children that Jesus told people not to worry about food which doesn’t satisfy for ever – but to trust him for food that gives eternal satisfaction and everlasting life.

Scripture Reading

John 6:1-13, 25-40

Memory Verse

John 1:5

The light shines in the darkness, but the darkness has not understood it.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

When many people come to the church or your house for a feast, preparations have to start days ahead. Firewood has to be gathered, water collected, cooking pots sorted out and, of course, food collected.

Main points of the Lesson

1. Many people were talking about Jesus. Stories of his healing miracles and his teaching were being passed on, so that many wanted to see him for themselves. One day a huge crowd came to listen to Jesus. There were 5 000 men and many women and children (6:1-3).

2. No preparations had been made to feed all those people. Jesus asked his disciples for ideas on how to take care of them (6:5b-6).

3. Philip worked out that even with an enormous amount of money they would be unable to buy a little for everyone. Then Andrew found a boy who had brought along some food, but of course it was only enough for himself (6:7-9).

4. Describe the instructions Jesus gave his disciples and what he did with the food which the boy was willing to share. Show Picture 6. In the end everyone had enough to eat (6:10-13)!

5. Jesus then quickly left the people. He was sad because he knew they only wanted to see his miracles for selfish reasons. They had just eaten without making any preparations, but they did not understand what that told them about Jesus (6:25-26).

Our Response to the Story

Jesus wanted the people to understand he had not come to hand out physical food, but to satisfy them with spiritual food. God wanted the people to trust in Jesus, the Son whom the Father had sent (6:27-29). Feeding on Jesus, who is the bread of life, means having a friend for life who is always there with help and support, no matter what (6:35, 40).

Jesus said these words first of all to the people who could hear him speak during his life on earth, but he is also speaking to us. He is the bread of life to everybody who trusts him.

Lesson - 6: JESUS FEEDS 5000 PEOPLE

Scripture Reading: John 6:1-13, 25-40

1. Count and Write

Write the number:

  1. The boy had ____ loaves of bread. __________ 👉 5

  2. The boy also had ____ small fish. __________ 👉 2

  3. There were about ____ men in the crowd. __________ 👉 5000

2. Who Am I?

Choose from: Andrew, the boy, Jesus

  1. I shared my lunch. Who am I? __________ 👉 the boy

  2. I found the boy with the food. Who am I? __________ 👉 Andrew

  3. I am the bread of life. Who am I? __________ 👉 Jesus

3. True or False?

Write T for true or F for false:

  1. Jesus thanked God for the food. ( ___ ) 👉 T

  2. Everyone was still hungry at the end. ( ___ ) 👉 F

  3. There were baskets of leftovers. ( ___ ) 👉 T

4. Discussion Questions

  1. What did the boy do with his small lunch?

    Sample answer: He shared it, and Jesus used it to feed everyone.

  2. What did Jesus mean when he said, “I am the bread of life”?

    Sample answer: He gives us what we need most: a friend and life for ever.

  3. What small thing can you share this week?

    Sample answer: Food, a toy, a smile or a prayer.

5. Memory Verse:

John 1:5 — “The light shines in the darkness, but the darkness has not understood it.”

John 6:35 — “I am the bread of life. He who comes to me will never go hungry.”

Craft 1: Loaves and Fishes Basket

Materials: Paper plate, brown and blue paper, glue

Instructions:

  1. Fold the plate to make a basket.

  2. Cut five little loaves and two fish.

  3. Glue them inside the basket.

Lesson Point: Jesus can do much with what we share.

Craft 2: Twelve Baskets Strip

Materials: Paper strips, crayons, glue

Instructions:

  1. Draw 12 little baskets on a long strip.

  2. Colour them.

  3. Write: “Jesus gives enough.”

Lesson Point: Jesus gives more than enough.

Game 1: Share the Lunch

How to Play:

  1. Give some children two crackers or paper “fish”.

  2. They share with a friend until everyone has a piece.

  3. Say: “Jesus fed everyone!”

Lesson Point: Sharing makes a little go a long way.

Game 2: Collect the Leftovers

How to Play:

  1. Scatter paper pieces around the room.

  2. Children collect them into baskets (cups or boxes).

  3. Count how many baskets are filled.

Lesson Point: Jesus gives so much that there is some left over.
