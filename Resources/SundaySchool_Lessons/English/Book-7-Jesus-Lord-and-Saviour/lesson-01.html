Jesus is Born in Bethlehem

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 7: Jesus Lord and Saviour

Aim

To show the children that it was God the Father’s plan to send the Son, Jesus Christ, to this world so that he could show us what God really is like.

Scripture Reading

John 1:1-14; Luke 2:1-20

Memory Verse

John 1:1

In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God …
NOTE: During this set of lessons the class will learn by heart John 1:1-14, a passage that testifies that Jesus is the Son of God. The lessons are taken from the Gospels of Luke and John.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Talk about making plans. You have to plan your journey. It’s no good travelling in the rainy season, for you get stuck in the mud. You have to plan the route, or the journey may take longer than necessary. Everything works much better when we plan ahead, otherwise things are disorganised.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. God the Father is a master planner. He planned the whole world and all of creation! He also keeps the world in existence.

2. When the time was right God chose to send the Son, Jesus Christ, into this world as a human baby. People so easily forget God, that our Heavenly Father decided to send the Son among us so that he could show us what God really is like.

3. Jesus was born in a stable in the land of Israel about 2 000 years ago. His mother, Mary, was a virgin (she had never slept with a man). This means that Jesus’ father was the one true God. That is why Jesus also is called “the Son of God”.

4. Tell the story from Luke 2:1-7: how Mary and Joseph travelled to Bethlehem, where Jesus was born.

5. The angels told the shepherds that their Saviour was born. He was the Son of God, Christ the Lord. Explain to the children that Christ means “Saviour”. God’s heavenly choir of angels celebrated the event (2:8-14).

6. The shepherds hurried to Bethlehem. Show Picture 1. They told everybody they met what they had seen. The people were amazed (2:15-20).

7. However, Jesus was alive before he was born as a human baby into this world. He was with God before the beginning of time, and at the creation. This is what today’s memory verse tells us.

Our Response to the Story

People in Africa often have several names. These are given for special reasons. Jesus, too, had more than one name and every one had a special meaning. It was the will of God the Father that Jesus would come into this world to save us from sin and death. This is what the name “Saviour” says about Jesus. By his words and actions he showed us what God is like – so that we may love and obey the Father, just as the Son does.

Instil the Memory Verse

Point out that another name is used here for Jesus: the Word. The Bible calls Jesus “the Word” because he carries out the Father’s plans and brings the Father’s message to his people.

Lesson - 1: JESUS IS BORN IN BETHLEHEM

Scripture Reading: John 1:1-14; Luke 2:1-20

1. Put the Story in Order

a) Angels told the shepherds the good news.
b) Mary and Joseph travelled to Bethlehem.
c) The shepherds hurried to see the baby.
d) Jesus was born in a stable.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. Mary and Joseph travelled to Bethlehem.

  2. Jesus was born in a stable.

  3. Angels told the shepherds the good news.

  4. The shepherds hurried to see the baby.

2. Who Am I?

Choose from: Mary, an angel, a shepherd

  1. I said, “A Saviour is born!” Who am I? __________ 👉 an angel

  2. I am the mother of baby Jesus. Who am I? __________ 👉 Mary

  3. I looked after sheep and hurried to Bethlehem. Who am I? __________ 👉 a shepherd

3. Yes or No?

Write Y for yes or N for no:

  1. Did God plan to send Jesus? ( ___ ) 👉 Y

  2. Was Jesus born in a big palace? ( ___ ) 👉 N

  3. Is Jesus the Son of God? ( ___ ) 👉 Y

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did God send Jesus to the world?

    Sample answer: To save us and to show us what God is really like.

  2. Where was Jesus born?

    Sample answer: In a stable in Bethlehem.

  3. How can we tell the good news like the shepherds did?

    Sample answer: By telling friends and family that Jesus is our Saviour.

5. Memory Verse:

John 1:1 — “In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God ...”

Luke 2:11 — “Today in the town of David a Saviour has been born to you; he is Christ the Lord.”

Craft 1: Star of Bethlehem

Materials: Yellow card, scissors, glue, string, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Cut a big star from yellow card.

  2. Decorate it with crayons.

  3. Write: “Jesus is born!”

  4. Tie a string on top to hang it up.

Lesson Point: God planned to send Jesus, our Saviour, at just the right time.

Craft 2: Stable Scene

Materials: Brown paper, small card, crayons, glue

Instructions:

  1. Fold brown paper into a little stable.

  2. Draw Mary, Joseph and baby Jesus on card.

  3. Stand them inside the stable.

Lesson Point: Jesus came as a little baby to be near us.

Game 1: Shepherds Hurry

How to Play:

  1. Mark a start line and a “Bethlehem” line.

  2. On “Go!” the shepherds walk quickly (no running) to Bethlehem.

  3. At the line, each child whispers: “Jesus is born!”

Lesson Point: The shepherds hurried to see Jesus and told everyone.

Game 2: Pass the News

How to Play:

  1. Sit in a circle.

  2. Whisper “Jesus is our Saviour” to your neighbour.

  3. The last child says it out loud.

Lesson Point: God’s good news is for everyone to share.
