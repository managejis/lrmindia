Mary and Jesus at the Tomb

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 7: Jesus Lord and Saviour

Aim

To show the children that death could not hold the Son of the living God.

Scripture Reading

John 20:1-2, 10-18

Memory Verse

John 1:9

The true light that gives light to every man was coming into the world.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

If you have ever seen something very surprising, you will remember how at first you could not believe it was real. Give an example of this, such as someone unexpectedly returning to the community after a long absence.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Mary Magdalene had such a great surprise. When she went to Jesus’ tomb on the third day after he was crucified some amazing things happened. When she set out that morning, she probably was very sad remembering all that had happened. Show Picture 10. Her first surprise was when she saw that the large stone in front of the entrance to Jesus’ tomb had been rolled away. She was upset and ran to tell Simon Peter (20:1-2).

2. While Peter and John went into the tomb to investigate, Mary stood outside crying. When they had left she looked inside. Show Picture 11 and describe what she saw and heard (20:3, 10-13). Use a piece of paper to cover up the figure of Jesus (on the left in the picture) when you show it to the class.

3. Mary was weeping so much that she couldn’t see clearly when a man appeared beside her outside the tomb. She thought he had to be the gardener and that he might know where the body of Jesus had been put (20:14-15). Tell what happened when Jesus said Mary’s name (20:16). Remove the paper from Picture 11 as you tell this part of the story.

4. For the second time that morning Mary had a huge surprise. Only this time it filled her with joy! Jesus told her to go and tell the disciples that he was alive. Mary was so delighted that she ran to them with the wonderful news (20:17-18).

Our Response to the Story

Mary knew Jesus when he said her name. Jesus knows the name of everyone who belongs to him. Are you as delighted as Mary was that Jesus is alive? He overcame death because he had triumphed over sin and evil. He rose from the grave to give us everlasting life.

Lesson - 11: MARY AND JESUS AT THE TOMB

Scripture Reading: John 20:1-2, 10-18

1. Put the Story in Order

a) She thought Jesus was the gardener.
b) Mary went to the tomb early in the morning.
c) Jesus said her name and she knew him.
d) She saw that the stone was rolled away.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. Mary went to the tomb early in the morning.

  2. She saw that the stone was rolled away.

  3. She thought Jesus was the gardener.

  4. Jesus said her name and she knew him.

2. Who Am I?

Choose from: Mary Magdalene, Peter, Jesus

  1. I wept outside the empty tomb. Who am I? __________ 👉 Mary Magdalene

  2. I ran to the tomb to look inside. Who am I? __________ 👉 Peter

  3. I called Mary by her name. Who am I? __________ 👉 Jesus

3. Choose the Right Answer

Circle a), b) or c):

  1. What did Mary tell the disciples?
    a) “I have seen the Lord!” b) “I am lost.” c) “I am hungry.” 👉 a) “I have seen the Lord!”

  2. Why was Mary so happy?
    a) She found money b) Jesus was alive c) It was sunny 👉 b) Jesus was alive

4. Discussion Questions

  1. How did Mary know it was Jesus?

    Sample answer: He said her name.

  2. What does Jesus’ rising teach us?

    Sample answer: Jesus is stronger than death and is alive.

  3. Who did Mary go to tell?

    Sample answer: The disciples.

5. Memory Verse:

John 1:9 — “The true light that gives light to every man was coming into the world.”

Luke 24:6 — “He is not here; he has risen!”

Craft 1: Empty Tomb Door

Materials: Grey card, brown paper circle, tape

Instructions:

  1. Cut a doorway in the card.

  2. Tape a paper circle as the stone beside it.

  3. Roll the stone away and write: “He is alive!”

Lesson Point: Jesus rose from the grave.

Craft 2: Easter Garden Card

Materials: Folded paper, green crayon, paper flowers

Instructions:

  1. Draw a garden on the front.

  2. Glue paper flowers on it.

  3. Write inside: “Jesus is alive!”

Lesson Point: We rejoice because Jesus is alive today.

Game 1: Say My Name

How to Play:

  1. One child stands in the middle with eyes closed.

  2. The teacher points to a child who says, “Hello, (name).”

  3. The middle child guesses whose voice it is.

Lesson Point: Jesus knows each of us by name.

Game 2: Walk and Tell

How to Play:

  1. Place a card that says “Jesus is alive” at one side.

  2. Each child walks quickly (no running) to pick it up and tell a friend.

  3. Everyone says: “He is alive!”

Lesson Point: Mary told the good news; so should we.
