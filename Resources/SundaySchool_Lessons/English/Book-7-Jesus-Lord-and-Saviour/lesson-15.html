The Lost Son Comes Home

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 7: Jesus Lord and Saviour

Aim

To show the children how Jesus taught that God loves sinners and rejoices when we repent and turn to him.

Scripture Reading

Luke 15:11-32

Memory Verse

John 1:12a

Yet [there were some] … who received him, … those who believed in his name …

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Talk about what it feels like to be hungry. Most of the children probably will be only too familiar with this feeling.

Show Picture 14 and review Lesson 14, reminding the class why the young man in the picture was hungry. He realised he had done wrong and decided to go back to his father and beg for forgiveness (Lk 15:17-20). That is a hard thing to do!

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Describe how the young man walked many kilometres back to his home country. While he was still a long way from the family home his father, who must have looked out for him every day, ran to meet him and greeted him warmly. The son didn’t even have time to say his whole prepared speech of how he knew he did not deserve to be received back in his father’s house (15:20-21). Show Picture 15.

2. It was a very different welcome from what that young man deserved. Describe how the father welcomed him (15:22-24).

3. However, his older brother was very angry at all the celebrating. Describe what he said to his father (15:28-30).

4. The father explained that the older son was free to enjoy everything that belonged to him and that he could have a feast at any time. However, it is not every day that a person everyone thought was dead returns home (15:31-32)!

Our Response to the Story

A Sudanese family in Khartoum arranged a gathering to mourn the death of their mother, who they believed had been killed fleeing from the civil war. But the mourning turned into rejoicing when they received news that she was alive, safe and well in a neighbouring country!

Jesus told this story about the lost son to show us how God, who is the father in this story, loves repentant sinners. He desires to forgive them and welcomes them back into his arms with great rejoicing.

Jesus also told this story to teach the religious leaders something, for they were like the older brother in the story. They, who could enjoy God’s love and abundance all the time, needed to rejoice when a sinner turned from his or her bad ways. Instead, they reminded the person again and again how wicked he or she had been. God’s forgiveness and membership of his family is open to all who repent. Jesus Christ has paid for our sins.

Lesson - 15: THE LOST SON COMES HOME

Scripture Reading: Luke 15:11-32

1. Put the Story in Order

a) The father ran to meet him.
b) They had a big feast.
c) The son walked home hungry.
d) The father gave him a robe and a ring.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. The son walked home hungry.

  2. The father ran to meet him.

  3. The father gave him a robe and a ring.

  4. They had a big feast.

2. Choose the Right Answer

Circle a), b) or c):

  1. How did the father greet his son?
    a) He ran and hugged him b) He shut the door c) He was cross 👉 a) He ran and hugged him

  2. How did the older brother feel?
    a) Happy b) Angry c) Sleepy 👉 b) Angry

3. Complete the Sentences

Word box: forgives, home, lost

  1. The son was ____________ but now is found. __________ 👉 lost

  2. The father ____________ his son. __________ 👉 forgives

  3. The son came ____________. __________ 👉 home

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Who is the father in the story like?

    Sample answer: God our Father, who loves us.

  2. Why did the father celebrate?

    Sample answer: His lost son had come home.

  3. What should we do when someone says sorry?

    Sample answer: Forgive them and be glad, not angry.

5. Memory Verse:

John 1:12 — “Yet to all who received him, to those who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God ...”

Luke 15:24 — “For this son of mine was dead and is alive again; he was lost and is found.”

Craft 1: Robe and Ring

Materials: Cloth or paper, paper strip, glue

Instructions:

  1. Cut a small paper robe.

  2. Make a ring from a paper strip.

  3. Write: “Welcome home!”

Lesson Point: God welcomes sinners who come back.

Craft 2: Feast Plate

Materials: Paper plate, crayons, coloured paper

Instructions:

  1. Draw food on the plate.

  2. Add bread and fruit from coloured paper.

  3. Write: “Let us celebrate!”

Lesson Point: Heaven rejoices when someone turns to God.

Game 1: Welcome Home

How to Play:

  1. One child walks in from the back of the room.

  2. The class cheers and says “Welcome home!”

  3. Take turns.

Lesson Point: God runs to meet us when we come to him.

Game 2: Party Plans

How to Play:

  1. Children name foods for a welcome feast.

  2. The class claps for each idea.

  3. Finish with a thank-you prayer.

Lesson Point: God rejoices when someone comes home.
