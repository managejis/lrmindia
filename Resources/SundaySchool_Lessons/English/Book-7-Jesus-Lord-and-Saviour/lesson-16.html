What the Rich Fool Forgot

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 7: Jesus Lord and Saviour

Aim

To teach the children that there are more important things in life than possessions.

Scripture Reading

Luke 12:15-21

Memory Verse

John 1:12

Complete the memory verse from Lesson 15. Yet to all who received him, to those who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God …

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Nearly everyone wants to be rich! What would you like to own if you were rich?

Main Points of the Lesson

1. What most people forget is that great riches bring great responsibilities. Jesus told a story about a rich man. Listen and see if you think he remembered his responsibilities.

2. The rich man owned fertile ground that produced good crops. In fact, he soon ran out of storage space. Show Picture 16. He decided the solution was to build bigger barns (12:16-18).

3. The rich man did not think of sharing his wealth with any of the poor around him. He did not even realise that his blessings came from God. The only one he cared about and wanted to please was himself (12:19).

4. Money can’t buy everything, as the rich man found out in the end. God, who had given the rich man his fertile crops, took his life away from him. His possessions were of no use to him then. The rich man was a fool for forgetting God and living for the riches and pleasures of this world (12:20-21).

Our Response to the Story

Jesus wants us to understand that the things we own have to occupy second place in our lives. God gives us all we have and own, so he is to have first place. We should be concerned first of all with pleasing him.

This means that the lesson of this story is meant for all of us, not only people whom we think are rich. Poor people can also become so greedy or jealous that possessions become the most important thing in their lives. In the story God had no place in the rich man’s life or plans. It pleases God when we share what we have received from him. God asks us to trust him for what we need in this world, as he gives us the energy to work, and he will give us riches in heaven (12:15, 21).

Lesson - 16: WHAT THE RICH FOOL FORGOT

Scripture Reading: Luke 12:15-21

1. Circle the Right Words

Circle the right word:

  1. The rich man’s land gave a (big / small) harvest. 👉 big

  2. He decided to build (bigger / smaller) barns. 👉 bigger

  3. He forgot about (God / farming). 👉 God

2. Share or Keep? S/K

Write S if we share or K if we only keep:

  1. I give some of my food to a hungry friend. ( ___ ) 👉 S

  2. I hide my toys so no one can use them. ( ___ ) 👉 K

  3. I thank God for all I have. ( ___ ) 👉 S

3. Yes or No?

Write Y for yes or N for no:

  1. Did the rich man think of the poor? ( ___ ) 👉 N

  2. Does everything we have come from God? ( ___ ) 👉 Y

  3. Is God more important than things? ( ___ ) 👉 Y

4. Discussion Questions

  1. What did the rich man forget?

    Sample answer: God, and sharing with others.

  2. What matters most in life?

    Sample answer: Loving God and pleasing him, more than owning things.

  3. What can you share?

    Sample answer: Food, toys, time and help for others.

5. Memory Verse:

John 1:12 — “Yet to all who received him, to those who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God ...”

Matthew 6:33 — “Seek first his kingdom and his righteousness, and all these things will be given to you as well.”

Craft 1: Barn Picture

Materials: Red paper, yellow crayon, glue

Instructions:

  1. Cut a barn from red paper.

  2. Draw golden grain.

  3. Write: “God comes first.”

Lesson Point: Everything we have comes from God.

Craft 2: Sharing Bag

Materials: Paper bag, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Decorate the bag.

  2. Write: “I will share.”

  3. Put in a small gift for someone.

Lesson Point: It pleases God when we share.

Game 1: Fill the Barn

How to Play:

  1. Children put paper balls into a box labelled “barn”.

  2. Then they give some to a “neighbour” box.

  3. Talk about sharing.

Lesson Point: God wants us to share what we have.

Game 2: Share or Keep

How to Play:

  1. Hold up a picture of a toy or snack.

  2. Children say “Share!” and show a sharing action.

  3. Swap pictures.

Lesson Point: Sharing is better than keeping everything.
