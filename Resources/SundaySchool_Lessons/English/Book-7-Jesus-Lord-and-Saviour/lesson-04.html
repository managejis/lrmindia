The Government Official Kneels Before Jesus

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 7: Jesus Lord and Saviour

Aim

To show the children that people believed in Jesus when they saw how he healed a government official’s son.

Scripture Reading

John 4:46-53

Memory Verse

John 1:3

Through him all things were made; without him nothing was made that has been made.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Where do people go to find help if someone is ill? Give the class time to respond.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. An important man once came to Jesus. His son was about to die and he was desperate for help. He probably was a rich man as he was an official of the government, so he would have already tried other healers. He had heard about Jesus, and came a long way to find him (4:46-47).

2. Show Picture 4. The official asked Jesus to come to his house and heal his son. Jesus refused to go! He wanted to perform an even greater miracle than the man asked for. Jesus would show the official that he did not need to go to the boy in order to heal him (4:47-50a).

3. That important official believed it when Jesus said his son would live. So he went off home. When he was still some way from the house his servants met him with good news (4:50b-51).

4. He found out that his son had started to recover at exactly the moment when Jesus had said, “Your son will live.” That made the official and all his family believe that Jesus was the Son of God (4:52-53).

Our Response to the Story

In 1987 a fatal illness broke out in the Akot area in the south of the Sudan. There were no doctors or medical help of any kind available, so the disease never was identified. Hundreds died. But no sick person who was prayed for by Christians died, nor were there any graves in the Christian compounds. These healings were such a witness to the power of God that the church grew amazingly.

Lesson - 4: THE GOVERNMENT OFFICIAL KNEELS BEFORE JESUS

Scripture Reading: John 4:46-53

1. Choose the Right Answer

Circle a), b) or c):

  1. Who came to Jesus for help?
    a) A government official b) A fisherman c) A soldier 👉 a) A government official

  2. Who was very ill?
    a) His wife b) His son c) His servant 👉 b) His son

2. Put the Story in Order

a) Jesus said, “Your son will live.”
b) His servants met him with good news.
c) The official asked Jesus for help.
d) The official believed and went home.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. The official asked Jesus for help.

  2. Jesus said, “Your son will live.”

  3. The official believed and went home.

  4. His servants met him with good news.

3. Circle the Right Word

Circle the right word:

  1. The official (believed / doubted) Jesus’ words. 👉 believed

  2. The boy got (better / worse) at that very hour. 👉 better

  3. The whole family (believed / forgot) in Jesus. 👉 believed

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Where do you go for help when someone is sick?

    Sample answer: Many people go to a clinic, and we can also pray to Jesus.

  2. Why did Jesus not need to go to the boy’s house?

    Sample answer: Jesus has power to heal from far away.

  3. What happened to the official’s family?

    Sample answer: They all believed that Jesus is the Son of God.

5. Memory Verse:

John 1:3 — “Through him all things were made; without him nothing was made that has been made.”

John 4:50 — ““You may go,” Jesus replied. “Your son will live.””

Craft 1: Get Well Card

Materials: Folded paper, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Draw a heart on the front.

  2. Write inside: “Jesus can help you.”

  3. Give it to someone who is sick.

Lesson Point: We can pray for sick people because Jesus has power to heal.

Craft 2: Walking Home Picture

Materials: Paper, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Draw a long road to a house.

  2. Draw the official walking home.

  3. Write: “Your son will live.”

Lesson Point: The official believed Jesus’ word and went home trusting him.

Game 1: Messenger Walk

How to Play:

  1. Mark a start and a finish line.

  2. One child walks fast (no running) to give a “message” card to a friend.

  3. The friend reads it: “Your son will live!”

Lesson Point: The servants brought good news that Jesus’ word was true.

Game 2: Pray for a Friend

How to Play:

  1. Sit in a circle.

  2. Each child says the name of someone who needs help.

  3. The class says together: “Jesus, please help them.”

Lesson Point: We can ask Jesus to help the people we love.
