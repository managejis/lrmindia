The Sick Man at the Pool

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 7: Jesus Lord and Saviour

Aim

To show the children that Jesus, the Son of God, is equal to God the Father. He can exercise divine power.

Scripture Reading

John 5:1-18

Memory Verse

John 1:4

In [the Word] … was life, and that life was the light of men.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Have you ever tried to do something that proved to be so difficult that you had to try several times? Perhaps you almost gave up? Use a specific example, such as young children who often find it difficult to tie their shoelaces.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Jesus met a man who had almost given up. Describe how the man had been ill for 30 years and why he had almost given up hope that he would ever get well (5:2-5). Show Picture 6. Jesus challenged him with a question (5:6).

2. The man told Jesus he really did want to get well, but he had no friends to help him. Jesus answered by commanding the sick man, who could not walk, to get up. And he did (5:7-9a)!

3. However, some of the religious leaders were angry with Jesus because he had healed the man on the Sabbath, the day when the Jews stopped all work to worship God. Those angry Jewish leaders could not find Jesus, so they stopped and questioned the man who had been healed. He really did not know who had healed him (5:9b-13).

4. Later Jesus did meet him again. Then the healed man realised his whole life needed to change now that he had met the Son of God. The man was at the temple when Jesus found him again; perhaps he was thanking God for having been healed (5:14).

Our Response to the Story

The Jewish leaders continued to be very angry with Jesus because they could not accept that there is no difference between the works of the Father and those of Jesus, the Son (5:16-18).

God is sovereign. He always knows when the time is right for something to happen. There is no wrong timing in his plans.

Let us praise God the Father for sending the Son, Jesus Christ, into the world when the time was just right. Refer to Lesson 1 about God’s planning, if you have time.

Perhaps there is some desire that you believe God has planted in your heart, but that has not been fulfilled yet. Don’t give up; God will exercise his power according to his perfect timing.

Lesson - 5: THE SICK MAN AT THE POOL

Scripture Reading: John 5:1-18

1. Count and Write

Write the number:

  1. Jesus healed ____ man by the pool. __________ 👉 1

  2. Jesus told him to get up, pick up his mat and ____. __________ 👉 walk

  3. Jesus and the Father are ____ God. __________ 👉 one

2. Match the Following

A B
The sick man Had no one to help him
Jesus Told him to get up
The leaders Were angry about the Sabbath
The mat He picked it up and walked

Answers:
The sick man → Had no one to help him
Jesus → Told him to get up
The leaders → Were angry about the Sabbath
The mat → He picked it up and walked

3. Trust or Give Up? T/G

Write T if we trust God’s timing or G if we give up:

  1. I wait for God and keep praying. ( ___ ) 👉 T

  2. I say, “God will never help me.” ( ___ ) 👉 G

  3. I know God’s time is always right. ( ___ ) 👉 T

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why do you think the man had almost given up?

    Sample answer: He was ill for a very long time and had no helper.

  2. What did the man do when Jesus spoke?

    Sample answer: He got up, picked up his mat and walked.

  3. What can we do when God’s answer seems slow?

    Sample answer: Keep trusting God, because his timing is perfect.

5. Memory Verse:

John 1:4 — “In him was life, and that life was the light of men.”

Ecclesiastes 3:11 — “He has made everything beautiful in its time.”

Craft 1: Rolled-up Mat

Materials: Paper strips, string, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Weave or glue paper strips to make a small mat.

  2. Roll it up and tie it with string.

  3. Write: “Jesus makes me well.”

Lesson Point: Jesus has power to heal and make us new.

Craft 2: Clock of God’s Time

Materials: Paper plate, card hands, pin

Instructions:

  1. Write the numbers on the plate.

  2. Fix two card hands in the middle.

  3. Write: “God’s time is best.”

Lesson Point: God always knows the right time.

Game 1: Pick Up Your Mat

How to Play:

  1. Place a small cloth on the floor for each child.

  2. On “Jesus says: Get up!” the children pick up the cloth and stand.

  3. On “sit” they sit again; praise those who listen best.

Lesson Point: Jesus’ words have power; we should obey him.

Game 2: Waiting Game

How to Play:

  1. Each child holds a small paper “seed”.

  2. Count slowly to ten with eyes closed before opening.

  3. Talk about how hard it is to wait and how God’s timing is best.

Lesson Point: Waiting for God is worth it.
