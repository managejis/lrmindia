Jesus Returns to Heaven

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Good News Sunday School Lessons

Pictures

20

Aim

To teach that Jesus went back to Heaven but will return to this earth one day.

Teacher Preparation

As you prepare this lesson thank God for Jesus' first coming and for the certain hope of His second coming. Then read and study Acts 1:1-11, Luke 24:50-51 and the lesson plan.

Memory Verse

Acts 1:11

This Jesus, who was taken from you into heaven, will come back in the same way that you saw him go to heaven.

Lesson Plan

Introduction

Talk about saying goodbye to someone.

When someone from the family goes to the store you know they will come back soon. You say goodbye quickly. When a member of the family leaves for a long holiday or moves somewhere else far away it is harder to say goodbye. You don't know when you will see them again.

The teacher could begin with 2 short acts of drama to show these different goodbyes. This needs to be prepared with a few children before Sunday School.

Main Points of Lesson

1. Jesus had to say goodbye to his friends and disciples. Put Luke 24:50 into your own words.

2. Jesus was returning to God the Father in Heaven. He told his disciples two things. He would return but the date is unknown Acts 1:7. God would send the gift of the Holy Spirit so that the disciples could continue the work of Jesus on earth. Acts 1:8.

3. Jesus was taken away from the earth v. 9. Show picture 20.

4. God's messengers talked to the disciples v.10

Review

Briefly go over the lesson emphasising that when Jesus returns He will come in majesty and power as King of kings and Lord of lords.

Teach Memory Verse

Our Response to the Lesson

When someone returns to the family after a long journey or when they have been away a long time they often come unexpectedly. Jesus is going to return. He always keeps his promises. Are we expecting his return? If we are following him, loving him and obeying him, we are ready for His return.

Sing a song about Jesus' return.

Lesson - 17: JESUS RETURNS TO HEAVEN

Scripture Reading: Acts 1:1-11; Luke 24:50-51

1. Yes or No

Write Yes or No:

  1. Did Jesus go back to the Father in heaven? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

  2. Did the angels say Jesus would return? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

  3. Do we know the day Jesus will come? ( ___ ) 👉 No

2. Complete the Sentences

Word box: cloud, Holy Spirit, return

  1. A ____________ took Jesus up. __________ 👉 cloud

  2. Jesus will ____________ one day. __________ 👉 return

  3. He promised to send the ____________. __________ 👉 Holy Spirit

3. Choose the Right Answer

Circle a), b) or c):

  1. Where did Jesus go?
    a) To heaven b) To Egypt c) To Rome 👉 a) To heaven

  2. How will Jesus come back?
    a) In the same way he left b) As a baby c) In a ship 👉 a) In the same way he left

4. Discussion Questions

  1. What did Jesus tell His disciples before going?

    Sample answer: He would return, and the Holy Spirit would help them.

  2. How do we feel when a loved one goes away?

    Sample answer: Sad, but we can wait for them to return.

  3. How can we be ready for Jesus?

    Sample answer: By loving and obeying Him.

5. Memory Verse:

Acts 1:11 — “This Jesus, who was taken from you into heaven, will come back in the same way that you saw him go to heaven.”

John 14:3 — “I will come back and take you to be with me.”

Craft 1: Cloud and Hands

Materials: Cotton wool, blue card, glue

Instructions:

  1. Colour the card blue.

  2. Glue cotton wool clouds.

  3. Add two hands reaching up.

  4. Write: “Jesus is coming back.”

Lesson Point: Jesus went up to heaven and will return.

Craft 2: Goodbye Card

Materials: Folded paper, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Draw Jesus and friends on the front.

  2. Inside write: “He will come again.”

Lesson Point: Jesus promised He would return.

Game 1: Goodbye and Welcome

How to Play:

  1. Two children act a short goodbye.

  2. Then act a happy welcome home.

  3. Talk about how we welcome Jesus.

Lesson Point: We wait with joy for Jesus.

Game 2: Look Up!

How to Play:

  1. Everyone looks up and says “Jesus will come back!”

  2. The teacher points to different spots.

  3. Repeat in a whisper.

Lesson Point: We watch for His return.
