Jesus Appears to His Disciples

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Good News Sunday School Lessons

Pictures

17, 19

Aim

To teach that believing Jesus rose from the dead brings joy. By his victory over death he saved us from sin.

Teacher Preparation

Pray for your students that they will not be doubters but believers. Then read and study John 30:19-29 and the lesson plan.

Memory Verse

John 20:20

The disciples were filled with joy when they saw the Lord.

Lesson Plan

Introduction

Tell the class about some surprising news you once had. It was so surprising that at first you didn't believe it. Try to make it an example of good news. For example, you gained high marks in an examination which you didn't expect to pass.

Main Points of Lesson

1. Jesus' friends were very sad or very frightened. Why? Show picture 17. Jesus had been crucified.

2. John 20:19-21. Describe how the disciples were together on the Sunday after Jesus was crucified on Friday. They were hiding. Suddenly Jesus was there with them. They couldn't believe it. Was it really him? Jesus showed them his hands, feet and side. They were so happy to see him.

3. Put v. 24-25 into your own words. Thomas was not with the other disciples and did not believe the news.

4. v. 26-27. A week later Jesus came again. Thomas was present. Show picture 19. Say Jesus' words and give Thomas' reply. Thomas saw Jesus and believed in him v. 28.

5. Jesus asked Thomas a question v. 29. He said as well: "How happy are those who believe without seeing me."

Review

Briefly go over the lesson and explain that Jesus is speaking to us now as he spoke to the disciples then. We cannot see Him with our eyes yet. We know He died but is now alive. It is by believing in Jesus that we are saved for everlasting life with God.

Teach Memory Verse

Our Response to the Lesson

The children should be encouraged to act out the story of Thomas. Do not doubt that Jesus is alive like Thomas did at first. Believe that Jesus is alive as he said. Thank him for dying for your sin on the cross. Ask him to be your Lord as Thomas did.

Lesson - 16: JESUS APPEARS TO HIS DISCIPLES

Scripture Reading: John 20:19-29

1. Who Am I?

Choose from: Thomas, Jesus, a disciple

  1. I would not believe until I saw. Who am I? __________ 👉 Thomas

  2. I showed my hands and my side. Who am I? __________ 👉 Jesus

  3. I hid in a room because I was afraid. Who am I? __________ 👉 a disciple

2. Circle the Right Word

Circle the right word:

  1. The disciples were filled with (joy / anger). 👉 joy

  2. Thomas said, “My Lord and my (God / friend)!” 👉 God

  3. Jesus came to them when the doors were (locked / open). 👉 locked

3. Put the Story in Order

a) Thomas heard the news and doubted.
b) Thomas believed.
c) Jesus showed them his hands.
d) Jesus came a week later.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. Jesus showed them his hands.

  2. Thomas heard the news and doubted.

  3. Jesus came a week later.

  4. Thomas believed.

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did the disciples hide?

    Sample answer: They were very afraid after Jesus died.

  2. What did Jesus say about those who believe without seeing?

    Sample answer: They are blessed.

  3. Have you ever doubted something good?

    Sample answer: Let the children share a surprise, then talk about believing Jesus.

5. Memory Verse:

John 20:20 — “The disciples were filled with joy when they saw the Lord.”

John 20:29 — “Blessed are those who have not seen and yet have believed.”

Craft 1: Hands Picture

Materials: Paper, red crayon, scissors

Instructions:

  1. Trace your hand.

  2. Mark a red dot in the palm.

  3. Write: “My Lord and my God.”

Lesson Point: Jesus showed Thomas his hands and Thomas believed.

Craft 2: Door Hanger

Materials: Card, string, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Cut a rectangle and a hole at the top.

  2. Draw a happy face.

  3. Write: “Peace be with you.”

Lesson Point: Jesus brings peace and joy.

Game 1: Doubting Thomas

How to Play:

  1. One child hides an object.

  2. Others guess what it is.

  3. When they see it they say “I believe!”

Lesson Point: Seeing helps us believe, but Jesus blesses those who believe without seeing.

Game 2: Joy Parade

How to Play:

  1. March around with a happy song.

  2. Stop and shout “Jesus is alive!”

  3. Repeat.

Lesson Point: We are filled with joy.
