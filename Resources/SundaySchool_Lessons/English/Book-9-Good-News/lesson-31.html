How to Worship God

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Good News Sunday School Lessons

Pictures

37

Aim

To teach how Christians worship God together in church.

Teacher Preparation

Pray for your students and their families that their hearts and minds might be open to the Word of God as you faithfully teach the children week by week. Then read and study Acts 2:43-47, 1 Corinthians 10:16-17, Hebrews 10:24-25, Psalm 122 and the lesson plan.

Memory Verse

Hebrews 10:25

Let us not give up the habit of meeting together.... let us encourage one another.

Lesson Plan

Introduction

Ask the class the question - "What do Christians do on a Sunday?" One answer could be that they go to church to worship God. It may be to a building or a place in the shade of a tree.

Main Points of Lesson

Show picture 37.

1. Here are some different people sitting together. Some are old, some are young.Some have dark skins, some have light skins. They are Christians. A group of Christians is called the church. God says in his word the Bible that is good for Christians to meet together to worship Him.

2. What do Christians do when they meet together in church to worship God?

Get the children to give you some answers like these:

  • sing to God

  • pray or talk to God.

  • listen to the Word of God, that is the Bible, being taught.

  • share testimony with each other, that is, how God has helped them in the past week

  • bring gifts for God's work. Tell the children what this money is used for.

3. Look at picture 37 again. What is the church doing in this picture? They are having a service of the Lord's Supper or Holy Communion. Christians remember what Jesus did at the last meal he had with his disciples before he died. He broke bread into pieces and gave it to his disciples to eat. This was like his body being broken when he was crucified. He gave his disciples wine to drink. This was to show his disciples that his blood was shed. Christians do this to remember Jesus died and to say thank you to him.

Review

Briefly go over the lesson emphasising that when we come together to worship God we have an opportunity to use the gifts God gives us and to learn from each other. It is a happy and special time of fellowship and sharing.

Our Response to the Lesson

When we come to church to worship God He is pleased and we encourage one another and learn more about Him.

Teach Memory Verse

When we come to Sunday School and church we are obeying Jesus and giving him joy. It will help us to follow and obey him.

Lesson - 31: HOW TO WORSHIP GOD

Scripture Reading: Acts 2:43-47; 1 Corinthians 10:16-17; Hebrews 10:24-25; Psalm 122

1. What Do We Do in Church?

Write S for sing, P for pray, L for listen to the Bible:

  1. We talk to God. ( ___ ) 👉 P

  2. We open the Bible and hear God’s Word. ( ___ ) 👉 L

  3. We praise God with songs. ( ___ ) 👉 S

2. Odd One Out

Circle the one that is NOT part of worship:

  1. singing – praying – quarrelling 👉 quarrelling

  2. sharing – giving – shouting at friends 👉 shouting at friends

  3. listening – thanking – sleeping 👉 sleeping

3. Choose the Right Answer

Circle a), b) or c):

  1. What do we remember at the Lord’s Supper?
    a) Jesus died for us b) Our holidays c) A game 👉 a) Jesus died for us

  2. What does the bread remind us of?
    a) Jesus’ body b) A cake c) Lunch 👉 a) Jesus’ body

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why do Christians meet together?

    Sample answer: To worship God and to encourage one another.

  2. What gifts do people bring?

    Sample answer: Money and help for God’s work.

  3. How do you feel when you worship with others?

    Sample answer: Happy and encouraged.

5. Memory Verse:

Hebrews 10:25 — “Let us not give up meeting together, as some are in the habit of doing, but let us encourage one another.”

Psalm 122:1 — “I rejoiced with those who said to me, “Let us go to the house of the Lord.””

Craft 1: Church Picture

Materials: Card, crayons, paper

Instructions:

  1. Draw a church or a big tree.

  2. Draw people of all ages.

  3. Write: “We worship God.”

Lesson Point: Christians of all kinds worship together.

Craft 2: Bread and Cup Card

Materials: Folded paper, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Draw bread and a cup.

  2. Colour them.

  3. Inside write: “Remember Jesus.”

Lesson Point: We remember Jesus when we share the Lord’s Supper.

Game 1: Church Mime

How to Play:

  1. Children act singing, praying and listening.

  2. The class guesses.

  3. Thank God for church.

Lesson Point: We worship in many ways.

Game 2: Invite a Friend

How to Play:

  1. Role play inviting a friend to church.

  2. Swap roles.

  3. Say: “Come with me.”

Lesson Point: We encourage others to worship.
