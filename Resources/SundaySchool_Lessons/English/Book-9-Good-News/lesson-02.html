God creates the first man and woman

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Good News Sunday School Lessons

Pictures

2, 3

Aim

To teach that God made men and women (and boys and girls).

Teacher Preparation

Ask God to help you prepare the lesson. Read and study Genesis 1:26-2:25 and the lesson plan. Prepare more questions to ask the children at the beginning of the lesson.

Memory Verse

Genesis 1:27

God created human beings, making them to be like himself. He created them male and female.

Lesson Plan

Introduction

This should take the form of questions which will review the previous lesson.

For example

Question: Who created the world?

Answer: God.

Question: What did God make in the world?

Answer: Light, trees, plants, animals, etc.

After the class have given some suggestions, picture 3 could be looked at again.

Question: There is something God made that we did not talk about in the last lesson. What is it?

Answer: Men and Women.

Main Points of Lesson

Tell the story of God's creation of men and women.

1. The first man Adam was made out of dust, God breathed life into him. Man was made in God's image. Adam is a name which means man. Genesis 1:27-28

2. Adam was put into a garden. Genesis 2:8-10.

3. God made Eve. Genesis 2:20-25

4. Men and women were to look after the garden. Genesis 2:15-20.

5. They were not to eat from the tree of knowledge of good and evil. Genesis 2:17

6. On Day 7 God rested. Creation was finished. When God spoke, at His Word creation took place. God speaks to us today through the Bible. The Bible is called the Word of God. Show Picture 2.

Review

Briefly go over the lesson emphasising that God's word is powerful and that he speaks to us today through His written word (the Bible) and through His living word (Jesus).

Teach Memory Verse

Our Response to the Lesson

Take a mirror to class. Hold it in turn in front of several children. Ask them who they see in the mirror. They will see a picture of themselves or their image. God made us in his own image. The mirror reflects our picture back to us. God made us to reflect his image. A mirror gets dirty and dusty. The story in God's Word, the Bible, is how the mirror of our lives has got dirty and how God can make us beautifully clean and shining for Him.

Lesson - 2: GOD CREATES THE FIRST MAN AND WOMAN

Scripture Reading: Genesis 1:26-2:25

1. Who Am I?

Choose from: Adam, Eve, God

  1. I made man from the dust of the ground. Who am I? __________ 👉 God

  2. I was the first man. Who am I? __________ 👉 Adam

  3. I was made to be Adam’s wife. Who am I? __________ 👉 Eve

2. Choose the Right Answer

Circle a), b) or c):

  1. What did God do to make Adam alive?
    a) He clapped b) He breathed into him c) He sang 👉 b) He breathed into him

  2. Where did God put Adam?
    a) In a garden b) On a boat c) In a cave 👉 a) In a garden

  3. On the seventh day God…
    a) rested b) built a house c) ran away 👉 a) rested

3. Odd One Out

Circle the one that is not a person:

  1. Adam – Eve – the garden 👉 the garden

  2. man – woman – tree 👉 tree

  3. boy – girl – river 👉 river

4. Discussion Questions

  1. In whose image did God make us?

    Sample answer: God made us like Himself.

  2. What was Adam’s job in the garden?

    Sample answer: To look after the garden.

  3. How does God speak to us today?

    Sample answer: Through His Word, the Bible.

5. Memory Verse:

Genesis 1:27 — “God created human beings, making them to be like himself. He created them male and female.”

Psalm 139:14 — “I praise you because I am fearfully and wonderfully made.”

Craft 1: Mirror Frame

Materials: Card, foil, glue, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Cut a card square for the frame.

  2. Glue foil in the middle as a mirror.

  3. Decorate the edges.

  4. Write: “God made me.”

Lesson Point: God made each of us like Himself.

Craft 2: Garden Scene

Materials: Paper, crayons, green and brown paper

Instructions:

  1. Colour a big garden on the paper.

  2. Glue on trees and flowers.

  3. Draw Adam and Eve in the garden.

  4. Write: “God’s garden.”

Lesson Point: God gave Adam and Eve a beautiful garden to look after.

Game 1: Name the Part

How to Play:

  1. Call out parts of the body.

  2. The children touch each part.

  3. Say together: “God made me!”

Lesson Point: God made every part of us.

Game 2: Garden Helpers

How to Play:

  1. Show actions: watering, digging, picking up.

  2. The children copy the actions.

  3. Say why we look after God’s garden.

Lesson Point: We should look after what God gave us.
