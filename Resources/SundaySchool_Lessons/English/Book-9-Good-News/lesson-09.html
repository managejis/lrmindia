A Sacrifice for Sin

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Good News Sunday School Lessons

Pictures

10, 11, 17

Aim

To teach that sacrifice takes away sin.

Teacher Preparation

Ask God to help each student understand that Jesus died on the cross for his/her sin. Then read and study Leviticus 6:1-7, Exodus 12:1-14 and the lesson plan.

Memory Verse

John 1:29

There is the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world.

Lesson Plan

Introduction

Make up a story of a boy or girl doing something wrong in their home and the punishment they had. When we do wrong, we suffer for it. We are punished.

Main Points of Lesson

1. Last week we heard about the commandments God gave his people. What were some of them? Show picture 10.

2. We often disobey God's commands. In the days when God first gave them, a person had to show how sorry he was when he disobeyed them by sacrificing an animal. Show picture 11. The animal was perfect. The man took it to the priest. He killed it. When we sin we should die because we have disobeyed God. The animal was killed instead of the man who sinned.

3. Moses led all God's people out of Egypt where they were slaves. Tell how Moses told the people to get ready a special meal. They were to put the blood from the sheep on the doorposts, Exodus 12:2-11.

4. God punished the Egyptians for their sin. He punished them with ten destructive plagues the last of which was death of the first born son. But if God saw blood on the door post he passed over the house, Ex. 12:12-13.

5. We don't sacrifice a sheep now (point to picture 11) because God sent his son Jesus to be the Lamb sacrificed for our sin. Show picture 17.

Review

Briefly go over the lesson to make sure the children understand that Jesus' perfect sacrifice on the cross means that we can become God's children and belong to His family. Jesus has saved us by His precious blood.

Teach Memory Verse

Our Response to the Lesson

God sent Jesus to die for my sin. Have the class repeat the memory verse quietly and have them substitute their own name instead of the world.

Lesson - 9: A SACRIFICE FOR SIN

Scripture Reading: Leviticus 6:1-7; Exodus 12:1-14

1. Complete the Sentences

Word box: lamb, blood, Jesus

  1. The animal sacrifice was a perfect ____________. __________ 👉 lamb

  2. The ____________ on the doorposts kept families safe. __________ 👉 blood

  3. ____________ is the Lamb of God. __________ 👉 Jesus

2. Put the Story in Order

a) God passed over their houses.
b) God’s people were slaves in Egypt.
c) Moses led them out of Egypt.
d) They put blood on their doorposts.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. God’s people were slaves in Egypt.

  2. They put blood on their doorposts.

  3. God passed over their houses.

  4. Moses led them out of Egypt.

3. True or False

Write T for true or F for false:

  1. An animal was a sacrifice for sin. ( ___ ) 👉 T

  2. We still have to sacrifice sheep today. ( ___ ) 👉 F

  3. Jesus died to take away our sin. ( ___ ) 👉 T

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why was an animal sacrificed in the old days?

    Sample answer: It died in the place of the person who sinned.

  2. Why do we not sacrifice animals now?

    Sample answer: Jesus was the perfect sacrifice for us.

  3. What does Passover remind us of?

    Sample answer: God saved His people and passed over the houses with blood.

5. Memory Verse:

John 1:29 — “There is the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world.”

1 John 1:7 — “The blood of Jesus, his Son, purifies us from all sin.”

Craft 1: Cotton Wool Lamb

Materials: Paper plate, cotton wool, black paper, glue

Instructions:

  1. Draw a lamb’s body on the plate.

  2. Glue on cotton wool.

  3. Add black paper face and legs.

  4. Write: “Lamb of God.”

Lesson Point: Jesus is our Lamb who takes away sin.

Craft 2: Red Door Card

Materials: Folded card, red crayon

Instructions:

  1. Draw a door on the front.

  2. Mark red along the top and sides.

  3. Inside write: “Jesus saves me.”

Lesson Point: Blood on the doorposts saved God’s people.

Game 1: Passover Doors

How to Play:

  1. Make “houses” with chairs.

  2. Children with a red paper strip are safe inside.

  3. Talk about how God kept them safe.

Lesson Point: God kept His people safe.

Game 2: Say Your Name

How to Play:

  1. Say John 1:29 together.

  2. Next time put your own name in place of “world”.

  3. Clap at the end.

Lesson Point: Jesus died for me.
