God’s Power, not Magic

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Good News Sunday School Lessons

Pictures

30

Aim

To teach that Christians do not need charms. God is more powerful than any charm or evil spirit.

Teacher Preparation

As you prepare this lesson renew your allegiance to the one true God as you worship Him and commit this lesson and your students to Him. Then read and study Acts 19:11- 20 and the lesson plan.

Memory Verse

Luke 10:27

Love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, with all your strength and with all your mind.

Lesson Plan

Introduction

The children will have seen charms being worn by people around the neck or wrist or else where, they may even wear them themselves. Ask the class (not a child who is wearing one) why people wear charms. The answer you want is that people believe they are a powerful protection.

Main Points of Lesson

1. Tell the following story. This is a story set in Africa.

There was no rain in Riwoto, Southern Sudan. The crops were dying. The elders came to see Pastor Peter - the missionary from Kenya. They asked him, "Please will you pray to God to send us rain?" Pastor Peter said he would but first they must take off all the charms they were wearing. If they prayed together with those on they were not trusting in God's power. The charms were taken off and with the Pastor they prayed for rain. That night it rained so hard that Pastor Peter could not leave his house the next day. It was a very powerful witness to people who did not know the power of God.

2. Show picture 30. Talk about what the men are doing. It is not easy to destroy charms etc. It is a big step of faith to trust in God's power. The men are obeying God's command.

Teach Memory Verse

God will bless us when we do this as he did the people at Riwoto.

3. Tell the story of Acts 19:18-20 in your own words. The people of Ephesus made a big fire of all the books of magic and charms. God blessed them because they showed they trusted his power. The church grew.

Review

Briefly go over the lesson emphasising that God's love and power are completely sufficient for our daily Christian living. No one else and nothing else has any power to help us.

Our Response to the Lesson

If possible, end this lesson with a story you know like the one the lesson begins with.

Lesson - 24: GOD’S POWER, NOT MAGIC

Scripture Reading: Acts 19:11-20

1. Yes or No

Write Yes or No:

  1. Is God more powerful than any charm? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

  2. Did the people at Riwoto trust charms for rain? ( ___ ) 👉 No (they took them off and prayed)

  3. Did the people in Ephesus burn their magic books? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

2. Choose the Right Answer

Circle a), b) or c):

  1. What did Pastor Peter ask the elders to do first?
    a) Take off their charms b) Dance c) Sleep 👉 a) Take off their charms

  2. What came to Riwoto after they prayed?
    a) Rain b) A storm of stones c) Nothing 👉 a) Rain

3. Complete the Sentences

Word box: trust, God, charms

  1. We do not need ____________. __________ 👉 charms

  2. We ____________ in God’s power. __________ 👉 trust

  3. ____________ is stronger than any evil spirit. __________ 👉 God

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why do some people wear charms?

    Sample answer: They think they give protection.

  2. Who really protects us?

    Sample answer: God, who is all-powerful.

  3. What happened when the Ephesians trusted God?

    Sample answer: The church grew.

5. Memory Verse:

Luke 10:27 — “Love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, with all your strength and with all your mind.”

Proverbs 3:5 — “Trust in the Lord with all your heart.”

Craft 1: Trust Heart

Materials: Red paper, scissors, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Cut a heart shape.

  2. Write: “I trust God.”

  3. Decorate with a border.

Lesson Point: We trust God, not charms.

Craft 2: Rain Cloud

Materials: Blue paper, cotton wool, blue strings

Instructions:

  1. Glue cotton wool in a cloud shape.

  2. Hang blue strings as rain.

  3. Write: “God sends rain.”

Lesson Point: God answers prayer.

Game 1: Pray for Rain

How to Play:

  1. Sit in a circle and say a short prayer.

  2. Tap fingers softly to sound like rain.

  3. End with “Thank you, God.”

Lesson Point: God hears prayer.

Game 2: God First

How to Play:

  1. Show pictures of different things.

  2. Children touch the one that is most powerful.

  3. The answer is always God.

Lesson Point: Nothing is greater than God.
