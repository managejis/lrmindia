You must be Born Again

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Good News Sunday School Lessons

Pictures

15, 22, 24

Aim

To teach that if we want to live with the Lord Jesus we must be born again.

Teacher Preparation

As you prepare this lesson thank God for the work of the Holy Spirit in your life. Ask Him to help you teach your students the Bible truth that in His love God offers us new birth in Christ. Then read and study John 3:1-17 and the lesson plan.

Memory Verse

John 3:3

No one can see the Kingdom of God unless he is born again.

Lesson Plan

Introduction

Begin the lesson by describing how you would prepare your house for the visit of an important person. Get the children to make suggestions too. You would sweep the house, put flowers around and so on.

Main Points of Lesson

1. One day when Jesus lived on the earth he had a very important visitor. He was a religious leader of the Jews. His name was Nicodemus. Show picture 24.

2. He came to see Jesus at night. Why did he do that? He didn't want anyone to know he had come to see Jesus. He came secretly because he was afraid of what the other leaders would say. He came because he had many questions to ask Jesus.

3. He knew Jesus was a teacher sent by God because of the miracles Jesus did, John 3:2. Show picture 15. Ask the children to name some miracles. Nicodemus wanted to know how he could follow God and live with Him.

4. Jesus told him he must be born again. Nicodemus asked: "How can a grown up man like me be born again?" John 3:3

5. Jesus said that this is a different birth. God's Holy Spirit is born in a person who wants to follow Him. He is changed so that he can enter heaven. Refer to Picture 22. We cannot change ourselves because of sin, John 3:5-6

Review

Briefly go over the lesson and emphasise that the Holy Spirit in a person's life enables him/her to be born into God's family and to become an inheritor of His Kingdom.

Teach Memory Verse

Our Response to the Lesson

End the lesson with a brief testimony about someone whose life has been changed. Provide a few examples of people whose lives have been changed by the power of the Holy Spirit and new birth in Christ (from personal experience, from the New Testament, or from church history). The children may be able to provide examples.

Lesson - 19: YOU MUST BE BORN AGAIN

Scripture Reading: John 3:1-17

1. Who Am I?

Choose from: Nicodemus, Jesus, the Holy Spirit

  1. I came to Jesus by night. Who am I? __________ 👉 Nicodemus

  2. I said, “You must be born again.” Who am I? __________ 👉 Jesus

  3. I give new birth to all who follow God. Who am I? __________ 👉 the Holy Spirit

2. True or False

Write T for true or F for false:

  1. Nicodemus was a religious leader. ( ___ ) 👉 T

  2. We can make ourselves new by trying hard. ( ___ ) 👉 F

  3. The Holy Spirit changes us. ( ___ ) 👉 T

3. Complete the Sentences

Word box: secretly, born, Spirit

  1. Nicodemus came ____________ because he was afraid. __________ 👉 secretly

  2. Jesus said we must be ____________ again. __________ 👉 born

  3. God’s Holy ____________ changes us. __________ 👉 Spirit

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did Nicodemus come at night?

    Sample answer: He was afraid of what other leaders would say.

  2. What does it mean to be born again?

    Sample answer: God’s Holy Spirit gives us a new life.

  3. Do you know someone God has changed?

    Sample answer: Share a short testimony together.

5. Memory Verse:

John 3:3 — “No one can see the Kingdom of God unless he is born again.”

John 1:12 — “To those who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God.”

Game 1: Prepare the House

How to Play:

  1. Children mime sweeping and tidying.

  2. Talk about how we prepare for a visitor.

  3. Say: “Come, Lord Jesus.”

Lesson Point: We should make our hearts ready.

Game 2: New Me

How to Play:

  1. Show a worn cloth and a fresh one.

  2. Children guess which is “new”.

  3. Link it to being born again.

Lesson Point: God makes us new.
