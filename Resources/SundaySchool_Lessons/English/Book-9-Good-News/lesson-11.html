Jesus is Born

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Good News Sunday School Lessons

Pictures

12, 13

Aim

To teach that as God had promised, His Son Jesus was born to Mary.

Teacher Preparation

As you prepare this lesson give thanks to God for His written word that tells us of the birth of Jesus, the living Word and Saviour. Then read and study Luke 2:1-20 and the lesson plan.

Memory Verse

Complete last week's verse, Matthew 1:21.

Mary will have a Son, and you will name him Jesus because he will save his people from their sin.

Lesson Plan

Introduction

Ask who has a new baby in their home.

Everyone is happy and there are visitors to see the baby.

Question: Who was told she was to have a baby?

Answer: Mary.

Question: Who was the baby?

Answer: Jesus Son of God.

Review memory verse: Show picture 12.

Main Points of the Story

Tell the story of Luke 2:1-20 in your own words. (Read the story in Luke several times so you know it well. Then say the story to yourself without looking at the Bible.)

1. The time for Mary to have her baby came very near. Just before it, Mary and Joseph had to leave their home in Nazareth and go back to Bethlehem where their family came from. This was about 120 km. The rulers of their country asked everyone to do this so they could count all the people.

2. Mary and Joseph have to stay in a shelter for animals (stable) because everywhere else was full.

3. Jesus is born and put in the manger. (A manger was the animal's food box). Show picture 13 and keep it out while you tell the story of the shepherds' visit.

4. The angel gives the shepherds the message.

5. The angel choir praises God.

6. The shepherds go to Bethlehem.

7. Mary thought about what the shepherds said and who this baby was.

8. The shepherds return home very happy to see what God has done.

Teach Memory Verse

Review

Briefly go over the lesson emphasising that God always keeps His promises (remember His promise to Abraham) and that despite Jesus' very humble birth He was truly God's Son and our Saviour.

Our Response to the Lesson

Ask the children to think of the joy in the family when a new baby arrives and to tell about it.

We are joyful too because God has sent Jesus. He is the Saviour of the world, King of Kings, God's Son.

Sing a Christmas song.

Lesson - 11: JESUS IS BORN

Scripture Reading: Luke 2:1-20

1. Put the Story in Order

a) The shepherds went home happy.
b) Mary and Joseph went to Bethlehem.
c) Angels told the shepherds.
d) Jesus was born and put in a manger.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. Mary and Joseph went to Bethlehem.

  2. Jesus was born and put in a manger.

  3. Angels told the shepherds.

  4. The shepherds went home happy.

2. Complete the Sentences

Word box: manger, shepherds, Bethlehem

  1. Jesus was born in ____________. __________ 👉 Bethlehem

  2. The baby was laid in a ____________. __________ 👉 manger

  3. The angels came to the ____________. __________ 👉 shepherds

3. Yes or No

Write Yes or No:

  1. Was there room for Jesus in the inn? ( ___ ) 👉 No

  2. Did the shepherds praise God? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

  3. Did Mary think about everything that happened? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did Jesus have a manger for a bed?

    Sample answer: There was no room anywhere else.

  2. How did the shepherds feel afterwards?

    Sample answer: Very happy; they praised God.

  3. Why are we happy at Christmas?

    Sample answer: Because God sent Jesus to save us.

5. Memory Verse:

Matthew 1:21 — “Mary will have a Son, and you will name him Jesus because he will save his people from their sin.”

Luke 2:11 — “Today in the town of David a Savior has been born to you; he is the Messiah, the Lord.”

Craft 1: Manger Scene

Materials: Brown paper, straw or yellow paper, cloth, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Make a manger from brown paper.

  2. Fill it with straw.

  3. Wrap a small doll in cloth.

  4. Write: “Jesus is born.”

Lesson Point: Jesus, the Saviour, was born in a humble place.

Craft 2: Shepherd Star

Materials: Yellow card, scissors, string

Instructions:

  1. Cut out a big star.

  2. Write: “Glory to God.”

  3. Add a hole and string.

  4. Hang it up.

Lesson Point: The angels gave glory to God.

Game 1: Journey to Bethlehem

How to Play:

  1. Mark a path from “Nazareth” to “Bethlehem”.

  2. Children walk it slowly.

  3. At the end shout: “Jesus is born!”

Lesson Point: Mary and Joseph travelled a long way.

Game 2: Angel Song

How to Play:

  1. Sing a Christmas song together.

  2. Half sing, half act as shepherds.

  3. Swap parts.

Lesson Point: We praise God like the angels did.
