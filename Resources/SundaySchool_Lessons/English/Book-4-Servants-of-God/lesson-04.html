Ruth and Boaz at the Threshing-Floor

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 4: Servants of God

Aim

To show the children that, just as Ruth had taken care of Naomi, Naomi now took care of Ruth. She helped Ruth find the husband God had meant for her.

Scripture Reading

Ruth 3:1-18

Memory Verse

Psalm 121:4

[H]e who watches over Israel will neither slumber nor sleep.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Ask the children what they want their lives to look like when they’re grown up. They will probably answer they want a house, a husband or wife and a family of their own.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Ruth and Naomi had each other, but in the society of those days they also needed the protection of a man in the household. Ruth was still a young woman. She could start a new family of her own. So Naomi began to make plans for Ruth to marry again (v 1).

2. It was the custom in Israel that when a man died his nearest relative should marry the dead man’s widow. That way she would become part of a new family instead of being left alone and defenceless. Boaz was a close relative of Naomi’s late husband, Elimelech, and therefore also of Ruth’s husband who had died (v 2).

3. Naomi told Ruth to dress herself with care that evening and go to the threshing-floor, where Boaz was sleeping to protect his harvest against thieves. She was to follow the custom of the Israelites and lie down quietly at Boaz’s feet when he had gone to bed (v 2-4).

4. Ruth agreed, and carefully followed Naomi’s instructions (v 5-7). Show Picture 4.

5. Describe what happened in verses 8-13. You could pretend to be Boaz. Act out his surprise at finding Ruth lying at his feet. Boaz treated Ruth with respect. He had admired her all along and thought it was a great compliment that she wanted to be his wife.

6. Boaz was careful to protect Ruth’s good name, so he sent her home before daybreak. He sent Naomi a gift of grain (2:14-15).

7. Back home Naomi listened eagerly to all Ruth had to tell her and gave her some very good advice: They had to be patient (v 16-18).

Our Response to the Story

Even though you are still only children it is not too early to take note of what makes a good marriage. Ruth and Boaz treated each other with respect and they admired each other for their kindness towards others. This is a very good foundation for a marriage.

Instil the Memory Verse

The God of Israel, who is our God, watched over Boaz and Ruth and he still watches over us.

Review the verses of Psalm 121 the children have learnt so far.

Finish the lesson with a short prayer about the need for Christian homes and ask God to help these children marry Christian husbands and wives one day.

Lesson - 4: RUTH AND BOAZ AT THE THRESHING-FLOOR

Scripture Reading: Ruth 3:1-18

1. Match the Following

A B
Naomi Gave Ruth good advice
Ruth Did what Naomi said
Boaz Treated Ruth with respect
Threshing-floor Where grain was beaten

Answers:
Naomi → Gave Ruth good advice
Ruth → Did what Naomi said
Boaz → Treated Ruth with respect
Threshing-floor → Where grain was beaten

2. Who Am I?

Choose from: Ruth, Boaz, Naomi

  1. I told Ruth to wait patiently. Who am I? __________ 👉 Naomi

  2. I sent Ruth home safely with a gift of grain. Who am I? __________ 👉 Boaz

  3. I listened to my mother-in-law. Who am I? __________ 👉 Ruth

3. Kind or Unkind? K/U

Write K for kind or U for unkind:

  1. Speaking to others with respect. ( ___ ) 👉 K

  2. Shouting at a friend. ( ___ ) 👉 U

  3. Waiting your turn patiently. ( ___ ) 👉 K

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why was Naomi planning for Ruth?

    Sample answer: She loved Ruth and wanted her to have a safe home.

  2. What did Boaz and Ruth do that was good?

    Sample answer: They treated each other with respect and kindness.

  3. Why is it hard to wait?

    Sample answer: We want things now, but God’s timing is best.

5. Memory Verse:

Psalm 121:4 — “Indeed, he who watches over Israel will neither slumber nor sleep.”

Psalm 37:7 — “Be still before the Lord and wait patiently for him.”

Craft 1: Grain Gift Bag

Materials: Paper bag, rice or lentils, ribbon, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Decorate the bag.

  2. Put in a small handful of rice or lentils.

  3. Tie it with ribbon.

  4. Write: “God watches over us.”

Lesson Point: Boaz sent grain home with Ruth. God cares for us in kind ways.

Craft 2: Watching Eye Card

Materials: Folded card, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Draw a large eye on the front.

  2. Draw a small house below it.

  3. Inside write: “God never sleeps.”

  4. Colour it brightly.

Lesson Point: God watches over His people day and night.

Game 1: Wait for It

How to Play:

  1. Choose one child as Naomi.

  2. Others stand at one end of the room.

  3. They may walk only when Naomi says “Go.”

Lesson Point: Waiting patiently is wise.

Game 2: Respectful Words

How to Play:

  1. Say a sentence like “Give me that!”.

  2. Children change it into a polite sentence.

  3. Give a clap for each polite sentence.

Lesson Point: Ruth and Boaz spoke respectfully to each other.
