Elijah and the Fire from Heaven

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 4: Servants of God

Aim

To show the children how Elijah defeated the prophets of Baal and demonstrated the power of the living God.

Scripture Reading

1 Kings 18:16-46

Memory Verse

Psalm 121

Review the whole psalm.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Talk to the class about contests and competitions. These are events in which people take part to find out who is the best at a particular activity. What kind of contests do you enjoy?

Main Points of the Lesson

1. Elijah had a contest with the prophets of Baal. King Ahab ordered the people of Israel and the prophets of Baal to meet Elijah at Mount Carmel. They would have a competition to see who was really the true God. Would it be God or Baal? (18:20-22).

2. Elijah announced the rules of the contest. The prophets or leaders of Baal – all 450 of them – would arrange a sacrifice of two bulls. Elijah, the only prophet of God, would do the same. They would not light the fire underneath the sacrifice; instead, they had to pray for fire to be sent from heaven. The one who answered by fire would be the true God. Everyone agreed to these rules (18:22-24).

3. The Baal worshippers built their altar first. They prayed all day but no fire came. Elijah started making fun of them (28:25-29).

4. In the evening Elijah called everyone to watch him. He repaired the altar to God that had been destroyed and prepared the sacrifice. He even had four large jars of water poured over it to make it harder to burn (28:30-35).

5. Then Elijah prayed to God and asked for fire from heaven to show the people that the Lord is the living God. Show Picture 22. God sent fire that burnt up everything, including the stones on which the sacrifice lay. All the people cried out, “The Lord – he is God!”The prophets of Baal, who had told the people lies, were all killed (18:36-40).

Our Response to the Story

Elijah was one person against the king and queen, 450 prophets of Baal, and the unfaithful Israelites. But he served the living God. He knew that he served the only true God and that there are no other gods. Elijah is an example to us of a courageous servant of God who stood against evil, even though he stood alone.

Some Christians are called and equipped by God to do this today. Let us pray for them and for ourselves, that we will hold on to the truth: that our God is the living God.

Instil the Memory Verse

Elijah experienced the truth of Psalm 121 and Psalm 1 in his life.

Lesson - 22: ELIJAH AND THE FIRE FROM HEAVEN

Scripture Reading: 1 Kings 18:16-46

1. Choose the Right Answer

Circle a), b) or c):

  1. Where did the contest take place?
    a) Mount Carmel b) Jerusalem c) Moab 👉 a) Mount Carmel

  2. Who answered with fire?
    a) Baal b) The living God c) Ahab 👉 b) The living God

  3. Who stood alone for God?
    a) Elijah b) Obed c) Jonathan 👉 a) Elijah

2. True or False

Write T for true or F for false:

  1. Baal sent fire from heaven. ( ___ ) 👉 F

  2. Elijah trusted the living God. ( ___ ) 👉 T

  3. The people said, “The Lord, he is God!” ( ___ ) 👉 T

3. Brave or Afraid? B/A

Write B for brave or A for afraid:

  1. Standing up for what is right. ( ___ ) 👉 B

  2. Staying quiet when others do wrong. ( ___ ) 👉 A

  3. Telling friends about God. ( ___ ) 👉 B

4. Discussion Questions

  1. What did Elijah ask God to do?

    Sample answer: To send fire so everyone would know He is God.

  2. What did the people learn?

    Sample answer: That the Lord is the true and living God.

  3. How can we be brave for God?

    Sample answer: By praying and speaking the truth.

5. Memory Verse:

Psalm 121:1-2 — “I lift up my eyes to the hills—where does my help come from? My help comes from the Lord, the Maker of heaven and earth.”

1 Kings 18:39 — “The Lord—he is God!”

Craft 1: Mountain and Flame

Materials: Paper, orange and red paper, glue

Instructions:

  1. Draw a mountain.

  2. Cut a flame from orange and red paper.

  3. Glue the flame on the altar.

  4. Write: “The Lord is God.”

Lesson Point: God answered Elijah’s prayer.

Craft 2: Stand Tall Banner

Materials: Paper strip, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Write: “The Lord, He is God!”

  2. Decorate with stars.

  3. Fold the base to stand it up.

  4. Show it to a friend.

Lesson Point: Elijah stood up for God alone.

Game 1: Who Is God?

How to Play:

  1. Two groups: one cheers “The Lord is God!”.

  2. The other group listens quietly.

  3. Switch and talk about how God is real.

Lesson Point: Only the Lord is the living God.

Game 2: Stand Up for God

How to Play:

  1. Read statements about right and wrong.

  2. Children stand if it is right to do.

  3. Sit if it is wrong.

Lesson Point: Servants of God stand up for what is right.
