Jesus enters into Jerusalem

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 4: Servants of God

Aim

To show the children how Jesus, the King of all kings, entered Jerusalem. We can look forward to the coming of his kingdom on earth one day.

Scripture Reading

Matthew 21:1-11; Isaiah 11:1-9

Memory Verse

Psalm 1:6

[T]he Lord watches over the way of the righteous…

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Families are important. We quickly get to know the members of our own family. As we grow up we also hear about our family’s history and where they used to live.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. David was the greatest king of Israel. God told him that his family would continue to rule over Israel if they remained God’s faithful servants (1 Chron 28:7).

2. About 1 000 years after David’s death a baby was born to a young woman called Mary. Her husband, Joseph, was a descendant of King David. God chose David’s family to be the human family of his own Son, Jesus Christ, the King of kings.

3. During his life on earth Jesus taught his disciples about the kingdom of God (Matthew’s Gospel calls it the kingdom of heaven). Many people expected Jesus to become king in Jerusalem, ruling all Israel just like David did and expelling all the foreigners that ruled over them.

4. One day Jesus entered Jerusalem and was welcomed by the people, but he rode on a donkey. Great earthly kings rode on camels or horses. Donkeys were used to carry goods, not important people. Today, state presidents and important leaders ride in big grand cars, not in cheap little cars or on bicycles! Jesus was the greatest Person ever to serve God, but he was humble. He did not use possessions to show his power. He sent two disciples to fetch a donkey for him to ride into the city (Matt 21:1-2).

5. The disciples had instructions to say the donkey was for their Lord’s use if anyone asked what they were doing. These events would fulfil what God’s prophets had foretold many years before (21:3-6).

6. Crowds gathered and greeted Jesus, shouting, “Praise to the Son of David! Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord! Praise God!” (21:7-11).

Show Picture 20.

The children could act out this part of the story – some waving branches and others laying down clothing for the donkey to walk on. Everyone can sing a song of praise and welcome to the King of all kings on earth.

Our Response to the Story

The people of Jerusalem did not really understand the kingdom Jesus was talking about. He wants us to be part of his kingdom by becoming part of his family, as children of God (Jn 1:12).

One day Jesus will return as King of all the earth. He will set up a kingdom of peace (Is 11:1-9). Only those who belong to God’s family will have a place in that wonderful kingdom.

Lesson - 20: JESUS ENTERS INTO JERUSALEM

Scripture Reading: Matthew 21:1-11; Isaiah 11:1-9

1. Match the Following

A B
Donkey Carried Jesus into the city
Palm branches Waved by the crowd
Hosanna A shout of praise
Jesus The King of kings

Answers:
Donkey → Carried Jesus into the city
Palm branches → Waved by the crowd
Hosanna → A shout of praise
Jesus → The King of kings

2. True or False

Write T for true or F for false:

  1. Jesus rode a horse. ( ___ ) 👉 F

  2. The people praised Jesus. ( ___ ) 👉 T

  3. Jesus came from David’s family. ( ___ ) 👉 T

  4. Jesus showed He was humble. ( ___ ) 👉 T

3. Circle the Right Words

Circle the right choice:

  1. Jesus is the King of (kings / donkeys). 👉 kings

  2. Jesus entered the city of (Jerusalem / Moab). 👉 Jerusalem

  3. Jesus will set up a kingdom of (peace / war). 👉 peace

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did Jesus ride a donkey?

    Sample answer: He was humble and came in peace.

  2. What did the crowd shout?

    Sample answer: Praise to the Son of David!

  3. How can we welcome Jesus today?

    Sample answer: By praising and obeying Him.

5. Memory Verse:

Psalm 1:6 — “The Lord watches over the way of the righteous, but the way of the wicked will perish.”

Matthew 21:9 — “Hosanna to the Son of David!”

Craft 1: Palm Branch

Materials: Green paper, tape, stick

Instructions:

  1. Cut a long leaf shape from green paper.

  2. Cut slits along the sides.

  3. Tape it to a stick.

  4. Wave it for Jesus.

Lesson Point: The crowd waved branches to welcome their King.

Craft 2: Donkey Puppet

Materials: Paper bag, grey paper, glue, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Cut donkey ears from grey paper.

  2. Glue the ears to the bag.

  3. Draw eyes and a nose.

  4. Use it to tell the story.

Lesson Point: Jesus rode a donkey as a humble King.

Game 1: Welcome Parade

How to Play:

  1. Children wave paper branches.

  2. One child walks like a donkey.

  3. All sing “Hosanna!”

Lesson Point: We welcome Jesus with joy.

Game 2: King or Servant?

How to Play:

  1. Say a sentence about Jesus.

  2. Children stand if it shows humility.

  3. Talk about why Jesus is both King and servant.

Lesson Point: Jesus is a humble King.
