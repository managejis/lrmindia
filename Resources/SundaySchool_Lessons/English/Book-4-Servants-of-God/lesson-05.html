Boaz and the Elders of Bethlehem

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 4: Servants of God

Aim

To show the children that Ruth, a foreigner, became the obedient and blessed servant of the God of Israel.

Scripture Reading

Ruth 4:1-22

Memory Verse

Psalm 121:5

The Lord watches over you – the Lord is your shade at your right hand …

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Ask the class what happens when there is a problem in their community. Possible answer: The elders or leaders often meet to discuss it.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. When the Israelites of old had a problem they used the same method to reach a solution. They usually met at the town gate. So Boaz sat down with the elders of Bethlehem (4:1).

2. Boaz was not Elimelech’s closest relative. Elimelech was Naomi’s husband who had died, remember? So Boaz called the person who was a closer relative than himself to sit down with him and the town elders so that they could discuss the selling of a field that had belonged to Elimelech (4:1-4).

3. This other relative was first in line to buy the field because he was more closely related to Elimelech. At first he wanted to buy it. Then Boaz reminded him that also meant he had to take care of Naomi (who was Elimelech’s widow) as well as Ruth (who was the widow of Elimelech’s son). That made him change his mind. He said Boaz should buy the field and take care of the two widows (4:4-6).

4. That cleared the way for Boaz to marry Ruth. To seal the agreement that the closest relative gave up all claim to Elimelech’s possessions, he took off one of his shoes and gave it to Boaz. That was the custom in Israel (4:7-10). Show Picture 5.

5. Ruth and Boaz were married. God blessed them with a son called Obed. There was great rejoicing. The local women were full of praise for Ruth even though she was a foreigner (4:13-16).

6. This boy grew up to be the grandfather of the famous King David (4:17). It was into this human family that God chose his Son, Jesus Christ, to be born.

Our Response to the Story

Ruth left her own home and family and came to a foreign land to look after Naomi. She kept her word. Read 1:16-17 to the class. God honoured Ruth and blessed her with a new home and family.

Ruth is an example to us of trust in God in all circumstances. We can do the same.

Instil the Memory Verse

Think back to how God protected Ruth.

Review the verses of Psalm 121 the children have learnt so far.

Lesson - 5: BOAZ AND THE ELDERS OF BETHLEHEM

Scripture Reading: Ruth 4:1-22

1. Put the Story in Order

a) Ruth and Boaz had a son named Obed.
b) Boaz met the elders at the town gate.
c) Boaz married Ruth.
d) The closer relative gave up his right.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. Boaz met the elders at the town gate.

  2. The closer relative gave up his right.

  3. Boaz married Ruth.

  4. Ruth and Boaz had a son named Obed.

2. Complete the Sentences

Word box: David, Jesus, Obed

  1. Ruth and Boaz’s son was called ____________. __________ 👉 Obed

  2. Obed’s grandson was King ____________. __________ 👉 David

  3. God’s Son, ____________, was born into this family. __________ 👉 Jesus

3. Yes or No?

Write Y for yes or N for no:

  1. Was Ruth born in Israel? ( ___ ) 👉 N

  2. Did God bless Ruth? ( ___ ) 👉 Y

  3. Did Ruth keep her promise to Naomi? ( ___ ) 👉 Y

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Where did the leaders of the town meet?

    Sample answer: At the town gate.

  2. Why did God bless Ruth?

    Sample answer: She was loyal, kind and trusted God.

  3. How do we keep our promises?

    Sample answer: We do what we said, even when it is hard.

5. Memory Verse:

Psalm 121:5 — “The Lord watches over you—the Lord is your shade at your right hand.”

Proverbs 3:5 — “Trust in the Lord with all your heart.”

Craft 1: Family Tree

Materials: Paper, brown and green paper, glue, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Make a tree trunk with brown paper.

  2. Cut four green leaves.

  3. Write Ruth, Boaz, Obed and David on the leaves.

  4. Glue them on the tree.

Lesson Point: God blessed Ruth’s family, and Jesus was born from it.

Craft 2: Shade Umbrella

Materials: Paper plate, stick, tape, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Colour a paper plate.

  2. Tape a stick on the back as a handle.

  3. Write: “The Lord is your shade.”

  4. Hold it up like a sun shade.

Lesson Point: God protects us like shade from the sun.

Game 1: Family Chain

How to Play:

  1. Say the names: Ruth, Boaz, Obed, David, Jesus.

  2. Five children stand in a line.

  3. Each says their name and links arms.

Lesson Point: God used Ruth’s family to bring Jesus into the world.

Game 2: Promise Keepers

How to Play:

  1. Each child whispers a good promise to a friend.

  2. They act out keeping it.

  3. The class guesses the promise.

Lesson Point: Like Ruth, we should keep our promises.
