Samuel Anoints Saul with Oil

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 4: Servants of God

Aim

To show the children how Saul was chosen as king to lead the people of Israel.

Scripture Reading

1 Samuel 8:4-22; 9:1-27; 10:1-4, 9

Memory Verse

Psalm 1:1

(continue learning v 1) Blessed is the man who does not walk in the counsel of the wicked or stand in the way of sinners …

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Tell the class a short story about how you lost something and the hard time you had finding it.

Main Points of the Lesson

1. There once was a rich man in Israel called Kish, who lost his donkeys. He sent his son Saul with a servant to look for them. Describe what Saul was like (9:1-3).

2. Saul and the servant walked many kilometres but could not find the donkeys. Saul decided that they had to go back without the donkeys; otherwise his father might begin to worry that his son had become lost, too (9:4-5).

3. But the servant had a better idea. They were near the town where Samuel, the man of God, lived. They should ask him for advice, the servant suggested. Some girls directed them to where Samuel stayed (9:6-13).

4. Saul and his servant met Samuel, who told them the donkeys had been found. Saul was very surprised at the way Samuel welcomed them with special food and a good place to sleep. Saul’s father was an important man, but he came from the smallest tribe in Israel (9:14, 19-25).

5. Saul did not yet know that God had told Samuel that he had chosen Saul to be king of Israel (9:17). The people had asked for a king so that they would be like all the other tribes around them. That upset Samuel. The Lord was Israel’s only King, he told the people. And he warned them: Human kings often took more from the people than they gave them (8:4-22).

6. At dawn the next morning, after Saul and his servant had spent the night in Samuel’s house, Samuel escorted them to the edge of town. Then Samuel took Saul aside and anointed his head with oil to show that God had appointed him ruler over Israel. Show Picture 10 (9:26-27; 10:1-2).

Our Response to the Story

God did not leave Saul all on his own to carry the big responsibility of being king. The Lord changed Saul so that he would be able to do his task well (10:9).

Today, too, when God gives us a task he gives us the power to do it. We should trust the Lord to make us fit to serve him (Phil 1:6).

Lesson - 10: SAMUEL ANOINTS SAUL WITH OIL

Scripture Reading: 1 Samuel 8:4-22; 9:1-27; 10:1-4, 9

1. Complete the Sentences

Word box: donkeys, oil, Samuel

  1. Saul was looking for his father’s ____________. __________ 👉 donkeys

  2. He asked the man of God, ____________, for advice. __________ 👉 Samuel

  3. Samuel poured ____________ on Saul’s head. __________ 👉 oil

2. True or False

Write T for true or F for false:

  1. Saul found the donkeys by himself. ( ___ ) 👉 F

  2. God chose Saul to be king. ( ___ ) 👉 T

  3. God helped Saul with his new task. ( ___ ) 👉 T

3. Unscramble the Word

Write the word correctly:

  1. GNIK (a ruler) __________ 👉 king

  2. MUELSA (the prophet) __________ 👉 Samuel

  3. LIO (poured on Saul’s head) __________ 👉 oil

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did Saul go to Samuel?

    Sample answer: He could not find the donkeys and needed advice.

  2. What did the oil on Saul’s head mean?

    Sample answer: God had chosen him to be king.

  3. Who gives us the power to do our tasks?

    Sample answer: God does.

5. Memory Verse:

Psalm 1:1 — “Blessed is the man who does not walk in the counsel of the wicked or stand in the way of sinners or sit in the seat of mockers.”

Philippians 4:13 — “I can do all this through him who gives me strength.”

Craft 1: Oil Flask

Materials: Small paper cup, yellow paper, glue

Instructions:

  1. Decorate the cup.

  2. Cut a drop of oil from yellow paper.

  3. Glue it on the cup.

  4. Write: “God chooses.”

Lesson Point: Oil showed that God chose Saul to lead.

Craft 2: Crown Headband

Materials: Gold paper strip, tape, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Cut a long strip of paper.

  2. Cut points along the top.

  3. Decorate with crayons.

  4. Tape it around your head.

Lesson Point: God chose Saul, and he needed God’s help to lead.

Game 1: Find the Donkey

How to Play:

  1. Hide a paper donkey in the room.

  2. Children search quietly.

  3. The finder gives it to the leader.

Lesson Point: Saul searched, then asked Samuel for help.

Game 2: Ask for Help

How to Play:

  1. Give each child a small puzzle to do.

  2. Let them ask a friend when stuck.

  3. Talk about asking for advice.

Lesson Point: It is wise to ask for help from God’s people.
