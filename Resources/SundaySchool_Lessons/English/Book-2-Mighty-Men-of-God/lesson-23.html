Jesus Died for Us

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 2: Mighty Men of God

Aim

To show the children that Jesus died to take away our sins.

Scripture Reading

Luke 23:26-47; John 3:14-18

Memory Verse

John 3:16

“For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life.”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Retell the story of the boy who climbed the mango tree (see Lesson 20), but replace the last part with the following ending: While the boy was still in the tree eating mangoes, the owner came and saw him. He was angry. He shouted, “You! Come down at once! I’ll beat you for stealing my mangoes.” Just then the boy’s brother arrived there. He offered to pay the price of the mangoes the boy had eaten. The owner accepted this exchange. The boy came down from the tree. He was safe, as his brother had paid the price for his trespass.

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. Show picture 20 and ask some questions about it. The following are some suggestions: Q: What is wrong with these people?

A: They are sick/they have been bitten by snakes.

Q: Why?

A: They disobeyed God.

Point to Moses: Q: Who is this man?

A: Moses, their leader.

Q: What is he doing?

A: He is telling the people to look up at the snake to be healed.

2. Show picture 23.

Q: Who is this on the central cross?

A: It is Jesus, who died to take away our sins. We must look up at him to be healed.

3. When Jesus was crucified two criminals were crucified next to him. Tell the children what they said (Lk 23:39-43). They had done wrong, but Jesus was innocent.

4. The leaders of the people laughed at Jesus and mocked him (v 35).

5. The soldiers shared his clothes among them and also mocked him (v 34, 36-37).

6. One soldier, who stayed there until the very end, saw what happened when Jesus died (v 47). Tell the children how he reacted.

Our Response to the Story

Only the Son of God could take away our sins. He took our punishment on himself.

Act the introductory story and end the session by saying the memory verse together.

Lesson - 23: JESUS DIED FOR US

Scripture Reading: Luke 23:26-47; John 3:14-18

1. Complete the Sentences

Word box: price, sins, cross

  1. Jesus died to take away our ____________. __________ 👉 sins

  2. The boy’s brother paid the ____________ for the mangoes. __________ 👉 price

  3. Jesus died on the ____________. __________ 👉 cross

2. Match the Following

A B
Moses lifted up the snake People were healed
Jesus lifted up on the cross We are saved
The criminal Asked Jesus to remember him
The soldier Said Jesus was innocent

Answers:
Moses lifted up the snake → People were healed
Jesus lifted up on the cross → We are saved
The criminal → Asked Jesus to remember him
The soldier → Said Jesus was innocent

3. Yes or No

Write Yes or No:

  1. Was Jesus guilty of any wrong? ( ___ ) 👉 No

  2. Did Jesus take our punishment? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did Jesus die?

    Sample answer: To take the punishment for our sins.

  2. How is the mango story like Jesus’ death?

    Sample answer: The brother paid the price for the boy, as Jesus paid for us.

  3. How can we thank Jesus?

    Sample answer: By praying, loving Him and telling others.

5. Memory Verse:

John 3:16 — “For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life.”

Romans 5:8 — “While we were still sinners, Christ died for us.”

Craft 1: Three Crosses

Materials: Brown paper, black paper, glue

Instructions:

  1. Cut three crosses, one large and two small.

  2. Glue them on a dark background with the big one in the middle.

  3. Write: “Jesus died for me.”

Lesson Point: Jesus died to take away our sins.

Craft 2: Paid in Full Card

Materials: Folded card, crayons, red heart stickers

Instructions:

  1. Draw a big cross on the front.

  2. Inside write: “Jesus paid for my sins.”

  3. Add heart stickers.

Lesson Point: Jesus paid the price for us.

Game 1: Act the Mango Story

How to Play:

  1. Three children act the mango tree story: the boy, the owner and the brother.

  2. The brother pays the price and the boy is safe.

  3. Talk about how Jesus paid for us.

Lesson Point: Jesus took our punishment on Himself.

Game 2: Look and Be Healed

How to Play:

  1. Hang a picture of the cross high on the wall.

  2. Children point and say “Look to Jesus.”

  3. Say the memory verse together.

Lesson Point: We look up to Jesus to be saved.
