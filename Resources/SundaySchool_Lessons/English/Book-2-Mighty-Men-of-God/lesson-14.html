Moses and the Burning Bush

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 2: Mighty Men of God

Aim

To teach the children that God called Moses to be a leader and gave him the power to do the work.

Scripture Reading

Exodus 2:11 – 3:21; 4:1-20

Memory Verse

Exodus 3:14

“I am who I am … say to the Israelites: ‘I am has sent me to you.’”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

In front of the class, carefully light a piece of paper and let it burn up completely. (If you put the burning paper on the lid of a cooking pot, the class can see it and it will be safe.) Show the children that when something is burnt very little is left.

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. Moses was in the desert when he saw a bush that was on fire, but did not burn up. Briefly show picture 14.

Why was Moses in the desert? The last time we talked about him he was being cared for in the king’s house! God had saved and protected him as a baby. Briefly show picture 13. Review the story and memory verse of Lesson 13.

2. Moses grew up in Pharaoh’s house, but when he visited his own people he could not bear the cruelty and injustice they were enduring. He tried to put things right in his own way and as a result had to flee for his life (2:11-14).

3. In this other land Moses married and lived for 40 years. As he was looking after his father-in-law’s sheep in the desert near Mount Sinai, God taught him how to be a leader (2:21-22).

Moses’ people, the Israelites, were still slaves in Egypt. They cried out to God in their suffering (2:23-25).

4. One day Moses saw a strange thing. A bush was burning without being destroyed! Moses went closer to have a better look. Show picture 14 again. God spoke to Moses from within the fire (3:1-3).

5. When Moses approached the bush God told Moses that he had to remember God is holy (3:4-6).

6. God told Moses he was chosen to lead the Israelites out of Egypt to a new land (3:7-10).

7. Moses made many excuses. He did not want to take on this task!

Moses said: a) He was an unimportant person (3:11). b) He did not know what to tell the Israelites about God (3:13; 4:1). c) He was not a good speaker at all (4:10).

8. God answered every excuse Moses offered and taught Moses about himself. God’s name, he said, is I am who I am (3:14). This name shows that God is always present, he is always active and he always stays the same. God gave Moses three signs he could use to convince the people that he was God’s messenger (we shall come to this in the next lesson; 4:2-9). Aaron, Moses’ brother, was to be his spokesman as he was a better speaker.

9. Moses returned to Egypt ready to do God’s work (4:18-20).

Say the Memory Verse

Our Response to the Story

When God asks us to do something for him he never leaves us without the power and skills to do it. If you talk to your friends about who God is, God will help you to say the right things.

Note to the teachers: The Egyptians called Moses’ people the Hebrews. The Hebrews called themselves the children of Israel or Israelites. Israel was the new name God gave to Joseph’s father, Jacob, later in his life.

Lesson - 14: MOSES AND THE BURNING BUSH

Scripture Reading: Exodus 2:11 – 3:21; 4:1-20

1. Moses’ Worry and God’s Answer

A B
“I am unimportant.” “I will be with you.”
“I cannot speak well.” “Aaron will speak for you.”
“What is your name?” “I AM WHO I AM.”
“They won’t believe me.” God gave him three signs.

Answers:
“I am unimportant.” → “I will be with you.”
“I cannot speak well.” → “Aaron will speak for you.”
“What is your name?” → “I AM WHO I AM.”
“They won’t believe me.” → God gave him three signs.

2. Complete the Sentences

Word box: sheep, fire, holy

  1. Moses was looking after ____________ when he saw the bush. __________ 👉 sheep

  2. The bush was on ____________ but did not burn up. __________ 👉 fire

  3. God told Moses to remember that God is ____________. __________ 👉 holy

3. Yes or No

Write Yes or No:

  1. Did God choose Moses to lead the Israelites? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

  2. Did Moses make excuses? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

  3. Does God leave us without help when He asks us to do something? ( ___ ) 👉 No

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did Moses go closer to the bush?

    Sample answer: He wanted a better look at the strange sight.

  2. What did God teach Moses about Himself?

    Sample answer: That He is always present, always active and never changes.

  3. How can we speak to our friends about God?

    Sample answer: By asking God for help and telling them what we know about Him.

5. Memory Verse:

Exodus 3:14 — “I am who I am. Say to the Israelites: I am has sent me to you.”

Exodus 4:12 — “Now go; I will help you speak and will teach you what to say.”

Craft 1: Burning Bush

Materials: Green paper, red, orange and yellow paper strips, glue

Instructions:

  1. Cut a bush shape from green paper.

  2. Glue red, orange and yellow flame strips on top.

  3. Write: “I AM.”

Lesson Point: God is always present, always active and never changes.

Craft 2: Take Off Your Sandals

Materials: Paper, crayons, scissors

Instructions:

  1. Draw and cut out a pair of sandals.

  2. Colour them and glue them on a card.

  3. Write: “God is holy.”

Lesson Point: We should respect God because He is holy.

Game 1: Excuses, Excuses

How to Play:

  1. The teacher gives a task, such as “Please lead the line”.

  2. Children give silly excuses and the teacher answers each one with “God will help you.”

  3. Then everyone does the task.

Lesson Point: God answers every excuse and helps us.

Game 2: I AM Name Game

How to Play:

  1. Sit in a circle and say your name with an action.

  2. The next child repeats all previous names and actions.

  3. Talk about how God told Moses His name.

Lesson Point: God’s name shows He is always with us.
