Jacob and Joseph Together Again

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 2: Mighty Men of God

Aim

To show the children that Joseph and Jacob were happily reunited. God blessed Joseph throughout his troubles.

Scripture Reading

Genesis 45:16 – 46:7; 46:28 – 47:12; 47:27 – 48:22; 50:15-21

Memory Verse

Acts 7:9-10

“God was with … [Joseph] and rescued him from all his troubles.”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Can you remember any family member or friend coming to visit your house after having been away for a long time? It is very exciting. Everyone has changed. The children have grown bigger. There is a lot to talk about.

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. Joseph had a lot to talk about with his father. They had not seen each other for about 20 years!

At first Jacob could not believe what his sons told him when they arrived home from Egypt. Joseph was alive! Joseph had sent many gifts with them (45:23-27).

2. They also brought an invitation from Pharaoh for Joseph’s whole family to move to Egypt. They were almost 100 people (45:17-20).

3. Jacob packed up to go. God promised to continue to be with them (46:1-7).

4. Joseph and his father, Jacob, were reunited at long last (46:28-30).

5. Joseph took some of his brothers and his father to meet Pharaoh. The family settled in the best part of Egypt (47:1-12).

6. Jacob lived in Egypt for 17 years, until he was 147 years old (47:28). Before his father died Joseph brought his own sons to him and Jacob blessed them (48:8-12). Show picture 12.

7. Joseph put Jacob’s right hand on the head of his elder son, Manasseh, and Jacob’s left hand on the younger son, Ephraim. But look at the picture: Jacob crossed his arms. The family of Ephraim, the younger son, became a great nation (48:13-14, 17-19).

8. After Jacob’s death Joseph’s brothers became afraid again. Had Joseph really forgiven them, or would he take revenge on them now that their father wasn’t there anymore? Joseph reassured his brothers (50:15-21).

Our Response to the Story

All through his life Joseph had many problems, but he overcame them all. That was because God was with him throughout his troubles. God honoured Joseph because Joseph honoured God. If we put our trust in God, we need never lose hope.

Lesson - 12: JACOB AND JOSEPH TOGETHER AGAIN

Scripture Reading: Genesis 45:16 – 46:7; 46:28 – 47:12; 47:27 – 48:22; 50:15-21

1. Yes or No

Write Yes or No:

  1. Did Jacob believe the news at first? ( ___ ) 👉 No

  2. Did Pharaoh invite the family to Egypt? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

  3. Did Joseph take revenge on his brothers? ( ___ ) 👉 No

2. Put the Story in Order

a) Jacob blessed Joseph’s sons.
b) Joseph sent gifts to his father.
c) Jacob and Joseph hugged each other.
d) Jacob travelled to Egypt.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. Joseph sent gifts to his father.

  2. Jacob travelled to Egypt.

  3. Jacob and Joseph hugged each other.

  4. Jacob blessed Joseph’s sons.

3. Circle the Right Words

Circle the correct words:

  1. After Jacob died, the brothers were (afraid / happy). 👉 afraid

  2. Joseph (forgave / punished) them. 👉 forgave

  3. God was (with / far from) Joseph all his life. 👉 with

4. Discussion Questions

  1. How did Jacob feel when he saw Joseph?

    Sample answer: Very happy after about 20 years apart.

  2. Why did God bless Joseph?

    Sample answer: Joseph honoured God throughout his troubles.

  3. What can we do when we have problems?

    Sample answer: Trust God and never lose hope.

5. Memory Verse:

Acts 7:9-10 — “God was with him and rescued him from all his troubles.”

Psalm 34:19 — “The righteous person may have many troubles, but the Lord delivers him from them all.”

Craft 1: Wagon Cart

Materials: Cardboard, paper plates, crayons, glue

Instructions:

  1. Cut a box shape from cardboard for the cart.

  2. Glue two paper plates as wheels.

  3. Draw the family riding to Egypt and write: “God is with us.”

Lesson Point: God promised to go with Jacob’s family to Egypt.

Craft 2: Family Hands Card

Materials: Paper, crayons, scissors

Instructions:

  1. Trace each child’s hand on paper and cut them out.

  2. Glue the hands around a big heart.

  3. Write: “Families together.”

Lesson Point: God brings families together again.

Game 1: Reunion Run

How to Play:

  1. Give each child a card with a picture, with two cards of each kind.

  2. Children search for the child with the matching card and hug.

  3. Cheer for each match.

Lesson Point: Jacob and Joseph were joyfully reunited.

Game 2: Welcome Home

How to Play:

  1. One child leaves the room and then returns.

  2. Everyone cheers and welcomes them home.

  3. Take turns and talk about how it feels.

Lesson Point: God’s people rejoice when they are together.
