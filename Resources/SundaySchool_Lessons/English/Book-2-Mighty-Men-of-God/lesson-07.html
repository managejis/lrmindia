Joseph the Slave in Egypt

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 2: Mighty Men of God

Aim

To teach the children that God was with Joseph, whatever his circumstances. In all his difficulties God blessed him.

Scripture Reading

Genesis 39:1-23

Memory Verse

1 Corinthians 10:13

God … will not let you be tempted beyond what you can bear.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Make up a story about two children in which the younger one breaks something in the house or steals some sugar, but the parents blame and wrongly punish the older child. Of course it is very unfair.

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. Some very unfair things happened to Joseph. Ask the class what they were (see Gen 37:11, 18-20, 23-24, 28). Joseph probably was not a very likeable person at the time his brothers sold him to the travelling traders, but their punishment was very cruel.

2. Joseph was sold as a slave to Potiphar, an important official of the king of Egypt. Joseph worked well and became a trusted servant. He was put in charge of Potiphar’s whole household. He was given great responsibility and God blessed him in everything he did (39:1-6).

3. But more trouble came to Joseph. Potiphar’s wife wanted Joseph for herself. Joseph refused her temptations many times (39:7-10).

4. Rather than give in to her demands Joseph ran away from Potiphar’s wife. She tore off a piece of his clothing (39:11-15). Show picture 7.

5. Potiphar believed the lies his wife told him about the incident. Joseph ended up in prison for something he did not do (39:16-20).

6. But God was with Joseph, even in prison (39:21-23).

Our Response to the Story

This was very unfair to Joseph. Although he was innocent he remained in prison for 8-10 years. Still, he trusted God and obeyed his laws. He believed God was in control, and God blessed Joseph in all he did. One day God will judge us all and reject those who choose to do evil. But to those who trust in him God gives the strength to resist the temptations that come their way. See what today’s memory verse says about this.

When things are difficult for you, remember Joseph’s example. Remind the class of the introductory story.

Lesson - 7: JOSEPH THE SLAVE IN EGYPT

Scripture Reading: Genesis 39:1-23

1. True or False

Write T for true or F for false:

  1. Joseph worked well for Potiphar. ( ___ ) 👉 T

  2. Joseph agreed to do wrong. ( ___ ) 👉 F

  3. Joseph ran away from temptation. ( ___ ) 👉 T

  4. God left Joseph alone in prison. ( ___ ) 👉 F

2. Match the Following

A B
Potiphar Joseph’s master
Potiphar’s wife Told lies about Joseph
Joseph Served God faithfully
God Was with Joseph

Answers:
Potiphar → Joseph’s master
Potiphar’s wife → Told lies about Joseph
Joseph → Served God faithfully
God → Was with Joseph

3. Say Yes or No to Temptation

Circle the right choice:

  1. When a friend says “Let’s steal”, I say (yes / no). 👉 no

  2. When someone tells me to lie, I (agree / refuse). 👉 refuse

  3. When I am tempted, I (pray / give in). 👉 pray

4. Discussion Questions

  1. What unfair things happened to Joseph?

    Sample answer: He was sold as a slave and put in prison for something he did not do.

  2. How did Joseph resist temptation?

    Sample answer: He said no and ran away.

  3. What helps you say no to wrong?

    Sample answer: Praying and remembering God’s Word.

5. Memory Verse:

1 Corinthians 10:13 — “God will not let you be tempted beyond what you can bear.”

Psalm 119:11 — “I have hidden your word in my heart that I might not sin against you.”

Craft 1: Stop Sign

Materials: Red paper, white paper, craft stick, glue

Instructions:

  1. Cut a red circle and glue it on a stick.

  2. Write STOP in white letters.

  3. Write below: “Say no to wrong.”

Lesson Point: God gives strength to resist temptation.

Craft 2: God Is With Me Window

Materials: Card, crayons, scissors

Instructions:

  1. Draw window bars on the front of a folded card.

  2. Inside draw Joseph smiling.

  3. Write: “God is with me.”

Lesson Point: God was with Joseph even in prison.

Game 1: Say No!

How to Play:

  1. The teacher calls out wrong things to do.

  2. All children shout “No!” and cross their arms.

  3. For good things they shout “Yes!”

Lesson Point: We must choose to say no to sin.

Game 2: Run Away!

How to Play:

  1. Place a “tempting” sweet in one corner.

  2. Children walk past it and move away without touching it.

  3. Talk about how Joseph ran from temptation.

Lesson Point: Joseph ran away from sin.
