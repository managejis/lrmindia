Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 2: Mighty Men of God

Aim

To teach the children that God gave Moses the Ten Commandments to show people everywhere how to worship and serve him.

Scripture Reading

Exodus 19:1-24; 20:1-17

Memory Verse

Exodus 19:5

“Now if you obey me fully and keep my covenant, then out of all nations you will be my treasured possession.”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Talk about rules. The children should be able to tell you about the rules at their school. Why do we have rules? One possible answer is: to help us live together in peace and harmony.

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. Moses was leading a large number of people through the desert. They needed some rules to show them how to live together in peace and, most importantly, how to serve God.

2. Three months after the Israelites had left Egypt, they came near a mountain called Sinai. God spoke to Moses there and made him a promise.

Say the memory verse.

3. God said he would reveal himself to the people, but they had to prepare themselves first (19:9-12).

4. God came to Mount Sinai. Say in your own words what happened (19:16-19).

5. God called Moses to the top of the mountain and gave him the Ten Commandments written on two flat stones. Show picture 19.

6. These are the laws God gave Moses:

1. I am the Lord your God. You must worship only me.

2. Do not put your trust in anything or anyone else. That is idolatry.

3. Do not use my name without respect for who I am.

4. Keep the seventh day holy. Set it aside for resting and for worshipping me.

5. Honour your parents.

6. Do not commit murder.

7. Do not commit adultery.

8. Do not steal.

9. Do not lie.

10. Do not envy what other people have.

Our Response to the Story

Choose one or two of these commands and explain what they mean. This could be done by means of simple little plays that should involve two or three children. Practice beforehand! Commandments 5, 8, 9 and 10 could be demonstrated easily in this way. Now you see: although God gave these commandments to the Israelites a long time ago, they still tell us how to serve God and the people around us.

Lesson - 19: THE LAWS OF GOD

Scripture Reading: Exodus 19:1-24; 20:1-17

1. Match the Following

A B
Honour your parents Obey and respect them
Do not steal Do not take what is not yours
Do not lie Tell the truth
Do not envy Be happy with what you have

Answers:
Honour your parents → Obey and respect them
Do not steal → Do not take what is not yours
Do not lie → Tell the truth
Do not envy → Be happy with what you have

2. Count and Write

Write the answer:

  1. God gave ____ commandments. __________ 👉 10

  2. They were written on ____ flat stones. __________ 👉 two

  3. God gave them on Mount ____________. __________ 👉 Sinai

3. Keep or Break?

Write K for keeping a commandment or B for breaking it:

  1. Telling the truth. ( ___ ) 👉 K

  2. Taking my friend’s pencil. ( ___ ) 👉 B

  3. Wanting everything my friend has. ( ___ ) 👉 B

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why do we have rules?

    Sample answer: To help us live together in peace and to serve God.

  2. Which commandment is easy for you to keep? Which is hard?

    Sample answer: Answers will vary.

  3. How can we keep God’s commandments?

    Sample answer: By loving God and asking Him for help.

5. Memory Verse:

Exodus 19:5 — “Now if you obey me fully and keep my covenant, then out of all nations you will be my treasured possession.”

Deuteronomy 6:5 — “Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your strength.”

Craft 1: Two Stone Tablets

Materials: Grey paper, black crayon, glue

Instructions:

  1. Cut two tablet shapes from grey paper.

  2. Write the numbers 1 to 10 on them.

  3. Glue them side by side on a card.

Lesson Point: God gave the Ten Commandments to show us how to live.

Craft 2: Our Class Rules Card

Materials: Card, crayons, stickers

Instructions:

  1. Write three rules for the class, such as “Be kind.”

  2. Decorate the card.

  3. Put it up at home or in class.

Lesson Point: Rules help us live together in peace.

Game 1: Commandment Acting

How to Play:

  1. Two or three children act out one commandment, such as honouring parents.

  2. The class guesses which one it is.

  3. Take turns with different commandments.

Lesson Point: God’s laws tell us how to serve Him and others.

Game 2: Keep or Break Sort

How to Play:

  1. Make cards with good and bad actions.

  2. Children sort them into “Keep” and “Break” piles.

  3. Check together which commandment each one matches.

Lesson Point: We should choose to obey God’s laws.
