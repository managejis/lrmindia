Manna and Water in the Desert

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 2: Mighty Men of God

Aim

To show the children that God will provide what we need.

Scripture Reading

Exodus 16:1-35; 17:1-6

Memory Verse

Philippians 4:19

And my God will meet all your needs …

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Talk about the air-lift of food into famine-struck Juba, in the Sudan. The people there cannot produce enough food for themselves, and it is dangerous to go outside the town to look for food. They depend on the aeroplanes to bring them food. This has continued for over three years.

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. Like the people in Juba the people of Israel did not know where they would find food. They had crossed the Red Sea. Show picture 17. Now they were in the desert, where there was no food or water. They complained to Moses and to God (Ex 16:1-3, 8).

2. God sent them food. Every evening he sent birds to the camp so that the people could catch and eat them.

3. Every morning, God sent them a food that they used like bread or maize. The Israelites called it manna (v 31). They collected it fresh from the ground every morning. If someone tried to keep some for the next day, it went bad. They had to collect just enough for each day (v 14-21).

4. Every sixth day (what we now call Friday) they collected twice as much as usual, to last them two days. That was because no manna was to be found on the seventh day, the Sabbath. That was a holy day, set aside for resting and for worshipping God (v 22-30).

5. The people also complained to Moses when there was no water (17:1-2).

6. Moses followed God’s instructions and water flowed out of a rock. Tell in you own words what happens in verses 2-6. Show picture 18.

The people of Israel lived in the desert for 40 years. All that time God provided enough food for two million people, which probably was their total number.

Our Response to the Story

God met the needs of the Israelites in the desert. Do you trust him to fulfil your needs today?

In this section remind the class of last week’s story.

A story from your own experience as a Christian would be very helpful here.

Lesson - 18: MANNA AND WATER IN THE DESERT

Scripture Reading: Exodus 16:1-35; 17:1-6

1. Count and Write

Write the answer:

  1. The Israelites lived in the desert for ____ years. __________ 👉 40

  2. Manna was collected every ____________. __________ 👉 morning

  3. On the sixth day they collected ____________ as much. __________ 👉 twice

2. Match the Following

A B
Birds Meat in the evening
Manna Bread in the morning
The rock Gave water
The seventh day A day of rest

Answers:
Birds → Meat in the evening
Manna → Bread in the morning
The rock → Gave water
The seventh day → A day of rest

3. Yes or No

Write Yes or No:

  1. Did God give the people what they needed? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

  2. Could they keep extra manna for the next day (on most days)? ( ___ ) 👉 No

  3. Should we complain when we are hungry or thirsty? ( ___ ) 👉 No

4. Discussion Questions

  1. What did the people complain about?

    Sample answer: They had no food or water.

  2. How did God provide for them?

    Sample answer: He sent birds, manna and water from a rock.

  3. How does God provide for you?

    Sample answer: Answers will vary, such as food, family and home.

5. Memory Verse:

Philippians 4:19 — “And my God will meet all your needs.”

Matthew 6:11 — “Give us today our daily bread.”

Craft 1: Manna Bowl

Materials: Paper bowl, white paper flakes or cotton, glue, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Colour a paper bowl.

  2. Glue white paper flakes inside like manna.

  3. Write: “God gives daily bread.”

Lesson Point: God provided food for 40 years.

Craft 2: Water from the Rock

Materials: Grey paper, blue paper, glue

Instructions:

  1. Cut a big rock from grey paper.

  2. Glue blue waves coming out of the rock.

  3. Write: “God gives us water.”

Lesson Point: God provides what we need.

Game 1: Collect the Manna

How to Play:

  1. Scatter paper “manna” pieces on the floor.

  2. Each child may pick up only enough for one portion.

  3. Talk about how God gave just enough each day.

Lesson Point: God gives us what we need each day.

Game 2: Thank You for Daily Bread

How to Play:

  1. Sit in a circle.

  2. Each child says one food they thank God for.

  3. End with a prayer of thanks.

Lesson Point: We should be thankful for God’s provision.
