Joseph Reveals Himself to his Brothers

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 2: Mighty Men of God

Aim

To show the children that Joseph forgave his brothers completely.

Scripture Reading

Genesis 43:1 – 45:15

Memory Verse

Ephesians 4:32

Be kind and compassionate to one another, forgiving each other, just as in Christ God forgave you.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Have you ever heard anyone say, “I will never forgive this”? It’s a very sad thing to hear. People may say it when they believe something very bad has been done to them. This is not what Joseph told his brothers. He tested them when they came to buy grain in Egypt. Show picture 10. They returned home with sacks of grain, but they had to leave their brother Simeon behind in prison.

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. When the food the brothers had brought back was finished, Jacob wanted his sons to return to Egypt to buy some more. The famine was still continuing.“We cannot go,” said the brothers, “without taking Benjamin along.” You could act the different parts of Jacob and the sons arguing with each other (43:1-7).

2. Finally, Judah – the one who had first suggested selling Joseph (Gen 37:26-27) – said he would give his life in Benjamin’s place if anything were to happen to Jacob’s youngest son. Judah certainly had changed! So they prepared some gifts for the Egyptian governor, and Benjamin left home with his brothers (43:11-15).

3. Joseph was very glad to see his brothers, and especially Benjamin. Benjamin was his only blood brother because their mother, Rachel, had only the two of them. Remind the class that Jacob’s other children were born of Leah, his other wife, and the two slave women Bilhah and Zilpah. Joseph treated them well and sent them all home with sacks of grain (43:16-34; 44:3).

4. Shortly after the brothers had left Joseph sent his servant after them to accuse them of stealing Joseph’s silver cup. They denied knowing anything about such a cup, but to their complete surprise it was found in Benjamin’s sack. Joseph wanted to test his brothers. Would they take care of Benjamin and stand by him, or would they run away to save themselves (44:1-13)?

5. Judah begged Joseph to keep him as his slave instead of taking Benjamin from their father (44:14-34).

6. Joseph knew then that his brothers had changed, so he told them who he was (45:1-4, 14). Show picture 11.

Our Response to the Story

Joseph was able to forgive his brothers (45:5-8). Where there is no forgiveness there is always separation. How did Joseph manage to forgive his brothers? Joseph had God’s Spirit within him. Review the memory verse for Lesson 9. God has forgiven us because of Jesus’ sacrifice, so we can forgive others. Do you know that God offers us forgiveness? Do you in your turn forgive others, or do you say, “I will never forgive you”? That is not what God told us, and that is not what God wants us to tell others.

Lesson - 11: JOSEPH REVEALS HIMSELF TO HIS BROTHERS

Scripture Reading: Genesis 43:1 – 45:15

1. Forgive or Take Revenge?

Write F for forgive or R for revenge:

  1. Say “I will never forgive you.” ( ___ ) 👉 R

  2. Forgive as Joseph did. ( ___ ) 👉 F

  3. Pay back evil with evil. ( ___ ) 👉 R

  4. Be kind and compassionate. ( ___ ) 👉 F

2. Put the Story in Order

a) Joseph’s silver cup was found in Benjamin’s sack.
b) Joseph said, “I am Joseph.”
c) The brothers returned to Egypt with Benjamin.
d) Judah offered to stay in Benjamin’s place.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. The brothers returned to Egypt with Benjamin.

  2. Joseph’s silver cup was found in Benjamin’s sack.

  3. Judah offered to stay in Benjamin’s place.

  4. Joseph said, “I am Joseph.”

3. Who Am I?

Choose from: Judah, Joseph, Simeon

  1. I stayed in prison in Egypt. Who am I? __________ 👉 Simeon

  2. I offered to become a slave for Benjamin. Who am I? __________ 👉 Judah

  3. I forgave my brothers. Who am I? __________ 👉 Joseph

4. Discussion Questions

  1. How did Joseph show forgiveness?

    Sample answer: He told his brothers that God had sent him ahead, and he welcomed them.

  2. Why do we forgive others?

    Sample answer: Because God has forgiven us through Jesus.

  3. Who do you need to forgive?

    Sample answer: Answers will vary.

5. Memory Verse:

Ephesians 4:32 — “Be kind and compassionate to one another, forgiving each other, just as in Christ God forgave you.”

Colossians 3:13 — “Forgive as the Lord forgave you.”

Craft 1: Silver Cup

Materials: Cardboard cup or paper cup, silver foil, glue

Instructions:

  1. Cover a paper cup with silver foil.

  2. Add a handle from card.

  3. Write on it: “Joseph forgave.”

Lesson Point: Joseph forgave his brothers completely.

Craft 2: Mended Heart

Materials: Red paper, scissors, tape

Instructions:

  1. Cut a heart and cut it in two jagged pieces.

  2. Tape the pieces back together.

  3. Write: “Forgiveness mends.”

Lesson Point: Where there is forgiveness, there is peace.

Game 1: Pass the Cup

How to Play:

  1. Sit in a circle and pass a cup.

  2. Whoever holds it says “I forgive you” to the next child.

  3. Continue around the circle.

Lesson Point: We can forgive as God forgave us.

Game 2: Heart Puzzle

How to Play:

  1. Cut a large heart into pieces and mix them.

  2. Teams race to put the heart together.

  3. Talk about how forgiveness brings people back together.

Lesson Point: Forgiveness restores broken relationships.
