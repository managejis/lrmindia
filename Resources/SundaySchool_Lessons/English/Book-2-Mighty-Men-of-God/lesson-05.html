Joseph’s Dreams

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 2: Mighty Men of God

Aim

To teach the children that God had a plan for Joseph’s life – and for our lives, too.

Scripture Reading

Genesis 37:1-11

Memory Verse

Ephesians 1:4

[God] chose us in him before the creation of the world …

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Ask the class if anyone had a dream the night before. What was it about? Sometimes our dreams do not make much sense. Today’s Bible story is about someone who dreamed. His dreams upset his whole family. His brothers even started calling him “that dreamer”.

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. Joseph was Jacob’s eldest son with Rachel, the wife he loved best. Do you remember from last time that God gave Jacob a new name? It was Israel. Show picture 4.

Joseph was one of Israel’s 12 sons. He was not popular with his brothers. Explain why (37:2). He told tales about his brothers to his father.

With his father, on the other hand, Joseph was very popular. This made his brothers jealous. Their father also gave Joseph a special grand robe. This worsened the jealousy and bad feelings (v 3-4).

2. Then Joseph had a dream in which they bowed before him. He told his brothers about it (v 5-7). Show picture 5. They were angry.“Do you think you are going to reign over us?” they asked (v 8).

3. Joseph had a second dream, which was even more shocking (v 9). This time his father was angry with him, too (v 10). Parents are not supposed to bow down or pay respect like this to one of their children, and in those times elder brothers were considered to be above the younger ones in rank. Jacob couldn’t put Joseph’s dreams out of his mind.

Our Response to the Story

God had a plan for Joseph’s life. But Joseph was not a very nice person, it seems. It also wasn’t exactly sensible of him to boast about those dreams. God had to change him in order to work out his plan in Joseph’s life.

God has a plan for our lives even before we are born. Hear what the memory verse says.

Lesson - 5: JOSEPH’S DREAMS

Scripture Reading: Genesis 37:1-11

1. Count and Write

Write the number:

  1. Israel (Jacob) had ____ sons. __________ 👉 12

  2. In the second dream, ____ stars bowed to Joseph. __________ 👉 11

  3. Joseph’s father gave him a special ____________. __________ 👉 robe

2. Match the Following

A B
The brothers Were jealous
Jacob Loved Joseph
Joseph Had two dreams
The robe A special gift

Answers:
The brothers → Were jealous
Jacob → Loved Joseph
Joseph → Had two dreams
The robe → A special gift

3. Yes or No

Write Yes or No:

  1. Did God have a plan for Joseph? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

  2. Was it wise to boast about the dreams? ( ___ ) 👉 No

  3. Does God have a plan for you? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why were Joseph’s brothers jealous?

    Sample answer: Their father loved Joseph more and gave him a special robe.

  2. What was wrong with how Joseph shared his dreams?

    Sample answer: He boasted, which made his family angry.

  3. What plan does God have for your life?

    Sample answer: To know Him and serve Him.

5. Memory Verse:

Ephesians 1:4 — “God chose us in him before the creation of the world.”

Jeremiah 29:11 — “For I know the plans I have for you, declares the Lord, plans to prosper you and not to harm you.”

Craft 1: Coat of Many Colours

Materials: Paper, crayons or coloured paper strips, glue

Instructions:

  1. Cut a coat shape from paper.

  2. Glue coloured strips across the coat.

  3. Write: “God has a plan.”

Lesson Point: God had a special plan for Joseph, and for us.

Craft 2: Sun, Moon and Stars Card

Materials: Dark paper, yellow paper, glue

Instructions:

  1. Cut a sun, moon and eleven stars.

  2. Glue them on dark paper.

  3. Write: “Joseph’s dream.”

Lesson Point: Joseph’s dreams showed God’s plan.

Game 1: Dream Charades

How to Play:

  1. One child acts out a dream or a picture while others guess.

  2. Take turns.

  3. Say what the dream might mean.

Lesson Point: God spoke to Joseph through dreams.

Game 2: Colour Robe Relay

How to Play:

  1. Place coloured paper strips at one end of the room.

  2. Teams race to collect one strip at a time to make a robe.

  3. The first team to finish wins.

Lesson Point: Jacob’s special gift made the brothers jealous.
