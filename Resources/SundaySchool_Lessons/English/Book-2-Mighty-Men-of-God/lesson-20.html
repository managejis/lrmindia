The Snake in the Desert

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 2: Mighty Men of God

Aim

To teach the children that disobedience to God must be punished by death, but that God has provided us with a way of salvation.

Scripture Reading

Numbers 21:4-9; John 3:14-16

Memory Verse

Romans 6:23

For the wages of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Make up a story about a boy who climbed up a mango tree to pick some of the fruit. This tree did not belong to his family, but the mangoes were ripe so he decided to steal them. Then suddenly, the owner appeared. When the boy saw the owner he had a fright and fell from the tree. He hurt himself.

When we disobey God we sin. It’s like falling down instead of continuing forwards. When we fall we are hurt.

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. God took care of the people of Israel on their journey to the Promised Land.

Show pictures 18 and 29. Briefly review how God cared for them by asking questions, referring to the pictures.

2. God provided food and water for them and he gave them rules for living together in peace, but still they grumbled. They were ungrateful and they kept on doubting that God would take care of them (Num 21:5).

3. As punishment, God sent snakes among them (v 6).

4. The people of Israel understood that they had sinned and they repented (v 7).

5. On God’s instruction Moses made a bronze snake and put it up on a pole (v 8). Show picture 20.

6. When a person who had been bitten by a snake looked at the bronze snake, he or she was healed (v 9). God had provided a way to save their lives.

Our Response to the Story

Jesus said that because of our disobedience he would be lifted up like this snake was lifted up in the desert (Jn 3:14-15). He was indeed put up on a cross and he died for the sins of the world. Just as every sick Israelite needed to look up at the bronze snake in order to be healed, we need to look up at the cross and accept Jesus as the only One who can save us from sin and death.

Lesson - 20: THE SNAKE IN THE DESERT

Scripture Reading: Numbers 21:4-9; John 3:14-16

1. Put the Story in Order

a) Moses made a bronze snake on a pole.
b) The people grumbled against God.
c) Those who looked at it were healed.
d) God sent snakes among them.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. The people grumbled against God.

  2. God sent snakes among them.

  3. Moses made a bronze snake on a pole.

  4. Those who looked at it were healed.

2. Yes or No

Write Yes or No:

  1. Did the Israelites sin by grumbling? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

  2. Did God give a way to be saved? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

  3. Do we need to look to Jesus on the cross? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

3. Complete the Sentences

Word box: pole, grumbled, healed

  1. The people ____________ against God. __________ 👉 grumbled

  2. The bronze snake was lifted up on a ____________. __________ 👉 pole

  3. Everyone who looked at it was ____________. __________ 👉 healed

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did God send the snakes?

    Sample answer: The people were ungrateful and sinned.

  2. How were the people saved?

    Sample answer: They looked up at the bronze snake as God said.

  3. How does Jesus save us?

    Sample answer: He was lifted up on the cross, and we look to Him to be saved.

5. Memory Verse:

Romans 6:23 — “For the wages of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord.”

Isaiah 45:22 — “Turn to me and be saved.”

Craft 1: Snake on a Pole

Materials: Craft stick, paper, crayons, glue

Instructions:

  1. Draw and cut a snake from paper.

  2. Glue it to the top of a craft stick.

  3. Write: “Look and live.”

Lesson Point: God gave a way to be saved.

Craft 2: Look Up Cross Card

Materials: Folded card, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Draw a big cross on the front.

  2. Inside write: “Look to Jesus.”

  3. Decorate with stars.

Lesson Point: We look up to Jesus on the cross to be saved.

Game 1: Look Up and Live

How to Play:

  1. Children crawl or walk around the room.

  2. When you say “Look!” they stop and look up at a picture on the wall.

  3. Talk about how the Israelites looked at the snake.

Lesson Point: God’s way to be saved is simple: look and believe.

Game 2: Grumble or Thank?

How to Play:

  1. Call out situations, such as “Dinner is late”.

  2. Children respond with a grumble face or a thankful face.

  3. Talk about why thanks pleases God.

Lesson Point: Be thankful, not ungrateful.
