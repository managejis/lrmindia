Jesus is Alive Today

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 2: Mighty Men of God

Aim

To teach the children that Jesus returned to his Father, but will come again one day.

Scripture Reading

Acts 1:1-11

Memory Verse

Matthew 24:44

So you also must be ready, because the Son of Man will come at an hour when you do not expect him.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

What do you do in your family if someone is going away? Possible responses: Clothes are washed and preparations made for the journey. Everyone listens to the last words the person has to say.

What do you do in your family if someone is coming to your house? Possible responses: We tidy the house. We prepare enough food. We keep watch to see him or her coming.

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. Show picture 24. The Lord Jesus was returning to his Father in heaven. His followers were sad that he was leaving them. Three days after he died Jesus came back to life and all of them were full of joy. Jesus had defeated death!

2. Recount Acts 1:2-4 in your own words. After his resurrection Jesus met with his disciples many times. He kept on teaching them. He promised them the gift of God’s power, the Holy Spirit.

3. In this book, we talked about Moses. He was a great leader and teacher. By showing pictures 17, 18 and 19, briefly review how Moses led and helped the people of Israel. When he had grown old, God took him to heaven. Jesus also returned to God the Father in heaven. However, Jesus is far greater than Moses. He is the Son of God. He himself is God and shows us exactly what God the Father is like. Moses saved the people of Israel when he led them through the Red Sea, but Jesus came to save everybody who will follow him. Jesus made a promise to return to this world. He is coming to take those who believe in him to their true home, to live with God for ever. Sing a song about Jesus’ return.

4. Put verses 9-11 into your own words. After Jesus had been taken into heaven, the men in white came to comfort the disciples. They told them what to do now that Jesus was gone.

Our Response to the Story

How should we live while we wait for Jesus to return? We can eagerly watch out for his coming, pray about it and witness to it. To witness means to tell others about Jesus so that they, too, will expect him. Link your explanation to the introductory questions and answers.

Lesson - 24: JESUS IS ALIVE TODAY

Scripture Reading: Acts 1:1-11

1. Put the Story in Order

a) Jesus was taken up into heaven.
b) Jesus rose again on the third day.
c) Two men in white spoke to the disciples.
d) Jesus taught His disciples many times.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. Jesus rose again on the third day.

  2. Jesus taught His disciples many times.

  3. Jesus was taken up into heaven.

  4. Two men in white spoke to the disciples.

2. Match the Following

A B
Moses Led Israel through the Red Sea
Jesus Saves all who follow Him
The Holy Spirit The gift of God’s power
The men in white Comforted the disciples

Answers:
Moses → Led Israel through the Red Sea
Jesus → Saves all who follow Him
The Holy Spirit → The gift of God’s power
The men in white → Comforted the disciples

3. Ready or Not Ready?

Write R for ready for Jesus or N for not ready:

  1. Praying and watching for Jesus. ( ___ ) 👉 R

  2. Forgetting Jesus’ promise. ( ___ ) 👉 N

  3. Doing wrong when nobody is looking. ( ___ ) 👉 N

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Where did Jesus go?

    Sample answer: He returned to His Father in heaven.

  2. What did Jesus promise?

    Sample answer: He will come back to take believers to be with Him forever.

  3. How should we live while we wait?

    Sample answer: By watching, praying and telling others about Jesus.

5. Memory Verse:

Matthew 24:44 — “So you also must be ready, because the Son of Man will come at an hour when you do not expect him.”

John 14:3 — “I will come back and take you to be with me.”

Craft 1: Clouds and Jesus Going Up

Materials: Blue paper, cotton wool, white paper, glue

Instructions:

  1. Glue cotton wool clouds on blue paper.

  2. Cut a small figure from white paper and glue it above the clouds.

  3. Write: “Jesus is coming again.”

Lesson Point: Jesus returned to His Father and will come again.

Craft 2: Watch for Jesus Binoculars

Materials: Two toilet-roll tubes, paper, tape, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Tape two tubes side by side.

  2. Decorate them with paper and crayons.

  3. Look through them and say: “I am ready.”

Lesson Point: We watch and wait for Jesus to return.

Game 1: Get Ready for a Visitor

How to Play:

  1. Tell the children a special visitor is coming.

  2. They tidy the room and set out “food”.

  3. Talk about how we get ready for Jesus.

Lesson Point: We should be ready for Jesus’ return.

Game 2: Watchman Lookout

How to Play:

  1. One child stands on a chair or high place as the lookout.

  2. The others sit and wait, then cheer when the lookout calls “He is coming!”

  3. Take turns.

Lesson Point: We watch for Jesus’ coming.
