Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 2: Mighty Men of God

Aim

To show the children how God protected a small baby called Moses against a mighty king.

Scripture Reading

Exodus 1:8 – 2:10

Memory Verse

Isaiah 41:10

I am your God. I will strengthen you and help you …

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Many Sudanese Christians have experienced danger recently. Several thousand people, including many children, walked all the way from Tori in Northern Ghana to Juba in Southern Sudan. On the journey, Taban, the youngest in a group of children, lagged a little behind his brother and sister. They were sent back to fetch him and so escaped being blown up by a landmine, which hit the others they had been with.

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. Jacob’s family, who had been in danger of starving in their homeland, had become a large and prosperous tribe in Egypt. Now it was hundreds of years later; Joseph was dead and another Pharaoh ruled in Egypt. Jacob’s family was in danger once again. Describe how the people of Israel became the king of Egypt’s slaves and how he ordered their newborn baby boys to be killed (1:8-22).

2. Moses was born in these times. His family hid him from Pharaoh’s soldiers for three months, but then he became too big to hide. His mother put the baby in a watertight basket among the reeds that grew along the River Nile. Miriam, the baby’s sister, hid close by to watch what would happen (2:1-4).

3. Pharaoh’s daughter came to the river to bathe and saw the basket. She found the crying baby inside (2:5-6). Show picture 13. In the picture, Miriam is just coming to offer help.

4. Miriam offered to find the princess a woman to nurse the baby. She came back with the baby’s own mother (2:7-9)!

5. As the adopted son of Pharaoh’s daughter, Moses grew up in the king’s house. There he received a good education and learnt all about the Egyptians’ way of thinking.

Instil the Memory Verse

Our Response to the Story

God protected Moses hundreds of years ago, and he protected Taban and his family on their dangerous walk only a few years ago. God keeps us safe. He does not always take the danger away, but he always is with us all the way.

If you can share your own experience of God’s protection this will make the message even more real to the children.

Lesson - 13: BABY MOSES

Scripture Reading: Exodus 1:8 – 2:10

1. Who Am I?

Choose from: Moses, Miriam, Pharaoh’s daughter

  1. I hid and watched the basket. Who am I? __________ 👉 Miriam

  2. I found the crying baby. Who am I? __________ 👉 Pharaoh’s daughter

  3. I was put in a basket on the Nile. Who am I? __________ 👉 Moses

2. Put the Story in Order

a) The princess found the baby.
b) Pharaoh ordered baby boys to be killed.
c) She put Moses in a basket on the river.
d) Moses’ mother hid him for three months.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. Pharaoh ordered baby boys to be killed.

  2. Moses’ mother hid him for three months.

  3. She put Moses in a basket on the river.

  4. The princess found the baby.

3. Odd One Out

Circle the one that does not belong:

  1. basket – reeds – river – mountain 👉 mountain

  2. Miriam – Moses – princess – Noah 👉 Noah

  3. hid – protected – saved – lost 👉 lost

4. Discussion Questions

  1. How did God protect baby Moses?

    Sample answer: He kept Moses safe in the basket and arranged for the princess to find him.

  2. What did Miriam do?

    Sample answer: She watched and offered to find a nurse, who was Moses’ own mother.

  3. When has God kept you safe?

    Sample answer: Answers will vary.

5. Memory Verse:

Isaiah 41:10 — “I am your God. I will strengthen you and help you.”

Psalm 91:11 — “He will command his angels concerning you to guard you in all your ways.”

Craft 1: Basket Boat

Materials: Paper plate or brown paper, crayons, small doll or paper baby

Instructions:

  1. Cut a paper plate in half and colour it brown to look like a basket.

  2. Draw or place a baby inside.

  3. Write: “God keeps me safe.”

Lesson Point: God protected baby Moses against a mighty king.

Craft 2: River Nile Scene

Materials: Blue and green paper, crayons, glue

Instructions:

  1. Glue blue strips for the river.

  2. Add green reeds along the side.

  3. Draw a small basket among the reeds.

Lesson Point: God watches over small children.

Game 1: Hide and Seek Baby Moses

How to Play:

  1. One child hides a toy baby in the room.

  2. The others search quietly while one child “Miriam” watches.

  3. Whoever finds it leads the next round.

Lesson Point: God hid Moses safely.

Game 2: Floating Boat

How to Play:

  1. Fill a tub with water.

  2. Children float paper or foil boats and add small weights.

  3. Talk about how the basket stayed afloat because God was caring for Moses.

Lesson Point: God protects us in dangerous times.
