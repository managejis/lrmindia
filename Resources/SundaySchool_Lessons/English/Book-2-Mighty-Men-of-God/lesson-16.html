The Passover

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 2: Mighty Men of God

Aim

To show the children how God saved his people’s lives in Egypt by the sacrifice of a lamb … and how God has saved us by the sacrifice of the Lamb of God, who is Jesus Christ.

Scripture Reading

Exodus 12:1-14, 29-36; 1 Peter 2:24-25

Memory Verse

Exodus 12:13

“[W]hen I see the blood, I will pass over you … when I strike Egypt.”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Talk about the special meals or feasts that are prepared and eaten in the children’s families at certain times. What are the special occasions you mark in this way?

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. God instructed Moses to tell the people of Israel about a special meal they were to prepare on a special day. They had to prepare just the right amount and do it in just the right way (Ex 12:1-5).

2. Everyone was to kill the sheep or goat they had chosen on the same day and smear some of the animal’s blood on the door-frames of their houses (12:6-7). Show picture 16. The class could act this part of the story.

3. The meat was to be cooked quickly and all leftovers were to be burned. Everyone was to be ready to set out on a journey (12:8-11).

4. At midnight all the firstborn in Egypt died: the eldest son of Pharaoh and the eldest male child of all his people and of their livestock. But wherever God saw the blood on the doorframe of an Israelite house he passed over it. That is why this feast is called Passover (v 12-14, 29). Show picture 16 again.

5. The Egyptians were so afraid that they begged the Israelites to leave; they even gave them gifts to take with them (v 30-36).

6. Many years after this God sent his own Son, Jesus Christ, to this world. Like the Passover lambs that lost their lives in order to save the Israelites from death, Christ gave his life to save us from sin and death. Show picture 23. Sin is like slavery, because it holds us in bondage from which we cannot free ourselves. The people of Israel had to be delivered from slavery in Egypt and a lamb was sacrificed to save them from death. Jesus is the Lamb of God, whose blood saves us from death and who delivers us from slavery to sin.

Our Response to the Story

There once was a man who was a thief. The police caught and arrested him. He was taken before a judge, who ordered a very large fine to be paid before the thief could be set free. The man could not pay this fine himself, but his father came and paid it for him. In order to pay the fine that would buy his son’s freedom, the father had to sell all his cattle.

Jesus sacrificed all he had in order to wipe out the fine (or debt) we should have paid for our sins. Lead the class in a prayer to thank the Lord Jesus for his sacrifice for us.

Lesson - 16: THE PASSOVER

Scripture Reading: Exodus 12:1-14, 29-36; 1 Peter 2:24-25

1. Put the Story in Order

a) The Egyptians told the Israelites to leave.
b) God told the Israelites to choose a lamb.
c) God passed over the houses with blood.
d) They put the blood on their door-frames.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. God told the Israelites to choose a lamb.

  2. They put the blood on their door-frames.

  3. God passed over the houses with blood.

  4. The Egyptians told the Israelites to leave.

2. Match the Following

A B
The lamb Jesus, the Lamb of God
Blood on the door Saved from death
Passover God passed over the houses
Slavery Sin

Answers:
The lamb → Jesus, the Lamb of God
Blood on the door → Saved from death
Passover → God passed over the houses
Slavery → Sin

3. Complete the Sentences

Word box: blood, Lamb, Passover

  1. God passed over the houses with ____________. __________ 👉 blood

  2. Jesus is the ____________ of God. __________ 👉 Lamb

  3. This feast is called ____________. __________ 👉 Passover

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did the Israelites put blood on their doors?

    Sample answer: God said He would pass over the houses with blood.

  2. How is Jesus like the Passover lamb?

    Sample answer: He gave His life to save us from sin and death.

  3. Why can we thank Jesus?

    Sample answer: He paid for our sins and sets us free.

5. Memory Verse:

Exodus 12:13 — “When I see the blood, I will pass over you.”

John 1:29 — “Look, the Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world!”

Craft 1: Door Frame Picture

Materials: Paper, red crayon or paint, glue

Instructions:

  1. Draw a door with a frame.

  2. Paint a red line across the top and down the sides.

  3. Write: “God saves.”

Lesson Point: The blood on the door saved the Israelites.

Craft 2: Lamb of God Card

Materials: Folded card, cotton wool, crayons, glue

Instructions:

  1. Draw a lamb on the front and glue cotton wool on its body.

  2. Inside write: “Jesus is the Lamb of God.”

  3. Decorate with stars.

Lesson Point: Jesus is the Lamb who saves us.

Game 1: Pass Over

How to Play:

  1. Lay a rope or ribbon on the floor as the “door-frame”.

  2. Children walk past and jump over it.

  3. Talk about how God passed over the houses.

Lesson Point: God protected the people who obeyed Him.

Game 2: Ready to Go

How to Play:

  1. Say “Time to go!” and children race to put on shoes, take a bag and line up.

  2. See who is ready first.

  3. Talk about how Israel had to be ready to leave Egypt.

Lesson Point: God’s people were ready to follow Him.
