Jacob Meets God Face to Face

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 2: Mighty Men of God

Aim

To teach the children that Jacob was a changed person after he had met God. Jacob learnt that God is faithful and full of grace.

Scripture Reading

Genesis 32:3 – 33:4

Memory Verse

Philippians 1:6

[H]e who began a good work in you will carry it on to completion until the day of Christ Jesus.

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Make up a story about having to meet someone of whom you are afraid, for example the headmaster at a new school. You could also recount an incident from your own life.

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. Jacob was going back home with his new family. It was a large household (Gen 32:10, 22). Describe it. He was going to see his brother Esau again. Jacob was afraid of this, because he had not treated Esau well. Ask the class if they remember what he had done. Show picture 1. He had deceived his father and cheated his brother out of his birthright.

2. Jacob sent messengers to Esau. He was frightened by the news they brought back (v 3-6): Esau was coming to meet him with an army by his side.

3. When he heard this Jacob divided his family into two groups (v 7-8).

4. Then Jacob prayed. He reminded God that God had promised to protect him. He knew that he did not deserve God’s grace (v 10). This was the first time Jacob admitted that God had been faithful and good to him (v 9-12).

5. Jacob prepared gifts for his brother Esau and sent his family to a safe place (v 13-22).

6. Jacob remained behind all alone. That night, he wrestled with an unknown man till daybreak. Show picture 4. Before the man departed, he changed Jacob’s name to Israel, which means: “He struggles with God.” Jacob was left with a limp to remind him that he had met God personally (v 23-31).

7. The next day Jacob met Esau, who greeted him warmly, to his great surprise (33:1-4).

Instil the Memory Verse

God did not forsake Jacob, even though he was a sinner.

Our Response to the Story

Jacob used to trick people to get what he wanted. He had to learn this was not the right way. He could trust in God instead of relying on his own schemes.

Can you tell a story from your own experience of how God showed that he is trustworthy?

Lesson - 4: JACOB MEETS GOD FACE TO FACE

Scripture Reading: Genesis 32:3 – 33:4

1. Put the Story in Order

a) Jacob prayed to God.
b) Esau hugged Jacob.
c) Jacob heard that Esau was coming.
d) Jacob wrestled all night.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. Jacob heard that Esau was coming.

  2. Jacob prayed to God.

  3. Jacob wrestled all night.

  4. Esau hugged Jacob.

2. Match the Following

A B
Israel Jacob’s new name
A limp A reminder he met God
Gifts Sent to Esau
Esau Greeted Jacob warmly

Answers:
Israel → Jacob’s new name
A limp → A reminder he met God
Gifts → Sent to Esau
Esau → Greeted Jacob warmly

3. Yes or No

Write Yes or No:

  1. Was Jacob afraid to meet Esau? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

  2. Did Jacob pray to God? ( ___ ) 👉 Yes

  3. Did Esau come to hurt Jacob? ( ___ ) 👉 No

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why was Jacob afraid of Esau?

    Sample answer: Jacob had cheated him long before.

  2. What did Jacob do when he was afraid?

    Sample answer: He prayed and trusted in God.

  3. How can God change us?

    Sample answer: He helps us stop wrong ways and trust Him.

5. Memory Verse:

Philippians 1:6 — “He who began a good work in you will carry it on to completion until the day of Christ Jesus.”

2 Corinthians 5:17 — “If anyone is in Christ, the new creation has come!”

Craft 1: New Name Badge

Materials: Card circle, crayons, safety pin or tape

Instructions:

  1. Write ISRAEL in big letters on the card circle.

  2. Decorate it with stars.

  3. Wear it and say: “God changes me.”

Lesson Point: God gave Jacob a new name and a new life.

Craft 2: Brothers Hugging Picture

Materials: Paper, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Draw Jacob and Esau hugging.

  2. Colour the picture.

  3. Write: “God brings peace.”

Lesson Point: God can turn quarrels into friendship.

Game 1: Thumb Wrestle

How to Play:

  1. Pair the children for a gentle thumb wrestle.

  2. Talk about how Jacob struggled with God all night.

  3. Everyone shakes hands at the end.

Lesson Point: Jacob struggled, but God blessed and changed him.

Game 2: Say Sorry and Hug

How to Play:

  1. Two children act out saying sorry.

  2. The other forgives and gives a handshake or hug.

  3. The class claps for peace.

Lesson Point: Esau forgave Jacob and welcomed him.
