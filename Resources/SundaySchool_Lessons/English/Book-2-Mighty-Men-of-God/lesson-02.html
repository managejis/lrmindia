Jacob’s Dream

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 2: Mighty Men of God

Aim

To teach the children that Jacob was a deceiver; nonetheless, God came to him and began changing him.

Scripture Reading

Genesis 27:1-45; 28:10-22

Memory Verse

Genesis 28:15

I am with you and will watch over you wherever you go …

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Show picture 1. Revise last week’s story by asking the class questions like the following:

Q: Who are these two men? A: Esau and Jacob.

Q. What did Esau do for a living? A: He was a hunter.

Q: What kind of work did Jacob do? A: He helped around the house.

Q: What did Jacob want? A: Esau’s blessing as the firstborn. Q: What was Esau asking for? A: Food.

Q: What kind of person was Jacob? A: He deceived Esau and his father, Isaac. (Make sure the meaning of “deceived” is clear.)

Q: Did Esau and Jacob obey the memory verse? A: No.

Revise Proverbs 3:6, the memory verse for Lesson 1.

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. Begin by telling the story of Genesis 27 in your own words. Isaac, Esau and Jacob’s father, was very old by now. He had become blind. He wanted to give the eldest son, Esau, his blessing before he died. So he called for Esau to come to him (27:1-4).

2. Rebecca overheard Isaac talking to Esau. When Esau had gone to hunt for the wild game Isaac wanted, she called Jacob and prepared him to receive the blessing in Esau’s place (27:5-17).

3. Jacob went to his father. He pretended he was Esau. He deceived Isaac and received the blessing of the firstborn, even though he was the second son (27:18-29).

4. Esau returned from the hunt and went to see his father. He was very angry and sad when he heard Jacob had received the blessing that should have been his (27:30-41).

5. Jacob had to go away. It was not safe for him to stay after he had deceived his father and his brother. He had to go and live with his uncle, his mother’s brother Laban (27:42-44).

6. Jacob set out on his journey. While he was resting at night God spoke to him in a dream (28:10-15). Show picture 2.

Even though Jacob was a deceiver and had done something very wicked, the way to God was open to him (v 12). God gave Jacob a promise, which is today’s memory verse.

7. When Jacob woke up, he knew God would be with him everywhere he went. He made a promise to worship God and serve God with everything he had. He put up a stone to remind him that God had met him in that place (28:16-22).

Instil the Memory Verse

Our Response to the Story

Jacob had done wrong, but still God spoke to him. Even when we have done wrong God will not turn his back on us. After God had spoken to him, Jacob wanted to change his ways. The Lord expects us to change our ways, too, once we have become his followers. Give examples of what kinds of behaviour need to change in our lives. Suggest things that will remind the children of today’s story, like lying and cheating.

Lesson - 2: JACOB’S DREAM

Scripture Reading: Genesis 27:1-45; 28:10-22

1. Put the Story in Order

a) Jacob dreamed of a ladder to heaven.
b) Jacob pretended to be Esau.
c) Isaac wanted to bless Esau.
d) Jacob ran away to his uncle Laban.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. Isaac wanted to bless Esau.

  2. Jacob pretended to be Esau.

  3. Jacob ran away to his uncle Laban.

  4. Jacob dreamed of a ladder to heaven.

2. Match the Following

A B
Isaac Old and blind father
Rebecca Helped Jacob trick Isaac
Esau Very angry and sad
The ladder Reached up to heaven

Answers:
Isaac → Old and blind father
Rebecca → Helped Jacob trick Isaac
Esau → Very angry and sad
The ladder → Reached up to heaven

3. Complete the Sentences

Word box: stone, promise, deceiver

  1. At first, Jacob was a ____________. __________ 👉 deceiver

  2. God gave Jacob a ____________ in his dream. __________ 👉 promise

  3. Jacob set up a ____________ to remember God. __________ 👉 stone

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why did Jacob have to go away?

    Sample answer: Esau was angry, and it was not safe for Jacob to stay.

  2. Why is it special that God spoke to Jacob?

    Sample answer: Jacob had done wrong, but God still came to him.

  3. What wrong things should we stop doing?

    Sample answer: Lying and cheating.

5. Memory Verse:

Genesis 28:15 — “I am with you and will watch over you wherever you go.”

Hebrews 13:5 — “Never will I leave you; never will I forsake you.”

Craft 1: Jacob’s Ladder

Materials: Two long paper strips, short paper strips, glue, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Glue two long strips side by side.

  2. Glue short strips across to make steps.

  3. Draw small angels on the steps.

  4. Write: “God is with me.”

Lesson Point: God is with us wherever we go.

Craft 2: Stone Pillar Reminder

Materials: A smooth stone, paint or markers

Instructions:

  1. Wash and dry a small smooth stone.

  2. Paint it a bright colour.

  3. Write: “God is here.”

  4. Keep it where you can see it.

Lesson Point: Jacob set up a stone to remember where he met God.

Game 1: Climb the Ladder

How to Play:

  1. Draw a ladder with 5 steps on the floor with chalk or tape.

  2. Each child steps up one rung and says a thing they thank God for.

  3. At the top they say “God is with me.”

Lesson Point: God hears us and promises to be with us.

Game 2: Who Is It?

How to Play:

  1. A blindfolded child guesses who is speaking when another child says a short sentence in a disguised voice.

  2. Talk about how Jacob pretended to be Esau.

  3. Say why it is wrong to trick others.

Lesson Point: God wants us to be honest.
