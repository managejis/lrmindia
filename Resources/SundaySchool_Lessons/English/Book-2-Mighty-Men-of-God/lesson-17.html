God Saves the Israelites at the Red Sea

Download PDF Download Word

Teacher’s Guide · Look, Listen & Live – Book 2: Mighty Men of God

Aim

To show the children that God performed a great miracle that day when he saved the people of Israel from the Egyptian army.

Scripture Reading

Exodus 14:5-31

Memory Verse

Exodus 14:13

“Do not be afraid. Stand firm and you will see the deliverance the Lord will bring you today.”

Presenting the Lesson

Starting point

Think of some events in your life that you will never forget because they are very important to you. Tell the class about one or two of them.

Main Points of Today’s Teaching

1. The people of Israel now were about to experience something that they would never forget. They would thank God for it eternally. They had left Egypt and had started on their journey to the Promised Land. There probably were at least two million of them, and then there were their animals, too. They travelled through the desert. God showed them the way. During the day a pillar of cloud shielded them from the sun. By night, a pillar of fire gave them light so that they could travel (13:21).

2. In the meantime the Egyptians regretted that they had let their slaves go. Pharaoh and his army prepared to set out and make them come back (v 5-9).

3. The Egyptian army caught up with the Israelites just when they had reached the Red Sea. The people of Israel were caught between the sea and the Egyptians. They could not cross the sea. They could not go round it, because there were mountains on each side. They could not go back, because the Egyptians were waiting for them. There was no escape. They were frightened. They also were very angry with Moses; they said it was all his fault for leading them out of Egypt (v 10-12).

4. Moses was not afraid. He trusted God. He knew that God would save them – that there was no need to run away in fright. Say the words of the memory verse.

5. Describe in your own words what happens in verses 19-20: a) The cloud moved from its position in front of the Isralites to behind them, thereby hiding them from the Egyptians.

b) Moses stretched out his hand over the sea and a strong wind started blowing the waters back all night.

c) The next day, the waters of the Red Sea had been separated and Moses led the people through on dry land. Show picture 17.

6. The Egyptians followed them, but God let the waters flow back again and the whole army drowned (v 23-28).

Our Response to the Story

The memory verse is true for us, too, who live thousands of years after Moses’ time. The following is a true story that happened in Juba (which is in the Sudan): A man who had no food for his family left in the house wrote a letter to a friend of his who was a pastor, asking for help. The pastor replied that he did not have any extra food. The only gift he could offer them was to pray for them. The family also had to ask for God’s help, he said. The man’s family prayed. The next day a relative returned some money that he had borrowed from them even though they had not reminded him of the debt, and every child in the family received a tin of beans from their school. God gave more than they needed.

Lesson - 17: GOD SAVES THE ISRAELITES AT THE RED SEA

Scripture Reading: Exodus 14:5-31

1. Put the Story in Order

a) The Israelites crossed on dry land.
b) The Egyptian army chased them.
c) The Israelites left Egypt.
d) Moses stretched out his hand over the sea.

👉 Correct Order:

  1. The Israelites left Egypt.

  2. The Egyptian army chased them.

  3. Moses stretched out his hand over the sea.

  4. The Israelites crossed on dry land.

2. Match the Following

A B
Pillar of cloud Shaded them by day
Pillar of fire Gave light at night
Moses Trusted God
The sea Opened for the people

Answers:
Pillar of cloud → Shaded them by day
Pillar of fire → Gave light at night
Moses → Trusted God
The sea → Opened for the people

3. Complete the Sentences

Word box: afraid, dry, Moses

  1. God told them, “Do not be ____________.” __________ 👉 afraid

  2. The Israelites walked through on ____________ land. __________ 👉 dry

  3. ____________ trusted God to save them. __________ 👉 Moses

4. Discussion Questions

  1. Why were the Israelites frightened?

    Sample answer: The sea was in front and the Egyptian army behind them.

  2. How did God save them?

    Sample answer: He opened a path through the sea.

  3. When can we trust God for help?

    Sample answer: Any time, even when there seems to be no way out.

5. Memory Verse:

Exodus 14:13 — “Do not be afraid. Stand firm and you will see the deliverance the Lord will bring you today.”

Joshua 1:9 — “Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid; do not be discouraged.”

Craft 1: Walls of Water

Materials: Blue paper strips, brown paper, glue, crayons

Instructions:

  1. Glue blue wavy strips on both sides of a card.

  2. Glue a brown path down the middle.

  3. Draw small people walking and write: “God makes a way.”

Lesson Point: God opened a way through the sea for His people.

Craft 2: Cloud and Fire Pillars

Materials: Cotton wool, orange and red paper, glue, card

Instructions:

  1. Glue cotton wool on one side of the card for the cloud.

  2. Glue orange and red flames on the other side for the fire.

  3. Write: “God leads us.”

Lesson Point: God led His people day and night.

Game 1: Parting the Sea

How to Play:

  1. Two lines of children make waves with their arms.

  2. One child “Moses” walks between the lines as they step apart.

  3. The waves come together when the child has passed.

Lesson Point: God divided the sea for His people.

Game 2: Do Not Be Afraid

How to Play:

  1. Children walk slowly across a dark or “scary” room.

  2. The teacher says “Do not be afraid” at each step.

  3. They arrive safely at the other side and cheer.

Lesson Point: God is with us in scary times.
