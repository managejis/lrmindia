2. Which Is the Real Temple?

Text of this pamphlet

Temple is a place of worship and each religious group takes pride in such structures. People spend lakhs of rupees for the building and upkeep of these centres, Further, such religious groups employ its might to "safeguard" their places of worship against "encroachment" by other groups.

Every religion claims superiority, creating violence and bloodshed. They betray and kill each other. Instead of love they breed hatred. Instead of harmony, they divide people against people,

Anyway, these, religious faction-fightings are neither a new phenomenon nor confined to India. For centuries the world has been witnessing bloodshed over worship places. Such rivalries are bound to increase in the future.

However, if you think rationally, you will admit that God cannot be confined to any religious structure made by man. Therefore, it is vital to understand who God is, and in which cemple He lives.

He is Creator — The Greatness of God

Every thinking person asks, "where did | come from? why am | here? and where am | going". Science attempts to answer the first question, philosophy wrestles with the second, and religions are baffled at the third.

But the Holy Bible gives satisfying answers to the above questions. It says, "In the beginning God created the eavens and the earth. God made the world and everything in it, and He is Lord of all that He has made." He is not a distant God, divorced from His creation, nor is He an imprisoned God, locked in creation. He is too great to be housed in man-made temples or pilgrimage centres. Therefore, it is foolish to fight over religious structures, as God would always dwell in the hearts of men and women..

God in the beginning created Adam and Eve in whom the Spirit of God was dwelling. They were the temples of the living God, enjoying perfect peace with the Creator. But, when they sinned against God, not only they ceased to be the temples of God, but became the temples of Satan. From that time, man is manifesting sin namely lying, cheating, pride, jealousy, suicide, violence, murder, adultery etc. Thus man lost his relationship with the holy God.

Yet, the Creator in His mercy desires to re-establish the broken relationship with mankind.

He is Provider — The Goodness of God

Weall pride ourselves in serving God through our rituals, offerings and sacrifices. But, rationally speaking, it is God who serves us. God is self-sufficient and needs nothing from man. God gives us life, breath and everything else that we enjoy today freely. Therefore, we have to give an account of our life to the living God. The greatest sin that we commit is that we worship the creation instead of the Creator

He is the Ruler — The Government of God

The Creator and Provider God is also the God who governs the universe. He created mankind from one man Adam so that all nations are made of the same stuff and have the same blood. He is the Ruler of all mankind. Yet He is not far from anyone of us. He wants us to make Him as our absolute Ruler. We should allow the living God to govern and rule our lives.

He is the Saviour — The Grace of God

This Creator God who cannot be confined in man-made temples appeared to mankind in history about 2000 years ago in the person of the Lord Jesus Christ. He was crucified for your sins, He was buried but rose again on the third day. He is your living saviour. Jesus Christ wants to dwell in your heart, making your body the temple of the living God. Therefore you need to establish a personal relationship with Jesus Christ today.

When religions are sinking in the quagmire of confusion and communal chaos, Jesus is inviting you,"come unto me all who are weary and heavy laden, and | will give you rest". It is the blood of Jesus Christ that can save you from your sins. You can become the Temple of the living God by confessing your sins and accepting Him as the Ruler of your life through the following prayer:

"LORD JESUS CHRIST, 1 AM A SINNER. I REPENT FOR MY SINS AND I INVITE YOU TO COME INTO MY LIFE AND MAKE ME YOUR TEMPLE"

When you honestly confess your sins and accept Jesus Christ as your Lord, the following will take place in your

© God will forgive all your sins

® The Lord Jesus Christ will come into your life ® You will become a Temple of the living God ® God will give you eternal life (moksha)

Read God's word, the Holy Bible regularly Trust Jesus Christ for everything in your life ® Allow the Holy spirit to guide you
