The wisest man of his time, once asked a profound ques- the pause that follows that question, what answer would you give? Later the wise man answered it himself: He said in triumph, “I know that my Redeemer liveth, and that He shall stand at the latter day upon the earth and though worms destroy this body of mine, yet in my flesh I shall see God. I shall see Him for myself, and with my own eyes I shall behold Him, and not through the eyes of he will surely live again. What then is life?

Life is a moment of time in which to prepare for eternity. Death is the departure of life from the body; the cessation of life. Once we are born, the slow process of dying begins, “Zero” hour is coming to all some day. Death is when the heart stops beating, when the breath ceases, when the body becomes stiff.

A boy once asked his mother, “At what age do people die?” She told him to go to the cemetry and measure the graves. Some were very small and some were large, so his obvious conclusion was that people die at all ages. The real question is: “Where am I going to live after death?”, a question of great importance to every living person. The inescapable fact is that one day our mortal bodies will cease to function, But the grave is not the end, even though our physical bodies will be laid in it. To man death is the separation of body, soul and spirit. The Bible plainly teaches that after death we will be found in one of the two places, heaven or hell, The body goes to the grave and disintegrates. 1) Spiritual death. Adam and Eve did not die physically. 2) Physical death, The first human to die was Abel.

Why did Jesus Christ die on the cross? Was it an acci- dent? Certainly not. God’s Word said that before God could forgive man’s sin someone sinless must die. Jesus volunteered to be the one to die. He died to bear the punishment for our sins, for the wages of sin is death (Rom. 6:23) and “the soul that sinneth it shall die.” (Ezek. 18:4). You and I ought to have died, spiritually as well as physically, but Jesus Christ died on the Cross as a sacrifice for you and me, (Eph. 5:2). He tasted the bitter dregs of death for you. If you believe the death of a Saviour was necessary for the redemption of man- kind, (Luke 24:46), and that Jesus Christ died as your substitute, you will be saved. I implore you today to turn from your sins and repent and turn to God.

At the parting with a loved one several questions come to our mind that demand answers. What becomes of the body at death? Will a lame ugly man look that way in the resurrection? Will the body burnt in accident be like that for ever? Will we recognize one another in heaven? There were many who rose from the dead at the time Jesus Christ died. Every man, woman and child shall rise from the dead. Christ was the first to rise. (Matt.

Therefore we must and shall rise from death. Some shall rise to eternal blessing, while others will rise to be cast into the lake of fire for ever. We do not return in the same body. The dead body is like a seed planted in

the ground, and the plant is different from the seed that was planted. The crippled or burnt body will not be like that in resurrection. There will be no aged, deformed, crippled, or ugly bodies in heaven. The resurrection body is a God-given body. It will be a radiant glorious body, it will be a body of power. It will not be subject to the laws of earth. It will be a spiritual body. The body of Jesus had flesh and bones, but it did not have blood. The blood was poured out on the cross, Some will be alive when Jesus returns and will be raptured. The living shall be changed, Heaven is the house of the Lord God. Hell is a place of torment and punishment, It is a place of fire. Heaven and hell are eternal and everlasting. Death is no death for those who believe in Christ, but the way by which they enter for ever into their eternal home. Dear friend, choose this day while you are alive upon this earth, to go to heaven which is a real happy beautiful place. The only way is the Lord Jesus Christ.
