Tamil Monthly Magazine

Jebikkum Pillai

Published by Life Redemption Mission

The Tamil monthly magazine Jebikkum Pillai is published by our LRM mission. It is written to be used not only by adults but also by teenagers and children. Through this magazine we also tell the history of the missionaries who helped liberate the most marginalized people in India, and shed light on their lives for the younger generation.
